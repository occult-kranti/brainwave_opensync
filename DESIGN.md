# Open Sync Archive — Inside the Field

## Brief and protected boundaries
The user rejected the Resonance Gallery: a conventional flat website containing attractive 3D boxes is not the requested outcome. Replace that visual world with a continuous immersive rendered environment, across every route. Baseline source 592f96b. Only archive/dark-landscapes and the archive/ published subtree may change; master and every other deployed file remain protected.

## The visual world
Experience first: a full-viewport, persistent Three.js installation of copper and silver filaments, pale-blue suspended particles, projected higher-dimensional forms, dark reflective ground, deep space and deliberate lighting. A scene remains visible in the first viewport of Home, Sounds, Studio, Player and Research. Routes change the composition within the world. Home's enormous sculpture occupies the right field; sparse typography and direct linked destinations occupy the left and foreground. No static hero image masquerading as a live renderer.

DOM content is the interaction layer inside that environment. Instrument controls and reading surfaces are smoked glass with sufficient opacity for text; navigation is a restrained HUD. Native scrolling, keyboard focus, mobile controls and deep links remain. Research must still be readable and sources accessible. Scene manipulation never changes the audio graph. Active sound status and Stop all sound stay visible. Loading a preset never autoplays.

## Sand universe
Cymatics opens into a large dimensional sand-art stage: grain sculptures, vortices, dunes, toroidal forms and genuine mathematical 4D rotation followed by bounded perspective projection. Presets visibly morph the geometry. Orbit, zoom, reset, pause, capture, and expand/escape are direct actions. Advanced motion, dispersion, grain count and appearance are disclosed contextually. Retain the existing plate model and its audio controls as a distinct exploration mode.

Higher-dimensional sand is generative art. It is not a claim of physical grains travelling between dimensions or a new FEM/fluid solver. Mathematical and artistic renderers are labelled at their point of use; no clinical effects are inferred from visuals or frequency controls.

## Engineering contracts
One persistent world canvas across routes; avoid rebuilding the renderer for ordinary route/settings changes. Suspend world animation while a foreground sand/immersive scene is active. Bound frame rate, DPR, total drawing-buffer pixels and particle count; stop hidden-tab work and dispose GPU resources/listeners. Reduced motion starts paused. A usable Canvas projection survives unavailable or lost WebGL. React 19, TypeScript, Vite and the installed Three.js remain suitable; a framework migration is not required to make the scene immersive.

## Work and review
User explicitly requested subagents. Independent graphics/world, dimensional-sand, and UI/experience agents own separate files. A research/review agent supplies sources, constraints and an independent implementation review; the root integrates and verifies. These are AI perspectives, not human participant studies. See docs/design/immersive-world-research.md for open-source references and acceptance ledger. Impeccable launcher/reference files remain unavailable as documented in the previous redesign; use its available core instructions and the product contract.

## Release gate
Lint, TypeScript and complete repository tests; fully rendered production routes at desktop and phone sizes; keyboard/escape, scene controls, sand morphs, image export, fallback/reduced motion, sound load/stop, and no horizontal overflow. Bounded visual review: one batched inspection, one correction and confirmation pass. Publish only archive/ while preserving its redirect folders, and prove all non-archive Git blobs plus master unchanged. Confirm live Pages build and deployed routes.
