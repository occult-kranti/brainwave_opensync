# Open Sync

An independent browser listening space for meditation, rest, focus and sound exploration. Adults can find a short session by intention, time and playback equipment, then adjust it in Studio. Existing research, cultural collections, measurement tools, exports, safety settings and the preserved applications remain available.

Success means finding and loading an appropriate session without understanding DSP. Listening is voluntary; loading never starts sound. Clinical benefits and brain states are not inferred from a chosen frequency. The audio engine, safety governor, panic, privacy and GitHub Pages base remain contracts.

Current design instruction (2026-10-29): the user rejected the previous designs and persistent 3D/4D background and delegated complete design choice. Prioritize readable everyday listening and task-based access to all tools. Dedicated visual laboratories remain available as tools; ambient page renderers and stored backdrop preferences no longer define the experience.
