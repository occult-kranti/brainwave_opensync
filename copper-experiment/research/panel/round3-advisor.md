## Working thesis that survives

A position-associated spectrum is an observation of a specified specimen–fixture–source–receiver–environment system. Forward validity, calibrated channel definitions and identifying information against explicit nuisance alternatives are all necessary. Mirror symmetry, a support-parameter product and locked nuisance designs provide exact counterexamples to unique coordinate inference in the retained models. More FFT bins or repetition cannot remove those exact kernels.

The three-mode spring projection improves the declared contact toy by retaining modal masses and cross terms. It does not identify real preload/contact, include all modes or turn a location law into measured evidence. Ratios remove a stated common scale, not every alternative cause. A valid new spatial term would still need a dimensioned mechanism, frozen quantitative prediction and independently calibrated discriminating measurements.
