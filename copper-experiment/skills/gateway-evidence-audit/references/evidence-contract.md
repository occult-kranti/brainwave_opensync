## Claim/channel contract

Each claim needs a source passage, historical assertion, current interpretation, exact observable, units, alternative, status and discriminator. Separate carrierHz, interauralDifferenceHz, pulseRateHz, EEG voltage/spectrum, Focus text label, electrical measurement, mechanical mode and location coordinates. Numeric equality is not coupling. Official custody is documentary provenance, not empirical confirmation.

Use only typed relationships: `documents`, `attributes-to`, `methodological-analogy`, `proposes-test`, and narrowly justified `supports/contradicts`. A torus image or shared control cannot carry a causal edge.

## Stimulus reproducibility contract

A recoverable stimulus requires generator revision, waveform, routing, sample rate, phase/gain/envelope, duration, pulse details, noise seed/filter, encoder and output hash. Check spectra perL/R channel, and mixed/envelope channels separately. Five correct carrier subtractions do not reproduce missing MP3 bytes, proprietary processing, EEG effects or a mechanical force spectrum.

Reject false certainty: chosen7.83Hz arithmetic is not exact measured geophysics;40Hz pulse rate is not an interaural40Hz difference; Focus10 is not10Hz. Photon quantum arithmetic and an unspecified universalQ cannot replace classical input/transfer/loss measurement.

## Empty measured-coupling gate

Until evidence is supplied, retain null fields for source/target channel, law, units, parameters, calibration, transfer function, energy budget, raw data and held-out predictions. Null means unspecified, not measured zero. Never fill a body–sphere term to make an exhibit look complete.

Require independently measured input, source work/load/loss accounting, nuisance-identifiability and off/detuned/geometry/amplitude controls before evaluating a candidate. Force-reversal odd response includes odd-order nonlinearities and pickup; require amplitude scaling plus independent optical/dummy/drive-off controls. Parity is not a linearity certificate.
