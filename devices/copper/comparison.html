Read the common equation and radius conventions

Uniform membrane breathing: f = √[2E / (ρ(1 − ν))] / (2πRₘ). The mass convention is m = 4πRₘ²hρ; the exact geometric shell mass is recorded separately. Rₘ is the midsurface radius, h is wall thickness, E is Young's modulus, ρ is density and ν is Poisson's ratio.

Claude: outer radius 44.45 mm, inner radius 43.45 mm, midsurface 43.95 mm, wall 1 mm; E = 127.3478 GPa, ν = 0.3460123, ρ = 8,930 kg/m³. Existing default: midsurface 44.45 mm, wall 0.5 mm; E = 123.5 GPa, ν = 0.35, ρ = 8,960 kg/m³.

The original default breathing results, 23,916.18 Hz and 23,317.72 Hz, use different geometry, materials and reductions. Matching the inputs removes the implementation discrepancy in the shared membrane equation. The remaining finite-thickness difference is real within these declared mathematical models.

CONTACT / TRUNCATION

A last step is not an error bound.

Increasing angular cutoff from 34 to 44 changes S2 by −0.967 Hz and bounce by −6.889 Hz. The last differences are about 96.6 ppm and 0.985%. They do not certify the limit of a point-contact model.

PLATE / GEOMETRY

The short span matters.

The 0.25 m thick plate has thickness/short span = 0.2083. No Mindlin or 3D comparison was supplied to establish the original “few percent” correction. Keep its frequencies as thin-plate illustrations.

PRECISION / NOISE MODEL

Declare the SNR convention.

At 40 dB initial signal-power SNR, the explicit real-signal Fisher calculation gives σf = 0.001343 Hz for constant amplitude and 0.002244 Hz with unknown decay rate, versus 0.000949 Hz in the source formula. These ideal noise bounds omit systematic effects and instrument calibration.

MECHANISM / COUPLING

The assembled apparatus is still missing.

The contact eigenproblem uses rigid ground. The separate plate curves describe an empty plate. Air loading is first order near the declared modes. Combining these panels visually does not solve one coupled apparatus.
