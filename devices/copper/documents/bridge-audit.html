Invalid arguments on both sides

Planck step: Gateway §19 compares a speed written in cm/s with a distance written in cm. A length cannot be a speed threshold without an independently specified timescale and law. The ordinary harmonic solution q=A cos(ωt) has zero velocity at turning points while displacement is maximal. This contradicts that particular route to departure from spacetime; it does not constitute an experiment on every consciousness claim.

Photon/Q rebuttal: the derivative frequency dossier’s acoustic rebuttal uses a single-quantum energy and an unqualified tissue Q≈1. A classical sound field’s total delivered energy is not fixed by hf; amplitude, duration, mode occupation, coupling and dissipation matter. Q is a property of a specified mode and operating regime, not one universal number across unspecified tissues and frequencies. Reject the unsupported therapeutic/selectivity claim on its missing mechanism and controlled evidence, not with a false general mechanical impossibility theorem.

Same number, same cause: phase, a binaural difference, an electromagnetic oscillation and a mechanical resonance can all be described in hertz. A shared unit is necessary for numerical frequency comparison; it does not make their actuators, states or observation operators equivalent.

Challenge to the provisional mathematical thesis

In the stipulated isotropic radial family,

f_j = C_j(ν) sqrt(E/ρ)/R.

At fixed ν, changing (E,ρ) to (aE,aρ) leaves every radial frequency unchanged; changing E to a²E and R to aR also leaves them unchanged when ρ is fixed. Ratios f_j/f_1 remove the entire shared scale. More radial modes can challenge the chosen family or constrain ν in that family; they cannot independently recover E, ρ and R from these frequencies. This is a scale fiber, not a numerical-resolution problem.

The Position contact toy makes a different sharp prediction:

f_Bj² − f_Aj² = δ, with δ = position × stiffness /(4π²m_eff).

It assigns the same illustrative whole-sphere effective mass to each mode. For nonzero δ and distinct initial frequencies, the fractional ratios f_Bj/f_Aj = sqrt(1+δ/f_Aj²) differ between modes. A common multiplicative scale instead predicts equal fractional ratios. Holding out one mode can discriminate those two fixed families. But real contact uses mode-dependent participation/mass and may mix modes; rejecting the equal-δ toy does not reject every support effect or establish an exotic term. Keep that toy’s assumptions visible.

The inverse seat’s reported ranks—observability/reachability 4/4, then 6/4 after adding a sensor, then 6/6 after adding a drive—are a useful six-state example. The added sensor cannot excite an unforced mode. Full structural rank assumes the known finite generator and ideal records; finite-time noise, near-degeneracy, calibration, unknown parameters and omitted physical modes remain separate questions. It is not a certificate of total observability or global parameter uniqueness.

The temperature-locked design is especially relevant: T=0.2x leaves only β+0.2c_T observable, with reported design rank 3 rather than 4. Repeats do not separate β and c_T. The full-rank redesigned fixture proves separability only for its included columns, not that the actual apparatus contains no omitted confounder. ABBA timing does not repair permanent correlation with position.
