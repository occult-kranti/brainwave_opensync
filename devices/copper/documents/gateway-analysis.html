Five sine-pair difference calculations were executed from the JSON and agree within1e-10 Hz:4,10,6,2 and7.83 Hz. The other entries describe a40-pulses/s stimulus and noise. This is arithmetic verification only.

The claim that every advertised MP3 can be regenerated exactly is unsupported by the pin. A newly written generator would create a new study stimulus, not recover the original missing bytes or proprietary Hemi-Sync processing.

The “exact geophysics” wording attached to 7.83 Hz confuses a chosen oscillator difference with a measured environmental spectrum. A numeric seed, measured electromagnetic peak, cavity eigenfrequency, audio envelope, EEG response and material eigenmode need separate records. The historical/mechanism seat's geophysical reading further distinguishes source/observer-dependent peaks from cavity eigenfrequencies; that is a measurement analogy, not biological coupling evidence.

The dossier's photon-energy/universal-Q rebuttal is also unsuitable as a general acoustic null test. A small quantum energy does not determine the total classical acoustic energy delivered. A mechanically relevant proposal needs amplitude, force, coupling, damping, duration and loss accounting. An unspecified tissue-Q number cannot replace a mode-specific measurement. Reject unsupported advocacy and unsupported rebuttal symmetrically.

6. Copper Wall is a separate electrical channel

The 2018 Copper Wall proposal describes a copper-panel electrical measurement arrangement and motion monitoring. The 2023 institutional case account concerns DC/very-low-frequency electrical measurements and identifies motion, clothing, furniture, rug and grounding artifacts. It supplies cases and prospective work, not an independently replicated sphere-mechanics experiment.

Electrometer volts are instrument output. They require electrode geometry, reference and a calibrated transfer model before becoming field strength or charge. They are not EEG, mechanical displacement or radiated power. Shared use of copper does not identify the apparatus with a thin elastic shell. This source supplies artifact-control ideas, not a body–sphere force term or confirmation of intention-mediated transfer.

Objection to the history seat's strongest bridge: a shared source→medium→receiver pattern is a useful analogy but very general. It cannot provide positive evidence for a common Gateway/Earth/copper mechanism. The hypothetical deformation K=K0+εh(x)K1 is a declared model alternative, not a recovered field or permitted Gateway coupling. Objection to the measurement seat's strongest bridge: phase reversal and reproducible stimuli are valid general methods but do not establish Gateway efficacy or original processing. The odd half-difference of reversed-drive records contains odd-order nonlinear response, including cubic terms, and force-proportional pickup can also reverse.

Response and decision: the measurement seat accepted this criticism in Round 2 and retained independently measured force, drive-amplitude scaling, independent optical readout, dummy and drive-off controls. Admit reversal as a parity control; do not use it alone as a linearity, mechanism or calibration certificate. Amplitude scaling and artifact discrimination must precede the stronger interpretation.

8. Empty measured-coupling gate and proposed tests

The machine-readable coupling law, parameters, calibration, transfer function, energy budget and data fields are null. This is intentional missing evidence, not a zero-valued measurement or a coded negative conclusion. No source-derived forcing term is invented.

Before a physical coupling can be evaluated, a proponent must supply:

  1. A source channel, target channel and dimensioned constitutive/interaction term.
  2. Input/readout bandwidth, phase and amplitude calibration with environmental/background records.
  3. Delivered source work, stored-energy change, load response and losses under the same model.
  4. Predeclared observable and smallest relevant effect; off/detuned, geometry, amplitude and artifact controls.
  5. Identifiability against support, temperature, sensor and clock nuisance terms, plus held-out predictions.

Presently admitted next-step tests are bounded: recover/align a legitimate original workbook passage; verify any newly authored waveform against its complete stimulus contract; distinguish L/R spectra from an explicitly mixed/envelope channel; compare matched fitted mechanical modes with shared support/readout; characterize the empty table separately; retain nonlinear/pickup controls for reversal. An electrical experiment would require its own independently reviewed apparatus and protocol; none is created here.

Failure of a particular control blocks its associated inference. A null result bounds only the tested channel, band, sensitivity and conditions. A reproducible residual requires alternative-mechanism testing and replication; it does not automatically become a location code, consciousness effect or new force.
