Working thesis: apparatus response and identifiable interpretation

Round 2 project synthesis, 22 October 2026. This is a research contract, not a new theorem or a report of measured copper data.

A position-associated recorded spectrum is an observation of a specified specimen–fixture–source–receiver–environment system. Interpreting a difference requires a credible forward model and data that distinguish the target parameter from competing causes. A frequency does not, by itself, identify an object’s complete state, an absolute spatial address or an agent of motion.

Status and model

The current evidence consists of attributed source material, selected established physics, executed finite synthetic models and proposed physical protocols. No calibrated sphere A/B measurements, body-to-sphere forcing law or destination-selective motion are established.

For a restricted linear model,

y_g(ω,x) = C_g(x)[K(x) − ω²M(x) + iωD(x)]⁻¹B_g(x)F(ω) + noise.

M, D and K describe retained dynamics; B and C describe input and observation. Their dependence on x must come from a named mechanism or be labelled a deliberate model deformation. F must be measured or explicitly stipulated; a preset title or EEG-band name is not a mechanical force. A receptance uses displacement/force in m/N. A mobility uses velocity/force in (m/s)/N and differs by the complex factor iω under the stated Fourier convention. Normalized model curves are a separate quantity.

A response maximum, an FFT bin, a fitted pole and an eigenfrequency are different objects. The integrated workflow may report only what its implemented estimator supports. A future common-pole estimator needs validation; its absence cannot be concealed by a new label.

Exact consequences with bounded meaning

  1. Rigid translation of an isolated specimen and homogeneous modeled environment preserves a translated elastic operator’s spectrum. This is an exact fixed-model invariance. Changing supports, gradients, orientation or the reference clock changes premises. The result is not a physical theorem that all real positions have identical observed responses.
  2. The current symmetric sphere/fixture observation map identifies p with 1−p. A unique coordinate cannot be reconstructed from that map even with noiseless dense spectra. This says which extra information that model lacks.
  3. In the fixed isotropic radial family, mode ratios cancel the common sqrt(E/ρ)/R scale. All those frequencies cannot separately identify E, ρ and R. Independent dimensional, mass, material and boundary information is necessary.
  4. Changing input/readout overlap can change residues and hide a mode while leaving dynamics unchanged. A missing peak is not a missing state. Adding sensors and adding independent excitation are distinct operations.
  5. The simple Position contact toy identifies a product of its position coefficient and stiffness. A fitted coefficient derived from a stipulated position law is not discovered evidence for that law.

These statements are exact consequences or reported executions of named finite models. None establishes completeness of the physical apparatus model or universal absence of an additional effect.

Discriminating research program

The next useful outcome is a prospectively constrained comparison of explicit alternatives, with a rejection rule for each.

Candidate Predictive content Holdout/control
Observation-path change Common retained complex poles, changed source/readout residues Unused sensor geometry and independent input; matched state; complex phase as well as magnitude
Common material/size scale Common fractional scaling across the chosen radial family at fixed ν Unused identified mode, independent temperature/geometry data
Equal-δ contact toy Same squared-frequency increment across its modes under equal stipulated effective mass Unused mode and deliberate support change; retain that the toy is not a calibrated contact law
Finite nuisance model Stated temperature/contact/time dependencies Independently varied nuisance conditions, later day and full experimental blocks
Additional spatial term Frozen function/parameter, units, frame and quantitative effect distinct from nuisance predictions Fixture/laboratory coordinate crossover and independent replication

Select the primary mode or modal subspace, band, estimator, smallest meaningful effect, equivalence/contrast decision rule, missing-fit policy and uncertainty/power assumptions before inspecting confirmatory data. Freeze fitting and holdout sets at the level of complete trials, configurations or days. Adjacent bins and overlapping segments are not independent experiments. Include return-to-A, A-to-A movement, same-position remount, empty holder, reference resonator, drive-amplitude and intentional thermal/contact perturbations as appropriate to the target.

Rank checks should test target estimability against the declared nuisance family and identify global aliases as well as local deficiencies. Temporal observability/reachability of a finite known generator and practical noisy conditioning should be reported separately. Passing a rank test is not proof that the real nuisance family is complete. A model that fails controlled pilot conditions should be repaired and refrozen before a confirmatory contrast is interpreted.

Use one held-out mode or geometry to challenge competing models at equal or explicitly documented nuisance flexibility. Selecting whichever alternative looks best after the data and reporting only that comparison defeats the test. Ratios also inherit correlated fit and calibration uncertainties; their cancellation of some scale errors does not make them uncertainty-free.

Permitted conclusions

  • Synthetic check: the implemented finite equations pass a stated comparison or counterexample.
  • No shift resolved: the selected channel/interval does not resolve the prespecified contrast with the achieved sensitivity.
  • Bounded equivalence: a separately justified equivalence procedure places the whole relevant interval inside a declared margin.
  • Residual requiring follow-up: an association survives the listed controls, with uncertainty and remaining alternatives documented.

A nonzero residual does not identify its cause by elimination of only one toy. A null result does not establish all-channel physical absence. Additional modes, datasets and software can increase information within scope; they do not certify novelty or an extraordinary mechanism.

Historical and cross-domain boundary

Newton supplies a documented distinction between observed law and proposed cause. Tesla supplies concrete phase, circuit, loading and energy questions. Gateway supplies attributed historical claims and a useful opportunity to examine source and channel categories. These are modern methodological adaptations, not a continuous validated physics lineage.

Bentov/Purce books remain cited-original leads unless specifically inspected. The official GovInfo report’s Planck speed/length step is dimensionally invalid. The derivative dossier’s single-quantum/universal-Q rebuttal is not a general acoustic null theorem. A shared frequency or torus picture supplies no missing coupling operator. Keep these rejected bridges visible rather than silently repairing historical wording into a modern result.

The thesis is supported as an organizing research framework. Any claim to a new spatial-frequency law, full object reconstruction, consciousness transfer or directed motion needs its own dimensioned mechanism, calibrated observable, distinguishing prediction and reproducible measurements.
