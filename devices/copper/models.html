    THREE MODES / A SEPARATE SOLID REFERENCE

    Compare ratios and contact.

    This solid-sphere reference uses the applied radius, even when the main comparison uses a shell. A grounded point spring couples the three radial coordinates through a rank-one stiffness matrix.

    Only three radial modes are retained. Rigid and nonradial modes, finite-area or nonlinear contact, seam effects, and real fixture calibration are omitted. The grounded spring is a different model from the dynamic fixture above. Ratio changes can also result from material, anisotropy or mode-identification changes; they do not uniquely identify a cause.
