## Adjacent evidence: useful controls, limited interpretation

The original institutional Copper Wall2018 proposal describes an insulated platform, a copper panel connected to electrical instrumentation, and motion/video monitoring. The 2023 account concerns quasi-static electrical measurements and notes artifacts from motion, clothing, furniture and rugs; it reports replacing carpet and upgrading grounding. It presents cases and proposes further multichannel studies. The accessed text supplies neither raw data nor a blinded randomized replication nor a field-to-sphere force law. Electrometer volts are an instrument output; they cannot be relabelled V/m, mechanical displacement, EEG or radiated power without calibration. The lesson worth importing is artifact characterization. The word copper does not make the experiment the same as an elastic copper sphere.

For the dossier's EEG summary, Ingendoh, Posny and Heine2023 was checked at abstract, selected results and discussion depth. Its summary is 14 studies: five positive, eight negative, one mixed, with substantial heterogeneity. The results section counts six at least partially positive because the mixed study overlaps. All four pink-noise-embedded studies in this sample were negative; the dossier's capitalized universal statement exceeds that sample. The review did not settle entrainment and is not an audit of original Gateway recordings. Its own underlying studies and supporting tables were not all read here. No clinical outcome is adopted by this panel report.

Other dossier entries, trial status updates and government-program numbers remain unverified leads. The dossier's letters A–D combine replicated physics, intervention evidence and interpretation; retain separate claim-level grades instead of assigning one strength to an entire preset. In particular:

- A computed 7.83 Hz difference is exact only relative to chosen oscillator inputs. The corpus contains no contemporaneous field measurement establishing that the surrounding cavity is exactly that value.
- An EEG-band name on a preset does not measure EEG, demonstrate Focus attainment, reproduce a proprietary recording or establish material resonance.
- A 40-pulses/s waveform and a 40 Hz interaural difference need separate stimulus contracts. Their equal rate does not establish equivalence.
- The dossier's acoustic argument alternates between photon-energy arithmetic and pressure waves. A mechanical-drive hypothesis requires force, transfer, damping and power measurements. The photon-threshold calculation is not a general mechanical null test, and an unqualified universal tissue-Q number should not be imported.

## Proposed decision tests for the next round

These are proposed gates, not completed experiments. Let the critic decide which test is necessary before any import.

| Test | Observable/control | Acceptance or falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| G-T1 source fidelity | Recover legitimate original workbook scan; page-to-line align a selected Focus/EBT/Game Plan passage | Publish only aligned passage claims; inaccessible pages retain transcript-only status |
| G-T2 preset contract | If audio is imported, inspect a deterministic generator and lossless L/R output; verify carriers, difference, pulse shape, gain and stop behavior | Metadata arithmetic alone cannot pass waveform identity; original-Monroe equivalence requires originals and documented rights/access |
| G-T3 arithmetic/channel check | Generate independent L/R200/204 Hz records and inspect each channel spectrum; inspect explicitly mixed/envelope channels separately | Show exactly where4 Hz is a calculated difference, envelope, measured component or neural hypothesis; never create a low-frequency mechanical input by relabelling |
| G-T4 mechanical A/B control | Keep the same sphere, orientation, support and co-moving readout; randomize timed ABBA blocks; add same-position handling shams | Compare matched fitted modes in m/N with uncertainty; a changed tallest FFT bin cannot pass as intrinsic mode change |
| G-T5 table boundary separation | Measure empty table and holder independently; deliberately vary support in separate blocks | Position-dependent coupling/visibility is an alternative, not evidence for an innate location code; subtraction alone does not uncouple systems |
| G-T6 electrical-artifact control | Only if a distinct electrical channel is actually added: dummy electrode/input, grounding/charge-motion tests, cable-motion and time-synchronized video controls | Do not infer an electrical field from mechanical data, or intention from case timing; no human protocol is created here |
| G-T7 candidate coupling gate | Require a supplied physical coupling term with units, delivered input, transfer function, energy budget, predicted effect and off/detuned controls | With no such term in the present corpus, leave body–sphere coupling absent rather than invent it |

A null result would constrain the tested apparatus, channel, interval and sensitivity. A reproducible residual would invite replication and competing mechanisms. Neither outcome alone resolves historical consciousness claims.
