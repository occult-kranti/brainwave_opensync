# Historical and patent expansion: measuring a sphere, not naming a universal frequency

Research date: 22 October 2026. AI-assisted source review, not human peer review. This is a selected corpus of 22 records, including 10 patent/application records. It is not all patents, all historical works or an exhaustive freedom-to-operate search. The source register records passages actually read and access limitations. Original PDFs/OCR are research inputs, not an instruction to republish entire third-party works.

## What the sources change

The most useful connection is between historical normal-mode theory, resonant ultrasound spectroscopy (RUS), and spatially resolved metrology. Their measurable target is a frequency response or a set of mode parameters under specified conditions. A spectrum can help compare specimens or infer selected material properties. It does not automatically identify every microscopic degree of freedom or an absolute location.

| Lineage | Audited connection | Consequence for the lab |
|---|---|---|
| Newton → propagation | PrincipiaII discusses waves in a specified elastic medium. This is a conceptual link, not a claim that modern RUS derives directly from Newton. | Separate source motion, airborne propagation and sensor output. |
| Chladni → Faraday | Plate patterns can encode nodes, but particle transport also depends on air/fluid flow. Faraday varied those conditions. | Do not infer a mode shape from particles alone. Check with an independent displacement channel. |
| Rayleigh → elastic modes | Normal-mode coordinates separate a small, conservative system; its physical parameters and boundaries still matter. | A coordinate change preserves the model spectrum. A changed support is a different system. |
| Lamb → spheres and shells | A solid sphere has multiple modal families; a shell is a different problem. | The current breathing approximation is a selected mode, not the entire spectrum. |
| Tesla oscillators → source/load coupling | The mechanical source consumes pressure energy; generator loading and temperature enter the mechanism. | Include input energy and source stability. An observed drive tone is not proof of an intrinsic specimen pole. |
| RUS patents → inspection | The inspected patents distinguish instrument resonance, signature features, shape errors and local scanning. | Add fixture blanks, repeated source/readout geometry, multiplet-aware fitting and uncertainty. |
| CW laser RUS application → observability | The 2026 application proposes spatially selected excitation of modes. | Mode visibility depends on where/how the specimen is driven. Contactless does not mean unperturbed. |

These are curated research connections. Only explicitly labeled citation edges assert that one source cites another.

## Patent audit and families

The registry provides dates, claims read and apparatus scope for every included record. Tesla514169,517900 and511916 form a related mechanism series but are distinct applications. Radiant-energy685957 and685958 are recorded together as the mirror's apparatus/method family. US5062296 and its priority-linked WO/CA/DE/JP publications are one research cluster rather than five independent inventions. US10444202B2 and US20150300995A1 are grant/publication versions of one application. US20260036553A1 is a published application; it is not labeled as a grant or replicated result.

A patent’s claim of a “unique signature” has a declared inspection context. In particular, the 1991 RUS specification aims for a signature relatively insensitive to transducer placement and notes that its anomaly detector does not identify the anomaly’s location. That language cannot support an unrestricted frequency-to-destination law. The 2019 spatial-inspection patent instead adds distributed measurements, which illustrates how more observables change what can be inferred.

## Limitations found in the present work

1. **Modal completeness:** The breathing calculation does not represent torsional, nonradial spheroidal, bending, seam or support modes. Keep the implemented family explicit; do not call all modes harmonics. “Harmonic motion” means sinusoidal motion, while a harmonic frequency series means integer multiples.
2. **Invisible modes:** One source/readout pair may have near-zero overlap with a real mode. Missing peaks do not prove missing modes. Sweep several physically documented source and sensor positions before fitting.
3. **False modes:** The measurement chain can contribute resonances and noise. A dummy specimen/empty-fixture run and independent readout are needed. The1990 instrument patent addresses this directly.
4. **Split modes and orientation:** Geometric imperfections and anisotropy can separate or reweight near-degenerate modes. Tracking the strongest bin at A and B can accidentally track different modes.
5. **Actuation is a perturbation:** Piezoelectric contact changes loading; optical excitation deposits heat. A noncontact method reduces one disturbance while introducing another. Record source amplitude, duty and temperature.
6. **Signatures are model- and band-dependent:** Spectral-density features can discriminate chosen objects without solving the inverse material problem uniquely. Positive classification accuracy is not complete state reconstruction.
7. **Historical translation:** Source claims, theory and later anecdotes require separate status. No examined source supplies a validated absolute-location frequency or an authenticated translocation result.

## Proposed discriminating experiments

These are proposed protocols, not physical measurements or newly discovered laws. They should complement the implemented measurement simulations rather than masquerade as their validation.

| Test | Hypothesis and observable | Essential control | Result that challenges the hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visibility grid | At fixed A, vary source and readout direction. A common set of poles should fit multiple responses while modal amplitudes vary. | Fixed support, orientation and temperature; repeat initial geometry at end. | Claimed common poles fail a joint fit beyond calibrated uncertainty. |
| Rotate without translating | Rotate the sphere on the same portable support and compare a selected multiplet. | Separately repeat handling without rotation. | A purported location effect follows orientation instead. |
| Instrument blank | A suspected peak is created by the specimen rather than electronics or fixture. | Empty fixture, passive dummy and independent readout; identical acquisition chain. | The peak persists without the specimen or follows the instrument. |
| Source-power series | Within a stated linear range, fitted frequencies and damping are stable as drive amplitude changes. | Randomized amplitude order; temperature logging and recovery intervals. | Repeatable amplitude-linked shifts reveal nonlinearity or heating. |
| Complex-response check | The fitted pole is supported by phase evolution as well as a magnitude maximum. | Calibrated input reference and unchanged channel units. | Only the amplitude maximum shifts; joint phase/amplitude fit supports unchanged poles. |
| Particle-pattern audit | Visible grain patterns mark mechanical nodes under the selected conditions. | Independent vibrometry and controlled airflow; no inference from images alone. | Particle accumulations move while independently measured nodes remain fixed. |

The instrument-level optical proposal requires a properly equipped laboratory. The new photorealistic images should depict equipment and connection intent, while deterministic diagrams carry dimensions and channel assignments. Generated pictures are not evidence that apparatus was built.

## Newton, Tesla, philosophy and the unresolved name

Newton’s chymical notes are genuine historical material. Their useful methodological connection is record keeping, quantified outcomes and failed trials; they provide no new mechanical term. The 1896 Vivekananda letter documents philosophical discussion and anticipation of a demonstration, not the completed demonstration. Vedanta and Newtonian chymistry are different historical categories.

The niche Rex Research compilation is a lead map with uneven provenance. It includes attributed 1935 newspaper statements, later books and patent copies with the claims omitted. The original patent records were therefore read separately. Newspaper facsimiles and the listed books were not fully verified, so the extraordinary transmission anecdotes remain attributed claims.

“Freidman” is unresolved. Alexander Friedmann’s university archive concerns curved-space geometry and cosmology. It was checked as one candidate, not identified as the user’s intended author and not used as evidence for this experiment. Feynman’s official resonance chapter is separately relevant; its inclusion does not silently correct the user’s name. A full name or title would permit a precise follow-up search.
