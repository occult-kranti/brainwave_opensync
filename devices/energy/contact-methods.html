Round 2 · independent numerical check

What voltage would the stipulated field induce?

For a stationary ideal air-core coil in a spatially uniform sinusoidal field: Vrms = N A 2π f Brms |cos θ|. Resistor-only thermal voltage: √(4 kB T R Δf). The area, turns and resistance are stipulated independently; they do not constitute a feasible coil design.

This omits coil reactance, loading, real effective area, amplifier noise, local interference and calibration uncertainty. Nonzero sensitivity does not establish actual detectability or source identity. The body-sized circular-path calculation in the report is not tissue dosimetry or a universal biological limit.

Round 3 / preserved limitation

A correct test can be unable to detect anything.

Seven searched lags across 32 cyclic offsets force corrected p ≥ 7/32. At α = 0.05 this design cannot reject for any input. The invalid selected-lag test nevertheless rejected 7/32 offsets in one fixture.

What this does not establish: Zero adjusted rejections are not evidence that an effect is absent. This is a synthetic finite-group demonstration, not an empirical anomaly detector.

Round 4 / two distinct limits

Better statistical resolution does not identify the cause.

With 256 offsets, the aligned example reaches p = 7/256 = 0.02734375. Yet X = z, Y = 2z fits both Y = 2X and a shared-driver model. Their observational design has kernel (1, −1). A hypothetical independent perturbation produces rank 2 and Gram determinant 28.

What this does not establish: The cyclic null and hypothetical intervention are assumptions. These calculations do not validate them for actual field recordings or channeling reports.

Round 5 / fixed-trial arithmetic

A score cannot name its information source.

For 40/100 four-choice guesses, the specified no-information tail is 0.000686592. With m = 10 assumed compromised trials, the preselected-subset model gives 0.0472839 and the arbitrary-outcome-corruption bound gives 0.149541. A 20% perfect-leak mixture has q = 0.4 and the same count distribution as another independent source with q = 0.4.

What this does not establish: There were no human trials. The actual leakage count is unknown. Exact binomial scoring assumes a guess on every trial; pre-reveal abstentions retained as nonhits require the conservative-reference distinction in the protocol. No ET probability follows.

Round 6 / metadata is not authentication

Keep an attractive packet from certifying itself.

Malformed units, dates, Nyquist settings, reveal chronology, trial counts and unpaired intervals are challenged independently. Complete fabricated observational metadata can be structurally ready while evidenceAuthenticated remains false. The four-target unsalted-hash demonstration recovers the selected target by enumeration.

What this does not establish: This is not a cryptographic break or proof that a salted scheme is secure. The code does not fetch or authenticate referenced data, calibrations, randomness, custody or subjective reports.

Prospective design · not constructed

Two independent streams, one locked analysis.

Passive environmental acquisition and an independently blinded information task. This diagram specifies research dependencies, not a contact device or a demonstrated coupling.

Before acquiring human or field data

Admit a packet only for the question it can answer.

  1. Define the question and unit: field anomaly, physiological response, information accuracy, or sender identity. Do not substitute one endpoint for another.
  2. Freeze the actual original event time in UTC, clock uncertainty, location, inclusion rules, every eligible session and all misses. Upload and repost dates are not substitutes.
  3. Specify Bx/By or Ez units, sample rate, anti-aliasing, calibrated complex response, noise, station QC, checksum-bound data and matched baseline. A colored screenshot is not a calibrated numeric series.
  4. For hidden information, separate target custodian, participant, scorer and analyst. Lock responses before reveal, retain all trials and audit independent target generation and concealment.
  5. Do not expose a small target through an unsalted hash. Independently audit unpredictable secret nonces, commitment custody and disclosure chronology; a nonceBits declaration does not prove entropy or secrecy.
  6. Predeclare the estimator, lag family, null transformations, nuisance/calendar variables, stopping rule, uncertainty and held-out replication. Check resolution and power before acquisition.
  7. Keep information accuracy and environmental association as separate hypotheses. Audit shared drivers, leakage, missingness and ordinary receiver artifacts before interpreting either.
  8. Treat a schema pass as metadata readiness only. Verify referenced raw bytes and independent audit records; never auto-promote a ready packet into coupling, contact or ET evidence.
Readiness is not evidence of an effect.

A valid metadata packet does not authenticate its claims. Calibration records, chronology, independent custody and raw-byte checks need separate inspection. The current public quick looks and corpus texts do not satisfy the paired empirical contract.
