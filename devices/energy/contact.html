02 / Dimensioned electromagnetic acquisition

Question. What can the stated magnetic signal induce in a specified ideal coil?

Result. The illustrative 1 pT RMS, 7.83 Hz field gives 49.1973 nV RMS for 1,000 turns of 1 m². Producer 65 assertions and 151 independent comparisons passed, including zero-sensitivity cases.

Critique and next decision. A stipulated coil and resistor-only noise model are not a feasible detector, tissue dose, biological response or sender identifier. Next, test timing and lag-selection artifacts.

03 / Lag selection exposes a powerless toy

Question. Does correcting for searched lags control a known finite randomization null?

Result. One 32-offset fixture gave 7/32 rejections for the invalid selected-lag test. The adjusted test gave none. Independent review proved its minimum possible adjusted p was 7/32, so it could never reject at 0.05. The distinct-shape null fixture and all-lag p = 1 control were retained.

Critique and next decision. Correct calibration alone is not useful sensitivity. Preserve the failure and predeclare a larger group; real calendar dependence still needs its own justified null.

04 / Resolve test granularity; expose a common cause

Question. Can a larger finite test detect alignment, and does alignment identify a mechanism?

Result. The separately versioned 256-offset design permits an aligned p = 7/256. Both producer fixtures retain 7/256 adjusted null rejections, with naive counts 77/256 and 75/256. In the shared-driver example, observational rank is 1 with nullity 1; only a stipulated independent perturbation gives rank 2.

Critique and next decision. The sensitivity example is not a population-power estimate. More sensors or calendar labels do not establish the independent-intervention premise. Next, separate information accuracy from agency.

05 / Hidden information, leakage and numerical repair

Question. What would a fixed concealed-target score establish, and how does leakage change it?

Result. For 100 actual four-choice guesses under the declared null, the 5% rejection threshold is 33 hits. An ordinary independent leak with probability 0.2 gives the same q = 0.4 hit-count law as any stipulated independent mechanism with q = 0.4. A positive-tail underflow defect was reproduced, archived and repaired before acceptance.

Critique and next decision. At 40 hits and 10 compromised trials, the preselected-subset tail is 0.0472839, while the arbitrary-after-outcome corruption bound is 0.149541. Actual leakage is unknown. Next, require custody, chronology and data provenance without pretending metadata authenticates them.

06 / Adversarial evidence intake and a prospective protocol

Question. Can a complete-looking packet incorrectly become authenticated contact evidence?

Result. The staged validator separates documentary, environmental, information and paired metadata readiness. A fully fabricated packet can satisfy structural requirements but always remains unauthenticated. Actual quick-look/Kp/corpus availability descriptors do not become paired evidence. One published target digest was matched by hashing four candidate strings.

Critique and next decision. Hashes identify checked bytes, not truthful timestamps or secrecy. Declared nonce length does not prove entropy. A ready packet still requires independent calibration and custody review. Stop after these six rounds; acquire discriminating evidence before another discovery cycle.
