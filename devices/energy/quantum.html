Stored is not always
extractable.

Compare mean energy with ergotropy: ideal work available from a specified prepared quantum state under cyclic unitary control.

ONE QUBIT / FIXED HAMILTONIAN

d = 1 means no added dephasing. γ and d are channel parameters, not measured decay rates.

IDEAL STATE MODEL

The state was prepared using a resource. Extractable work is bounded by its stored energy; preparation, refrigeration, control, measurement and reset are outside this toy model. Apparatus efficiency is not returned.

THE DERIVATION

Order the state’s
eigenvalues.

H = diag(0, hf), E = hfp. With coherence c = κ√[p(1−p)], define r = √[(1−2p)² + 4|c|²]. A unitary preserves the state eigenvalues; assigning the larger to the ground state minimizes its final energy.

Werg = hf · (2p − 1 + r) / 2

At p = ¼, a pure coherent state has Werg = E. Its fully dephased counterpart has the same E and zero ergotropy. That difference concerns work accessibility, not energy creation.
