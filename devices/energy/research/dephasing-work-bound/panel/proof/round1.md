**Result.** The proposed bound holds for every admitted state, gap, and dephasing factor. With z=1−2p, r=|c|, R_d=√(z²+4d²r²), the spectrum and an explicitly attaining unitary give W(d)=ε(R_d−z)/2. The triangle inequality proves L≤εr(1−d), while squared-radius monotonicity proves L≥0. Lower equality holds exactly for r=0 or d=1. Upper equality holds exactly for r=0, d=1, or p=1/2. The coefficient is sharp on p=1/2 with r>0,d<1. The proof covers degenerate spectra, d=0, r=0, and p endpoints. No claim of novelty, apparatus implementation, or formal verification.

**Numerics.** Rationalized, dimensionless loss avoids cancellation and squaring tiny r. Joule products are separately regrouped so a normalized underflow can retain a finite Joule result. Exact integer-significand PSD validation admits only physical supplied binary64 values; a rounded decimal boundary can be rejected. The boundary helper returns the greatest admissible binary64 coherence. The analytic ratio is clipped at one for rounding, so testing this implementation's own upper inequality is not an independent verification of that bound.

**Limitations.** Binary64 range flags are not rigorous error bounds; subnormal relative error can be large.
