## Formalization inspection details

For family 372, `ElasticityUniqueness.lean` defines its boundary data as a variational trace quotient. Its opening note says the quotient norm is not identified there with fractional Sobolev norms in boundary charts. That is a further reason to compare the actual encoded definitions, not merely the English theorem title. The exported solution theorem is `OAI.Elasticity.global_uniqueness`, reached through the indicated `Rigidity` and `BoundaryJets` imports. Inspection of that final module is not a dependency audit.

For family 365, `Conductivity.lean` specifies two uniformly positive bounded measurable scalar conductivities on a three-dimensional ball with identical full weak Dirichlet-to-Neumann operator; unique weak solutions for each trace are part of the statement. The solution module exports `OAI.ScalarConductivity.main_nonuniqueness` via a weighted-source construction. Its English family heading does not change this exact proposition into a smooth-geometry uniqueness result.

The OpenAI Comparator README separately lists five configurations that check supporting results rather than the associated main theorems. This is a positive source-disclosure practice to preserve in our network. Failed direct requests for `lean/docs/265.md` and `268.md` returned 404; that alone was not treated as proof that the families have no formalization. The catalogue and exact available artifacts must decide status.

## Proposed adaptive second round

The most useful next step is an executable **claim-transfer audit**, selected by the advisor after peer findings, rather than another broad search. The frozen candidate contract in `round2-candidate-contract.json` proposes:

1. A positive finite-gap sequence with a vanishing limit, paired with a fixed positive-gap control. This tests the logical distinction between positivity at each size and a uniform physical lower bound. It is an elementary matrix counterexample, not a Yang–Mills counterexample.
2. Two finite-dimensional positive operators whose measured block agrees while the full operator differs. This demonstrates information loss in a specified partial observation, without claiming to refute family 372 or reproduce an elasticity PDE.
3. Typed source/claim records that reject transfer from companion formalization, topic vocabulary or an incomplete energy boundary, while admitting a genuinely identical narrow algebraic claim with a declared transformation.

Every failure example needs a matching legitimate control. A metadata validator can enforce documentary obligations and reject explicit mismatches; it cannot establish a theorem merely from correctly filled fields. No Round 2 experiment was executed in this round.

## Limits

The crosswalk does not certify either the entire OpenAI collection or the entire Yang–Mills research chain. It does not turn numerical replays into proof, a source’s “formalized” report into our own kernel run, or an unmet sufficient bound into proof of physical impossibility. Priority and scientific novelty remain separate investigations. Selected primary source dates and revisions are recorded in `sources.json`.
