Status: independent records proposal, before reading panel peers. Research cutoff: 21 October 2026. Role: government-program/patent evidence analyst model agent; no human or historical scientist participation. ## Finding and scope

The selected official records support real programs for externally supplied power transfer, exploratory energy research, and historical mechanism assessment. They do not establish energy generation without an identified source. Eight admitted records are listed in `sources.json`; three MARRS records share one program origin and are not three independent replications.

## Admitted distinctions

- **POWER / 16 May 2025:** the greater-than-800 W, 8.6 km, 30-second report is an agency demonstration claim. Greater-than-20% optical-to-electrical efficiency concerns shorter-distance tests. It excludes laser wall-plug cost and must not be relabeled the efficiency of the record-distance run.
- **ARPA-E / 17 February 2023:** the official index reports $10 million and eight LENR research projects. The direct page returned a JavaScript shell, so only title/date/indexed excerpt were inspected. Funding is not measured gain; current award completion and payment status remain unverified.
- **MARRS / 2025–2026:** an official solicitation and current program description exist. The February 26 FAQ explicitly separates a future gain objective from program metrics, requires more than calorimetry, and makes even brute-force proposals answer for trigger efficiency. No nuclear apparatus is proposed for this web experiment.
- **NASA BPP / 1996–2002, report 2004:** the review retains nonviable and unresolved tasks. Its 16-task count includes other sponsors; it is not 16 successful NASA devices.
- **Tesla US685957A / 1901:** radiant input and capacitor storage are explicit. A variant also has a separate local current source. Grant status and a historically asserted mechanism do not establish an unlimited-energy machine.
- **Gateway / 1983, released 2003:** the official report's inspected pages contain interpretation and proposed exercises, not a calibrated electrical-energy demonstration. Its custody is a provenance link, not a positive energy-validation edge.

## Source error retained, not silently repaired

MARRS BAA HR001126S0007, printed/PDF page 4, pairs “picobarn range” with `(10^-12 cm²)`. The downloaded official PDF SHA256 is `af99ec0fa3bb94f7a520e0b92f7e57e864e1800afa9089bf03e7343dd6434733`; the page image was visually checked. A barn is 10^-28 m² = 10^-24 cm², so a picobarn is 10^-36 cm². The printed value differs by a factor of 10^24. Record this as a unit inconsistency in that source version. Do not substitute a guessed physical cross section or infer that the entire program is disproven. Independent panel verification is requested for round 2.

## Proposed thesis and falsifier

**Thesis R-P1:** evidence can be made harder to overstate by requiring each graph edge to name its stage and energy boundary. A funding→research edge, patent→proposed mechanism edge, or record→historical assertion edge must never be displayed as demonstrated→net energy.

A source's reported efficiency is a typed quantity: `eta = E_useful_out / E_declared_input`, with both endpoints named. Optical-to-electrical, receiver-only, charging-to-ergotropy and wall-plug-to-load ratios are distinct observables. A reverse test is to remove the source label and ask whether the original denominator can be recovered. If it cannot, the card is incomplete.

Proposed acceptance checks for the site: every consequential program card has a document date, stage, actual reading depth, exact source locator, missing performance measurement and a source-family ID. No current-active label comes solely from a historical news date. Source assertions and this panel's inference must be visibly separated.

## Limitations and next record

This is a selected public-record review, not an exhaustive search, legal patent opinion, independent reproduction or search of classified programs. No messages were sent to institutions. The ARPA-E body and final project reports remain missing; newer award notices and full raw POWER input/output traces would change the evidence class. The source unit correction needs an independent arithmetic check. THREADS and an ARPA-E Stanford project were inspected as leads but not admitted to the eight-record evidence set; no performance claim is taken from them. Full third-party materials are not required for republication: source URLs, hashes and bounded notes suffice.
