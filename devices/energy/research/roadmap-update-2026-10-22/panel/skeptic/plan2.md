The decisive controls remain concrete. A successful receipt transplanted to another theorem/version must not establish local execution. Reversing only one reciprocal coupling, omitting mutual energy or nonzero Fv, and an mA/A mismatch must fail the six-state certificate; consistent signed coordinate changes must pass. Time-varying storage requires the extra half-z-transpose-Qdot-z term and explicit control accounting.

For E2, the correlated diagonal state with probabilities (1−ν,0,0,ν) and its product marginals have identical local populations/energy but different joint entropy. A reset cannot erase that distinction from the ledger. A four-state system swapped with a four-state thermal reference requires dimension16; sequential fresh-qubit swaps can fit dimension8 if the endpoint and used-bath records are retained. Actual work, its lower bound and measured hardware expenditure remain different fields.

For acoustics, arbitrary gain functions agreeing on every sampled point are untestable by those data. Fixed laboratory frequencies avoid estimated-pole adaptive covariance; otherwise that covariance must be propagated. The broad proposed parameter menu cannot mean 200 repetitions of every Cartesian combination under a2,000-record cap: the final scenario table must allocate records explicitly. Structural rank, conditioning and empirical interval coverage are separate acceptance gates.

For YM-P1, require actual approximant-product support, certified finite arithmetic and an analytic tail for the combined volume/exponential/boundary factor. The half-box all-N assertion and replacement of support volume by |R| remain rejection controls. Failed prerequisites produce an obstruction report, not retuning, an automatic fallback or a continuum claim.

Prioritize the minimal P0 interface, METHODS-1 and E1; then admit E2/A1 only after their stated conditions. Keep optional Lean bootstrap capped and nonblocking. Bosonic expansion, sensor optimization, model reduction, small-box Wilson signs and continuum-solve claims remain parked. Budgets are caps, not completion forecasts. This closes the review plus two planning rounds; no new research, implementation, simulation or experiment occurred.
