## Common acceptance rules

Every physical claim needs observable, units, input, receiver, calibration, alternatives and evidence status. Separate free-space wavelength from spherical mode wavelength; magnetic field from audio/EEG; amplitude from frequency; association from causal or agent attribution. Numeric proximity and graph adjacency are not mechanisms.

Synthetic controls must identify seed, model, null assumptions, statistic, selection family and scope. Independent checking must use a separate derivation or implementation. An attractive p-value does not identify a signal's origin. Source reading can support documentary findings without validating the source's underlying assertion.

No new human experiment or transmission is conducted. Design work may specify passive instrumentation and blinded evaluation; it must not claim to provide contact instructions or established health effects. Existing public-facing applications and archived scientific snapshots remain preserved.

Existing authorization permits GitHub/Pages publication and a merge attempt. Reuse public metadata and our original analyses, with source attribution and scope limits.
