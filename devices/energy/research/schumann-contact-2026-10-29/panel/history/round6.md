## Readiness versus authentication

The proposed independent lanes and unconditional false/null scientific flags are appropriate. A complete fabricated packet can satisfy date, hash and schema rules. The validator cannot thereby verify that the referenced bytes exist, match their claimed hashes, contain genuine calibration data, were recorded at the stated time, or came from independent custodians. Those are separate source-inspection tasks.

A declared original-event time basis is required, but self-attesting that basis is not proof. Likewise a 128-bit nonce declaration cannot establish actual secret unpredictability, proper generation, absence of early disclosure or unaltered response custody. The four-target unsalted-hash demonstration correctly exposes a concrete disclosure route; a salted example cannot certify the entire future experimental system.

For paired data, channel and timing completeness are necessary admission requirements. Passing a join cannot validate the inference: ordinary reporting schedules, electromagnetic pickup, missingness, common environmental drivers and information leakage still require independent controls. “Observational” is an origin declaration, not authentication. Synthetic fixture metadata must remain visibly synthetic.
