## What changes our research assessment?

The old sentence “no exact OpenAI catalogue target established” remains true of that dated, selected catalogue comparison; today's official existence announcement is a new related parent-goal claim and must be registered separately. It is not a public theorem statement we can presently align or check. Existence is one component of the Clay problem, not the positive mass-gap component. Neither confirmation nor contradiction of a particular local theorem follows from the announcement.

Our Round 33 admits restricted fixed-spacing, small-coupling-parameter results for named SU(2) lattice constructions. Its continuum obligation and uniqueness beyond the named limits remain open. The prior certificate obeys tau=96/g^4 and |tau|<=10^-8, so any trajectory g(a)->0 eventually exits its admitted regime. None of the newly public artifacts removes that obstacle.

No supported novelty claim is revoked by this comparison, because the earlier E1 audit already classified the qubit dephasing bound as prior art. Its earlier formula, upper-bound and endpoint sources still control that decision. No new originality claim follows from recombining the new catalogue with our network. Likewise, no inspected new paper disproves our established finite-model energy identity, thermal accounting or acoustic non-identifiability examples.

## Prioritized applicability gaps

1. **Proof-to-claim fidelity.** Retain manuscript, formal target, hypotheses, conclusion and actual execution status separately. The math and skeptic panels agree that challenge-file `sorry` placeholders are reference templates, not the solution modules. MUB's selected Lean bound <=5 is not the paper's <=3 result. A bounded execution of its restored Python checker can verify saved-record consistency, with its own receipt, without claiming C++ or theorem verification.
2. **Schumann inference.** The observation panel has 49 complete recent Kp intervals per provider, not calibrated seven-day Schumann waveforms. It found no complete Kp interval >=5 in those snapshots; that neither excludes a Schumann anomaly nor measures one. Its two station quick-looks support only qualitative inspection. Highest-value work is a forward model and falsification test separating forcing, cavity poles, receiver gain and reported spectral maxima.
3. **Wave-theorem transfer.** The mathematics panel identifies Euclidean half-wave/Sobolev and endpoint qualifications. Applying family 079 to a curved, lossy, forced Earth–ionosphere boundary problem needs a new operator/domain and perturbation/error bridge. Shared wave terminology is insufficient.
4. **SAT transfer.** The formal subexponential bound is weaker than the manuscript's sharper asymptotic exponent. Neither supplies a practical brute-force accelerator. A finite encoding, correctness proof, size/constant bounds and observed runtime are required before integration.
5. **YM next work.** Preserve Round 33's explicit route-B dynamics, broader-boundary uniqueness and finite-box-certificate goals. Its unexecuted roadmap cannot be relabeled as completed by another group's announcement. Await public physics statements for a dimension/group/axioms/coupling/limit dictionary.
