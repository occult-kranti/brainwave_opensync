# Novelty and SOTA gate before any next three-round cycle

Date: 29 October 2026. Advisor decision after four specialist literature reviews and a separate primary-source Yang–Mills follow-up. This gate responds to the user's explicit request to check prior art **before** spending more rounds on a proof. It is not a fourth research round. No next three-round cycle has started.

## Decision

**Do not start another discovery cycle on the present oscillator identities, spectral-compensation counterexample or calibrated inverse.** Exact precedents contain their mathematical ingredients, and several proposed Schumann extensions are also already published. Retain the independently checked implementation as a benchmark and educational tool. No new fundamental novelty or precisely demonstrated unsolved gap has been established.

**Conditional next task: a bounded replication of the published Nagycenk processed-data/model-curve benchmark.** This has an accessible small CC BY 4.0 archive and a close primary paper. It becomes executable only after the target curve/statistic, source geometry, component, estimator, schema discrepancy, model version and numerical acceptance criteria are frozen. It is replication, not a promised discovery. A new inference/theorem cycle requires a further exact problem definition and literature comparison.

## Recent methods: a benchmark must match their assumptions

The screen includes 2025–2026 Bayesian operational modal methods, latent-excitation models, multirate unknown-input filters and PSD-ratio modal identification. Some primary bodies were inspected; other recent publisher pages exposed only abstracts or returned access errors. They remain baseline leads, not audited theorems or reproduced performance. The detailed reports distinguish these cases. We do not claim a complete ranking of all current SOTA.

Two safeguards matter. First, a new method must beat an assumptions-matched classical estimator, not only an intentionally naive peak picker. Second, a restriction must actually exclude the existing counterexample. For our second-order models, H₂/H₁ is a proper stable causal rational response, even when the known mass is held fixed. Merely adding receiver stability or causality does not create uniqueness. Under the different restriction of constant positive real gain, frequency diversity already recovers poles by elementary coefficient ratios. Neither case is a new discovery.

A further known-model qualification makes the scope explicit: with one exact displacement spectrum, flat unknown forcing and no sensor coloring/noise, the reciprocal PSD is quadratic in ω². Its coefficient ratios can identify the positive pole parameters. That restricted example and our freely varying-source ambiguity coexist. Proposed future questions must specify their source, receiver and noise classes rather than alternate between incompatible assumptions.

## Data feasibility before another cycle

1. **Nagycenk benchmark — conditional replication candidate.** [Zenodo 13220677](https://zenodo.org/records/13220677), CC BY 4.0, about 278 kB of processed historical/February 2023 products and model curves. Two small files were inspected for schema only, with retained bytes, hashes and attribution. A source-radius column differs from the metadata description and must be reconciled before fitting. These are not raw waveforms or a complete calibration archive.
2. **Tacza event archive — partial reproduction only at present.** [Fairdata dataset](https://etsin.fairdata.fi/dataset/5d78867e-475b-40e9-8800-f7e2ef4487ed), CC BY-NC 4.0, six listed files totaling 17,499,814 bytes. Official metadata describes processed event windows. Those products can support event-window replay after download/schema checks, but do not alone reconstruct the eligible non-event continuous series used for the published random-epoch null. The archive was not numerically replayed here.
3. **Recent October 23–29 anomaly — insufficient current packet.** The saved Kp series is a different observable, and the quick looks lack a calibrated numeric SR baseline. Historical data cannot fill that missing recent timeline.
4. **Larger or rights-ambiguous archives — not immediate substitutes.** The observational review identifies a roughly 26.7-GB raw Sierra Nevada archive and a Drozdovo record with conflicting reuse text. Neither was bulk-downloaded or silently treated as a calibrated ready dataset.

The observed availability gap is not proof that the data do not exist elsewhere. No external contact, paid feed or restricted download was initiated. Full retrieval and byte-level provenance must precede any actual replication claim.

## Required admission record for the next three rounds

A future proposal must contain all of the following before execution:

- **Exact contribution:** mathematical proposition or measurable empirical improvement, including domain, observable, nuisance class, units and intended generality.
- **Closest prior result:** theorem/equation/experiment locator, date, actual reading depth, hypothesis comparison and the precise residual gap. If it is contained in prior work, label it replication.
- **Feasible evidence:** licensed accessible records or controlled apparatus, calibration, uncertainty and a baseline suited to the claim. State missing controls explicitly.
- **Discriminating test:** matched classical/SOTA baseline, untouched evaluation cases, uncertainty/false-alarm or bound criterion, and conditions that would reject the proposed advantage.
- **Advisor decision:** go as novelty research, go as replication/engineering, needs evidence, or no-go. Freeze this record before workers begin. Change a failed target only through an explicit new decision.

The current decision is **no-go for rediscovering the existing core formulas**, **conditional replication for the small Nagycenk benchmark**, and **needs evidence plus exact prior-art comparison for a joint source/receiver/cavity inference problem**. No next cycle is authorized by consensus alone.
