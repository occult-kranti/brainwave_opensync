What must be specified before more rounds?

  1. State the exact proposition or measurable improvement, with domains, units, observables and nuisance assumptions.
  2. Map it against the nearest primary theorem or experiment, including sections, assumptions, dates and actual reading depth. Search failure is not novelty evidence.
  3. Identify a residual gap that the cited work does not already solve. Separate theoretical novelty, empirical extension and useful replication.
  4. Secure the needed dataset or apparatus calibration; document licenses, raw bytes, uncertainty and what cannot be reproduced.
  5. Predeclare an assumptions-matched baseline, untouched evaluation cases, meaningful success metric and rejection/stop criteria.
  6. Freeze an advisor go/no-go decision before any next three-round cycle. Keep the existing cycle closed and preserve its superseded failures.
