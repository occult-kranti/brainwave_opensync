01 / THE WORKBENCH

Put the idea
under a little pressure.

Three physical systems. Adjustable conditions, explicit boundaries, and outputs you can download and inspect.

Every result here is simulated. These models do not establish a working hardware prototype, therapeutic effect, gravity control, or energy creation.

METHODS, NOT MAGIC

What can AI research
bring to a physical experiment?

Ideas, code, and candidate searches become useful when their constraints, checks, and failures stay visible. A new AI result is evidence about its reported task; it is not a substitute for a device measurement.

02 / THE HYPOTHESIS NOTEBOOK

risks being wrong.

These are proposed investigations. Each connects a use case to a prediction, a control, and a result that would count against it.

03 / CONNECTIONS WITH CONTEXT

Trace the idea.
Keep the distinctions.

A historical text, a patent, and a measurement answer different questions. Connections below describe the relationship, without turning association into proof.

FROM SIMULATION TO MEASUREMENT

The apparatus has to
answer the question.

A compelling rendering is only a way to discuss a setup. A test needs a defined input, calibrated measurements, a control condition, and an uncertainty budget.

  1. 01
    Define the observable

    Choose a measurable quantity and units before interpreting a result.

  2. 02
    Close the budget

    Include the source, load, losses, storage, and measurement bandwidth.

  3. 03
    Try to disprove it

    Compare a control, repeat the observation, and report disagreement.
