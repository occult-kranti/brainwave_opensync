# Archive preset practice guides

Updated 2026-10-29. Scope: `archive/dark-landscapes`; master is unchanged.

## User-facing behavior

Each preset has five explanations: why the sound was designed this way, what a listener may notice, how to try it, how often to consider repeating it, and the evidence limits. Explore shows a compact disclosure and full details; Listen exposes the same guide. Edited Studio sessions are identified so the original description is not mistaken for the current recipe.

OBE exploration has explicit subgroups for preparation, Gateway-inspired listening, hypnagogic and dream awareness, astral imagery, and remote-viewing journaling. These are intentions and practices, not verified outcomes. Related ordinary relaxation/dreaming presets may appear in several collections. Mathematical-pattern listening has its own collection. Existing setup, duration, sound, grade, search and infant restrictions remain in force.

## Interpretation rules

- Pitch, interaural difference, acoustic beating and pulse rate are different quantities. No sound setting measures the listener's EEG.
- The Bashar scale illustration uses a chosen constant `k = 5,000`, fitted to `200,000 ↔ 40 Hz`. The resulting arithmetic is reproducible; this is not a calibrated conversion of consciousness to sound.
- Golden-ratio pitch steps use `f[n] = 110 × φ^n`, with `φ = (1 + √5)/2`. These are pitch intervals; noninteger ratios are not a harmonic series.
- Body heaviness, imagery or a floating sensation may be reported subjectively. They do not establish external perception, contact or literal departure from the body. No OBE induction rate has been measured for these presets.
- Starter schedules are optional editorial trial plans. They are not research-backed dosages, and longer or more frequent listening is not presumed better. Overnight catalog duration is not an instruction to listen all night.
- Original sound beds do not reproduce Hemi-Sync recordings or source practitioners' complete methods. The supplied Bashar transcript digests remain unverified; new official source links establish provenance only.
- The existing loader keeps at most eight phases. Guides for longer stored recipes disclose the loaded duration and truncation in the explanation, instructions and limits; this change does not alter that playback behavior.
