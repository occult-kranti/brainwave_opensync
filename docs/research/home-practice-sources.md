## Decisions and limits

- The two-week planner, three/five/seven-session choices, two-session sequence spacing, quiet days and 10/20/45-minute budgets are **app choices**. They are not copied from a claimed universal official timetable.
- An owned commercial recording uses its complete player-reported duration. Forty-five minutes is a reserved window, not a verified duration for every edition. Shorter windows offer an original companion or quiet practice instead of encouraging a mid-instruction cut.
- Sleep-oriented material receives an explicit bedtime label rather than silently appearing in an alert morning schedule.
- The original 5/15/35-minute companion durations are checked against the existing catalog. They do not reproduce commercial narration, complete methods, or proprietary signals.
- No schedule persists outside component state; there are no reminders, messages, calendar writes, streak penalties or audio starts.
- Community reports and source metaphysical interpretations remain separate from measured clinical, neurological or remote-perception outcomes.
