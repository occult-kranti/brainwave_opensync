# Levitation: historical, patent and government provenance

Audit date: 2026-10-16. This selected inventory contains **13 source records**, including two Chinese documents about one program, two modern Russian grant records, and one university patent record from each country. It is not an exhaustive search of patents, books, national programs or classified material. It supplements the lab’s physical models; it does not establish a technological connection simply because two documents mention frequency or resonance.

The historical-physics-panel and tesla-mechanism-resonance research guides informed this audit: identify the actual passage, separate testimony from measurement, trace energy and momentum, and retain uncertainty about inaccessible or incomplete tests. Primary documents were located with public web searches and inspected at the reading depth specified below. Government custody, institutional funding and patent ownership establish provenance; they do not automatically validate performance.

## What the inspected sources establish

### Newton’s General Scholium

[Newton Project, 1729 English edition, pp. 387–393](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/NATP00056). The full normalized General Scholium transcription was read; the entire *Principia* was not. Page 388 distinguishes falling bodies from air resistance. Pages 392–393 distinguish the empirically described gravitational law from an unidentified cause. The final discussion of a subtle spirit acknowledges insufficient experiments to establish precise laws.

This supplies historical context for physical and theological inquiry. It does not supply a measurable bridge from occult terminology to gravity manipulation. The date labels this translation edition, not the first appearance of the General Scholium.

### Tesla’s 1892 lecture

[Experiments with Alternate Currents of High Potential and High Frequency](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/13476/13476-h/13476-h.htm). Selected tuning/capacitance passages, the Fig. 17 motor discussion and descriptions of suspended lamps were read in the public-domain transcription. This is a later transcription of Tesla’s account, not an independently instrumented replication.

The motor is powered; capacitance and phase matter to its rotation. A suspended plate has an electrical role, and lamps described as suspended are physically supported. Those words are not evidence of unsupported levitation. Electrical resonance can exchange stored energy and concentrate amplitude while requiring input and incurring loss. No sound-based gravity-cancellation mechanism follows from these passages.

### Tesla’s aircraft patent

[US1655114A, granted 1928-01-03](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1655114A/en). Both original drawing sheets, the complete description, all six claims and the 1928-03-20 correction were inspected in the eight-page scan. OCR equations are unreliable; no numerical performance was calculated from them. The application was filed 1927-10-04 and identifies an earlier application. The related method patent US1655113 is not counted as another discovered levitation technology.

Figures 1–2 show a propeller-equipped aircraft changing attitude between vertical and horizontal flight. Claims 1–4 concern power, tilt and control; claims 5–6 concern wheel arrangements. The text accounts for weight through vertical propeller thrust and wing lift, and names a fluid-pressure energy supply. The patent is a proposed aircraft design, not a flight-test dataset or an antigravity patent. No efficiency figure is accepted merely because its inventor claims it.

### NASA Breakthrough Propulsion Physics

[NASA/TM-2004-213082, May 2004](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20040070788), [official PDF](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20040070788/downloads/20040070788.pdf). The report body on printed pp. 1–8 was read, including sections 3.1.4, 3.2.3 and 3.2.7. Its bibliography was not fully audited. This is a historical assessment of work associated with the 1996–2002 program, not a 2026 state-of-the-art review.

The distinction between failed, unresolved and incomplete work matters. NASA’s Cavendish-style superconducting test suffered radiofrequency interference with instrumentation, so it was not a clean gravitational null. The fuller MSFC Podkletnov replication did not complete the intended hardware with available funding. The completed 2003 null replication cited by the report belongs to Hathaway, Cleveland and Bao; attributing it to NASA would be wrong. Their original article was not independently reviewed here.

The program’s goals were research questions. Neither its name nor its examination of vacuum-energy and gravity ideas establishes a propulsion breakthrough. The report also discusses capacitor/lifter results consistent with ordinary forces in gas.

### NASA capacitor tests

[NASA/TM-2004-213283, June 2004](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20040171466), [official PDF](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20040171466/downloads/20040171466.pdf). The exact title is *NASA Marshall Space Flight Center Barrel-Shaped Asymmetrical Capacitor*. Reading covered the introduction, background, section 6 vacuum tests, associated captions and section 9 summary, rather than all 62 PDF pages.

For the tested apparatus, rotation diminished below measurement capability with reduced surrounding gas and returned when air was restored. This discriminates a gas-mediated force from the asserted vacuum effect. It is a bounded experimental result with an apparatus and sensitivity, not a proof that all conceivable anomalous forces are impossible. No onboard propellant tank does not mean no momentum exchange: surrounding gas can carry momentum.

### NASA mechanical-claim controls

[NASA/TM-2006-214390, December 2006](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20070004897), [official PDF](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20070004897/downloads/20070004897.pdf). Printed pp. 1–6 and 12–14 were read. The detailed gyro derivations on intervening pages were not fully audited.

Its useful distinction is between motion of one component and motion of the isolated system’s center of mass. An internal mechanism can exploit ground friction and move a chassis; the ground then supplies an external interaction. Oscillation, torque, cable forces, sensor coupling and balance dynamics require controls before a signal is called sustained thrust. The report is a methodological source, not a universal test of every mechanism.

### Navy-assigned inertial-mass patent

[US10144532B2, granted 2018-12-04](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10144532B2/en). The six-page original scan’s front sheet, both drawings, full description, equations and four claims were inspected. Filing date is 2016-04-28. The front sheet identifies Salvatore Cezar Pais as inventor and the United States represented by the Secretary of the Navy as assignee.

The patent asserts that powered cavities modify vacuum behavior and inertia. The drawings include a power plant. The missing bridge is independent evidence that the stated electromagnetic arrangement produces the claimed inertial or gravitational result after ordinary forces and instrumental effects are controlled. The patent’s equations and dimensional arguments are not a measured dataset or a demonstrated complete energy/momentum balance. Neither classified prototypes nor their absence can be inferred from this record. No apparatus settings or hazardous construction instructions are reproduced.

### Chinese and Russian university patents

[CN102384983A](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN102384983A/zh), published 2012-03-21, is an application for an ultrasonic levitation accelerometer. The original Chinese cover names Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics. Its [official profile](https://arf2023.nuaa.edu.cn/18084/list.htm), institutional-history paragraphs, verifies national ministry participation in the university. The original claims 1–3 and figure 1 were visually inspected; Chinese description and translated text were read. The proposal uses powered acoustic support and capacitive sensing, retaining gravity in its force balance. Publication is not proof of a grant or calibrated performance. Current legal status was not independently verified; the mirror’s summary and event history should not be treated as an official register.

[RU2539304C2](https://patents.google.com/patent/RU2539304C2/ru), published 2015-01-20, identifies the federal state-budgetary Petersburg State Transport University as proprietor. Original front sheets, full description, sole claim and all three figures were inspected. The proposal relocates powered magnetic rotation from vehicle to track. Its statement about avoiding onboard levitation power therefore does not establish zero total input. No independent force or efficiency dataset is supplied. This is a Russian Federation record, not a Soviet program. Both patent summaries are English paraphrases of original-language records, not certified translations or present ownership opinions.

## Energy and connection audit

“Free energy” needs an explicit definition. Recovering previously stored energy, receiving externally supplied electromagnetic power, reducing losses, and producing net useful energy without a source are different propositions. The inspected sources do not provide an independently checked demonstration of the last proposition. Their common use of fields, oscillation or vacuum vocabulary is insufficient to connect their mechanisms.

For any future claim, specify the system boundary, external forces and momentum flow, supplied energy, stored-energy change and losses. A smaller scale reading can come from an added upward force or acceleration without a change in gravity. A resonant device can have a large internal amplitude without a corresponding new source of energy. Numerical simulations demonstrate consequences of their assumptions; they cannot validate an assumed gravity-control term by reproducing it.

## Two proposed digital controls

The typed data include two conceptual experiments. One follows the center of mass of a chassis and internal mass with and without external friction. The other separates support force, gravity, applied force and acceleration. For constrained vertical motion the ideal relation is `N = m(g + a) − F_up`; unilateral contact must be handled when the body leaves the scale. Neither is a new physical finding.

All extensions here remain digital or conceptual. No high-voltage, mains, radiofrequency, cryogenic, high-power acoustic or heated-material assembly procedure is provided. Original patent drawings were inspected for provenance; they are not adopted as validated construction plans.
