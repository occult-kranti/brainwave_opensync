# Listening, hypnosis and traditions: evidence review

Reviewed 15 October 2026. Supports `/meditate`, `/hypnosis`, `/traditions` and fifteen new original presets. This is a targeted primary-source review, not a systematic review or a clinical guideline. Searches covered hypnotic suggestion, sleep-onset cognition, auditory beats, contemporary Russian/Chinese work, official archives and primary spiritual texts. Inaccessible full texts are identified rather than treated as inspected evidence. No recording or commercial hypnosis script was copied.

## What the evidence supports

The strongest distinction is between a signal we can calculate, an auditory response we can measure, and a psychological or health outcome requiring a separate controlled test. Matching an audio-envelope rate to the name of an EEG band does not establish the third step. Hypnosis experiments involve instructions and responsiveness; sleep experiments depend on timing and measured state. A fixed browser soundtrack does not supply either measurement.

## Mathematics and what it does not imply

For separate-ear tones, the nominal binaural difference is

`Δf = |fR − fL|`.

The engine uses left = carrier and right = carrier + difference: 180 and 186 Hz give 6 Hz. This subtraction describes the stimulus arrangement, not a guaranteed percept or an EEG measurement. Stereo headphones preserve the intended ear separation.

For physically summed tones,

`cos(2πf1t) + cos(2πf2t) = 2 cos(π(f2−f1)t) cos(2π((f1+f2)/2)t)`.

The signed envelope reverses sign; its magnitude peaks at the difference rate. Confusing signed-envelope frequency with loudness repetition creates a factor-of-two error.

Sinusoidal amplitude modulation,

`x(t) = A[1 + m cos(2πfmt)] cos(2πfct)`,

has components at `fc` and `fc ± fm`, for modulation depth `0 ≤ m ≤ 1`. Isochronic gating has a different waveform and additional envelope harmonics. A 40 Hz pulse rate means a 25 ms repetition period; it does not mean a speaker emits a standalone 40 Hz sinusoid, a tissue receives a calibrated mechanical dose, or a listener enters a gamma state.

Musical ratios specify pitch relationships: `330/220 = 3/2`. A square-plate eigenmode model relates nodal patterns to geometry, boundary conditions and drive frequency. The fullscreen sand view uses a fixed illustrative fundamental instead of scaling the plate with the carrier, which previously made the ratio—and thus the selected pattern—nearly constant. The image is not a real sand measurement or a readout of consciousness.

Esoteric “vibration”, clinical EEG rhythms, sound pressure, electromagnetic fields and mechanical displacement are different quantities. A proposed connection needs explicit units, a transfer mechanism, calibrated exposure and an outcome definition. Numerically similar hertz values alone do not supply those missing links.

## Original recipes and product boundaries

Eight ordinary/experimental listening designs and seven archive-inspired experiments are registered with the existing catalog. All include explicit sound layers, phase levels and an automatic stop. All seven archive presets and the theta/dream-edge experiments are grade D, in the Experimental group, with that status in their titles. Their chosen rates and durations are disclosed. Existing sounds, source pages, previews, export, phase editing and safety controls remain available.

The guide ranks the entire catalog using explicit intention, available time and speakers/headphones. It excludes infant presets, hides adult suggestions in infant mode, and requires opt-in for grade D/Experimental sounds. It does not infer diagnosis, mood, EEG or readiness for hypnosis. A recommendation loads a bounded session but does not start playback.

A useful personal comparison fixes device/level/duration, compares silence, sound bed and rhythmic addition in varied order, and records comfort before and after alongside tiredness and expectation. Repeated subjective preference can inform a listening choice. It cannot by itself establish entrainment, a medical treatment or an occult mechanism.
