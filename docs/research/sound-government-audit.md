# Public sound-research records: USA, China and Russia

Audit date: 2026-10-16. Eleven selected records: USA 5, China 3, Russia 3. This inventory does not claim all national programs, classified work, patents or clinical studies have been found. A public record can establish provenance, funding or institutional activity without establishing a treatment benefit. Official institutional reports are identified separately from full trial papers.

The corresponding UI data is `src/sound-research/government.ts`. Its access field must remain visible alongside each record. English summaries of Chinese and Russian records are translations/paraphrases, not official translations. Dates below describe the record or report, not necessarily the experiment date.

## USA

### NIH: Sound Health concept

[Original NCCIH record](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/grants/concepts/the-science-of-music-interventions-a-sound-health-initiative), council date **2018-06-01**. Read the complete Background, Purpose and Objectives sections. The concept proposes staged research with progression criteria, not established effectiveness. [NCCIH’s timeline](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/about/nccih-timeline) separately records the initiative’s January 2017 launch workshop. The 2019 NIH award-news page returned HTTP 403, so no funding amount is carried into the UI from that blocked page.

### NEA: completed Sound Health Network

[Original NEA record](https://www.arts.gov/initiatives/sound-health-network). Read the entire short page. It identifies **2020–2025** collaboration with UCSF and says the wider Sound Health partnership ended in 2025. This qualifies older NIH pages written in the present tense: the record is historical, not proof of an active 2026 partnership. Page publication date is absent; network activity and partnership ending are distinct from the fate of individual research projects.

### Army assessment in the CIA archive: Gateway

[Official GovInfo scan](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-D101-PURL-gpo152943/pdf/GOVPUB-D101-PURL-gpo152943.pdf), memorandum dated **1983-06-09**, identifier CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210016-5. All **29 available scan pages** were reviewed through locally generated OCR, with selected visual checks. Original page 25 is absent: section 34 jumps to 37. This is a review of the available scan, not an assertion of a complete manuscript.

Checked the cover memorandum; sections 1–10 on Hemi-Sync and proposed mechanisms; 11–27 on consciousness and speculative physics; 28–33 on exercises and claimed information gathering; the available belief-system discussion; sections 37–38 and bibliography. The author recommends further experiments and makes favorable assertions, but supplies no controlled clinical trial establishing those claims. Section 33 itself discusses distorted information. Vendor testimony and physical analogies do not validate healing or paranormal outcomes.

[CIA catalog/PDF retrieval](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r001700210016-5) still redirected; GovInfo supplied the official alternative. The 1988 National Academies evaluation was located only at index level and is not treated as read.

The [1995 evaluation draft locator](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00791r000200180005-5) and [Soviet and East European Parapsychology Research locator](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/nsa-rdp96x00790r000100010041-2) redirected to the archive homepage. ### FDA: focused-ultrasound device order

[Original four-page order DEN220087](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf22/DEN220087.pdf), **2023-10-06**. All pages read, including indications, controls and labeling. ## Russia

[Original laboratory page](https://www.infran.ru/наука/научные-подразделения/лаборатория-физиологии-слуха/о-лаборатории/). Complete substantive research description read. ### Pavlov Institute: speech and auditory training

[Original laboratory page](https://www.infran.ru/наука/научные-подразделения/лаборатория-психофизиологии-речи/). Read the full history, applied-training and current-research paragraphs. The **undated** page references the Pavlov Center’s **2020–2025** project period and developments from 2022–2024. Clinical use is reported institutionally, without controlled numerical outcomes. Rehabilitation software is an interactive training context; it cannot serve as efficacy evidence for a passive sound generator.

[Official grant 24-28-01252](https://rscf.ru/project/24-28-01252/). Read general details, proposal, annual reports for **2024 and 2025**, publication lists and practical-use section. Listed 2024–2026 papers were not reviewed in full, and no clinical efficacy conclusion is assigned to this grant record.

## Gaps and interpretation

- The country totals reflect this selected search, not comparative national investment or scientific quality.
- Patent databases and classified-program claims are not comprehensively audited. No patent is treated as proof of benefit, and no rumor is promoted into a public finding.
- Russia coverage here is institutional and grant-based; it is not a review of Russian clinical music-treatment trials. China coverage includes public project and research reports; linked full-paper retrieval gaps remain explicit.
- Gateway was reviewed from the official GovInfo scan, with its missing page disclosed. The full 1995 remote-viewing evaluation and Soviet assessment remain retrieval gaps; the CIA retrospective does not replace them.
- NIH/NEA programs, cognitive experiments, auditory rehabilitation and medical ultrasound belong to different evidence categories. Their presence in one inventory does not establish a shared mechanism or justify transferring a clinical result to browser audio.
