# Sound, harmony and vibration: four primary-text studies

Research date: 2026-10-16. This is a close reading of identified passages in four accessible primary texts, not a claim to have reviewed every edition, occult tradition or sound-healing publication. The reader-facing records are in `src/sound-research/texts.ts`.

Each reading keeps three things separate: the author’s intended account, a charitable modern question inspired by it, and the evidence needed for that question. The mathematical expressions below are our translations into explicitly defined variables; they are not equations attributed to the historical authors. No original recording, commercial script or paid text has been reproduced. There are no extended quotations.

## 1. Plato: proportion without an absolute frequency

**Primary text and access.** [Timaeus, Benjamin Jowett translation](https://classics.mit.edu/Plato/timaeus.html), especially 35b–36b and 47c–47e. The complete English text is accessible. The host does not identify a particular print edition of Jowett’s nineteenth-century translation. The work is a fourth-century BCE dialogue; modern Hz units must not be projected into it.

**What the passage does.** The speaker constructs the world-soul using portions in the sequence 1, 2, 3, 4, 9, 8, 27. Arithmetic and harmonic means fill intervals, yielding relations including 3:2, 4:3 and 9:8, with a residual 256:243. Later, harmony and rhythm are connected to the ordering of the soul. This joins mathematics, cosmology and ethical education. It is richer than the claim that an isolated pitch has a special effect.

**A defensible calculation.** Between 1 and 2, the arithmetic mean is 3/2 and the harmonic mean is 4/3. The ratio of these means is 9/8. Filling a fourth with two such tones leaves

\[
\frac{4/3}{(9/8)^2}=\frac{256}{243}.
\]

If a modern sound illustration assigns the first term a root \(f_0\), the other pitches are ratios multiplied by \(f_0\). Choosing 220 Hz produces a fifth at 330 Hz; choosing 440 Hz produces 660 Hz. Both preserve the relation. Thus the source does not choose a unique modern frequency. A fifth spans \(1200\log_2(3/2)\approx701.955\) cents, approximately 1.955 cents above a seven-semitone equal-tempered fifth.

**Constructive reading and failure point.** Proportional listening can train comparison, attention and musical understanding. That interpretation does not establish that the world-soul is a physical oscillator. Moving from a numerical ratio to biological repair requires an independently measured interaction, dose and outcome; none follows from the construction.

**Proposed question.** With order balanced and labels hidden, compare interval preference across roots and timbres. Separate recognition of tuning differences from preference. A stable preference would concern the tested listeners and sounds, not establish a cosmology or a treatment.

## 2. Inayat Khan: the listener and the situation matter

**Primary text and access.** [Volume II preface](https://wahiduddin.net/mv2/II/II_0.htm); Part I, [The Silent Life](https://wahiduddin.net/mv2/II/II_1.htm), [Vibrations](https://wahiduddin.net/mv2/II/II_2.htm), and [Harmony](https://wahiduddin.net/mv2/II/II_3.htm); Part II, [Chapter XIII, The Healing Power of Music](https://wahiduddin.net/mv2/II/II_21.htm). These are publicly accessible full chapters in Richard Shelquist’s Wahiduddin transcription of compiled lectures. The preface dates the first publication of *The Mysticism of Sound* to 1923 and describes *Music* as a series of 1921 lectures with additional papers. It does not establish the date of Chapter XIII individually. No first-edition facsimile was collated.

**What the text means.** In Part I, sound and vibration belong to a Sufi account of manifestation, spirit and return to peace. In *Harmony*, musical language also becomes practical advice about listening, patient turn-taking, vocal tone, context and consideration for another person. These ethical and interpersonal meanings should not be collapsed into a table of pitches.

Chapter XIII connects music, sympathetic care, rhythm and spiritual development. It also makes sweeping claims about the mental or spiritual origin of illness. Those statements are the author’s claims, not established conclusions of this project. Crucially, the same chapter narrates a hospital concert whose listeners wanted the musicians to leave. It explicitly considers whether music fits the person and the circumstances. The anecdote is not controlled evidence, but it is a counterexample within the text to an unconditional beneficial effect.

**A defensible translation.** Replace the undefined universal vibration with separately measurable musical features: tempo, amplitude envelope, spectral brightness, vocal pitch contour and familiarity. Keep the outcome specific, such as a post-listening comfort rating. A possible comparison is selected versus assigned music with expectations recorded. Any such design is ours; it does not quantify the soul or reproduce Khan’s healing practice.

**Constructive reading and failure point.** The emphasis on care, expressive voice and individual preference motivates useful questions about listening context. It does not justify diagnosing illness from a voice, blaming a person’s thoughts or lack of love for illness, or substituting a chosen tone for treatment. The chapter itself acknowledges the usefulness of external treatment and that one rule does not cover everything; that qualification should remain visible alongside its broader metaphysical assertions.

## 3. The Kybalion: separate the analogy from its physical claims

**Primary text and access.** [Project Gutenberg eBook 14209](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/14209/pg14209.html), complete Chapter IX and Chapter II’s summary of the principle. The accessed transcription prints *Three Initiates* as the author and **1912** on its title page. This record preserves that edition date rather than silently substituting another publication date. Its assertion of an ancient oral lineage is internal to the book; the transcription does not authenticate that lineage.

**The argument, read in sequence.** Chapter IX first presents motion as universal, then moves among thermal motion, planetary motion, particles and a proposed ether. An imagined accelerating object proceeds from audible sound through heat, colors and radiation, before the analogy is extended to mental and spiritual states. The closing paragraphs claim deliberate control of mental states and influence over others. The text is proposing a unified metaphysical scale and a practice of mental change, not simply describing an audio oscillator.

**A defensible translation.** For an identified periodic quantity, \(f=1/T\) and \(x(t)=A\cos(2\pi ft+\phi)\) are meaningful. They require definitions of what changes, how it is measured and over what interval. Frequency in inverse seconds does not identify the underlying quantity: pressure, displacement, electric field and an observer’s rating remain different variables. The book supplies no instrument or calibrated mapping from a mental condition to a frequency in Hz.

**Counterexample and constructive remainder.** A pressure wave and an electric signal can repeat 440 times per second without becoming the same phenomenon. Increasing an audio oscillator’s frequency does not by itself convert its sound into visible light. An object can heat and radiate through other energy-transfer processes; this does not validate the imagined single ladder. As a metaphor, however, the passage can invite attention to changing experience and deliberate interpretation. That possibility is distinct from its physical and paranormal claims.

**Proposed question.** Present the identical audio under neutral and contemplative descriptions, with randomized order and a clear explanation afterwards. Compare reported engagement or comfort. Such an exercise examines framing and attention; it neither measures spiritual rank nor demonstrates influence between minds.

## 4. Helmholtz: an acoustic comparison that has an observable prediction

**Primary text and access.** [On the Sensations of Tone, full OCR](https://archive.org/stream/onsensationsofto00helmrich/onsensationsofto00helmrich_djvu.txt), [University of California scan record](https://archive.org/details/onsensationsofto00helmrich), Part II, Chapter VIII, printed pages 159–166. The title and bibliographical notice identify the **1895 third English edition**, reprinted from the 1885 edition, translated and annotated by Alexander J. Ellis in accordance with the fourth German edition of 1877. The selected pages and edition notice were read; this is not a claim to have read all appendices. OCR has evident transcription errors. The volume explicitly marks translator additions in brackets, which must not be attributed automatically to Helmholtz.

**What is established in the passage.** Helmholtz begins by distinguishing the objective motions of vibrating bodies from the sensations heard by a listener. He contrasts interference and beats with combinational tones, describes reinforcement and cancellation, and uses a double siren to explain why beat count follows frequency difference. That distinction gives this historical source a useful role alongside the metaphysical texts: it identifies what to vary and what to observe.

**Modern calculation.** For two equal-amplitude tones mixed in the same channel,

\[
x(t)=A\cos(2\pi f_1t)+A\cos(2\pi f_2t)
=2A\cos\!\bigl(\pi(f_2-f_1)t\bigr)\cos\!\bigl(\pi(f_1+f_2)t\bigr).
\]

The signed envelope reverses sign; its magnitude repeats at \(|f_2-f_1|\). The linear spectrum contains the two input frequencies, with no extra spectral line at their difference. This differs from carrier-plus-sine-amplitude-modulation, which has a carrier and two sidebands. It also differs from presenting one tone to each ear. The chapter is not evidence that an EEG rhythm equals the beat rate.

**A useful counterexample.** Two equal-frequency waves can cancel at a point when their phases differ by half a cycle; in phase they reinforce. A frequency list is therefore insufficient to specify even the local sound. The historical passage also uses loudness language around an amplitude-squared relation; in modern analysis that relation concerns acoustic intensity under stated conditions, not a universal law of subjective loudness.

**Proposed question and connection to this app.** Count envelope maxima and inspect FFT lines for a short, quiet two-tone simulation, then compare the result with explicit sine AM. The current app’s live shared-channel mode uses two tones while preview/export uses sine AM; the research presets disclose that existing difference. A successful acoustic demonstration validates its signal arithmetic, not a claim of healing.

## Scope and next evidence

These readings support specific historical interpretations and small questions about acoustics, attention, choice and context. Proposed comparisons have not been conducted here. They are not clinical experiments. No Hz value is assigned to a spiritual quality, no historical claim is promoted to biological evidence, and no Newton or Tesla quotation is added without a relevant primary passage. A separate evidence review is needed for any claim about a patient outcome.
