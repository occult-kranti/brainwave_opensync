# Sound traditions and commercial products: selected source survey

Checked 2026-10-16. This is a selected source survey, not an exhaustive review of all sound research, occult literature, complete books or paid products. Sources below establish provenance and what authors or vendors claim; they do not validate every claim. Original listening sketches are design proposals, not study-tested treatments. No paid recordings, voices or scripts were copied; no purchases were made.

## 1. Plato — Timaeus

- **Source:** [Benjamin Jowett translation, Internet Classics Archive](https://classics.mit.edu/Plato/timaeus.html).
- **Access:** Full translation accessible; the relevant world-soul and music passages were checked. The source identifies the dialogue as 360 BCE.
- **What it supports:** A historical philosophical connection between musical ratios, cosmology and harmony. Ratios including 3/2, 4/3 and 9/8 occur explicitly.
- **Limit:** A philosophical cosmology is not biological evidence. Ratios describe intervals, not a universally privileged absolute pitch or a treatment dose.
- **Original idea:** Play a fifth-and-octave drone while showing ratios. Compare interval preference against equal temperament without health scoring.

## 2. Joachim-Ernst Berendt — The World Is Sound: Nada Brahma

- **Source:** [Official publisher/distributor description](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-World-Is-Sound-Nada-Brahma/Joachim-Ernst-Berendt/9780892813186).
- **Access:** Publisher description and edition details read; full book not reviewed. Destiny Books edition dated May 1, 1991, 272 pages, ISBN 9780892813186.
- **What it supports:** A modern synthesis of musical cultures, listening and spirituality. This edition is not an ancient text, and should not stand in for the diverse traditions it discusses.
- **Limit:** Publisher copy contains sweeping scientific language that was not validated here. Spiritual interpretations do not establish physiological mechanisms.
- **Original idea:** An evolving harmonic drone for attention to timbre and a private listening journal; label any benefits as user-reported experience.

## 3. Jonathan Goldman — Healing Sounds: The Power of Harmonics

- **Source:** [Author’s 30th-anniversary book sales page](https://www.healingsounds.com/product/healing-sounds-book/).
- **Access:** Public description read; full book and associated paid audio not accessed.
- **What it supports:** The author markets vocal-harmonic practice, spiritual interpretation and downloadable examples. The author also sells related recordings and sound tools.
- **Limit:** Promises concerning immunity, chakras or transformation are commercial assertions. Testimonials and a bibliography are not substitutes for a controlled clinical study.
- **Original idea:** Synthesize a voice-like tone from harmonics and let listeners isolate partials. Do not copy the book’s exercises, branded audio or sacred-voice performances.

## 4. Hans Jenny — Cymatics

- **Source:** [MACROmedia Publishing / CymaticSource](https://www.cymaticsource.com/). [Catalog confirmation of the combined volumes](https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1971149384776286977).
- **Access:** Publisher listing verified in indexed results; direct page fetch timed out. Catalog confirms combined originals from 1967 and 1974. Full books not reviewed.
- **What it supports:** Books documenting visible vibration and wave phenomena, with later publisher commentary.
- **Limit:** A geometric pattern in a driven material does not demonstrate a therapeutic effect in tissue. A browser membrane model does not replicate Jenny’s complete experiments or all the physics of plates, powders or fluids.
- **Original idea:** Clearly labelled membrane-mode visualization paired with an original tone, allowing comparisons of mode numbers and boundaries. Keep physical-model assumptions visible.

## 5. Modern Solfeggio claims — provenance exhibit

- **Source:** [Tetrahedron publisher-hosted review and Leonard Horowitz reply](https://tetrahedron.org/healing-codes-for-the-biological-apocalypse/).
- **Access:** Public page read. The page is dated March 20, 2015; the embedded review carries a 1999 copyright. Full Healing Codes for the Biological Apocalypse book not reviewed.
- **What it supports:** A publisher-associated example of modern esoteric claims connecting numerology, biblical interpretation and musical tones. The author's reply describes learning the alleged codes in 1998 and acknowledges limitations in music and physics knowledge.
- **Limit:** This is evidence of claim provenance, not evidence of an ancient fixed-Hz system, DNA repair or disease treatment. The page contains broader unsupported allegations that should not be imported into OpenSync as facts.
- **Original idea:** Hidden-label comparisons of an original tone and nearby pitches at comfortable matched levels. Preference cannot validate biomedical or historical claims.

## 6. Hemi-Sync — Gateway Experience

- **Source:** [Official Gateway product page](https://hemi-sync.com/gateway-experience/). Historical assessment: [official GovInfo scan](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-D101-PURL-gpo152943/pdf/GOVPUB-D101-PURL-gpo152943.pdf).
- **Access:** Vendor page read; paid albums and manuals not accessed. CIA item/PDF requests redirected, but the official GovInfo copy was subsequently retrieved and all 29 available scan pages reviewed through OCR. Original page 25 is absent. See `sound-government-audit.md` for sections checked and interpretation limits.
- **What it supports:** The vendor currently describes eight progressive albums and claims expanded awareness, including metaphysical exercises.
- **Limit:** A vendor’s claims or presence in a government archive are not clinical or paranormal validation. Program labels such as Focus 10 are not EEG-frequency measurements. Do not imply OpenSync reproduces proprietary Hemi-Sync processing.
- **Original idea:** Generic stereo-beat demonstration with original attention prompts and gentle fades; avoid copied narrations, scripts and branded stage structures.

## 7. Brain.fm — modulation-based music

- **Sources:** [Vendor science page](https://www.brain.fm/science); [Woods et al., Communications Biology 7, 1376 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-024-07026-3); [2025 correction](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-025-08777-3); [2026 correction](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-026-10015-3).
- **Access:** Vendor page and indexed full paper read. Direct Nature page access was inconsistent. The current paper includes both correction links. The 2026 correction text was accessible in a publisher-provided author-paper mirror; the 2025 publisher correction was separately checked by the integrating reviewer and adds Bernardo Cordovez to the author list.
- **What it supports:** Controlled experiments investigated modulation and sustained attention. Reported benefits depended on conditions, including order of presentation and participant attention characteristics. This is narrower than a general promise of healing.
- **Financial interests:** Current disclosures identify Woods as an employee with unexercised equity options and Hewett as a former employee who held equity at the time. The April 13, 2026 correction adds these disclosures to replace an earlier declaration of no competing interests. It is not a retraction.
- **Original idea:** Compare the same original ambient composition with and without mild amplitude modulation. Do not copy commercial tracks or call the result Brain.fm technology.

## 8. Endel — adaptive soundscapes

- **Sources:** [Vendor science page](https://endel.io/science); [Haruvi et al., Frontiers in Computational Neuroscience (2022), DOI 10.3389/fncom.2021.760561](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computational-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fncom.2021.760561/full).
- **Access:** Public vendor page and full study checked; paid app not tested.
- **What it supports:** The study compared soundscapes, playlists and silence with an EEG-derived focus measure. It recruited 62 participants; reported sound comparisons included 51. Effects varied by task and subgroup; individual preferred/best conditions differed.
- **Financial interests:** Authors were employed by Arctop. Arctop and Endel funded the study; Endel participated in study design and provided audio. Authors state Endel did not collect or analyze the data or write the paper. This is company-associated research, not independent clinical validation.
- **Limit:** Do not translate a modeled focus measure into a general sevenfold productivity promise, a diagnosis or a clinical treatment claim.
- **Original idea:** Create a slowly evolving noise-and-tone soundscape and compare it with a static version. User settings control texture; no claim to recreate Endel’s algorithm.

## Integration rules

- Keep `claim`, `limits`, `access` and `idea` together in each exhibit; avoid stripping caveats when shortening UI text.
- Place historical, spiritual and product exhibits alongside evidence summaries with distinct labels.
- A mathematical/audio simulation predicts its specified signal or mechanical model. It does not simulate healing, brain state or disease outcomes.
- New listening presets are original experiments in sound design. Product and book associations are context, not endorsement, equivalence or demonstrated efficacy.
- Full-book coverage, exhaustive current research coverage and testing of paid products remain uncompleted; never label this survey as all SOTA or a complete reading of the literature.
