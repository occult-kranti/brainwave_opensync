# Energy Observatory
A multi-page Fieldwork research workspace for electromagnetic conversion, acoustic harvesting, quantum stored work and complete energy accounting. Audience: curious experimenters and technically capable reviewers. Every calculator is a stated mathematical model; no hardware has been measured. Six specialized AI agents debate three sequential rounds under one coordinating advisor. Current primary literature, program documents and patents have explicit reading limits. Earlier apps remain inspectable, usable and versioned. Deployment: /brainwave_opensync/devices/energy/. No autoplay or instrument control.
