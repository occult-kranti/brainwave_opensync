# Physical assumptions — stage 0

Role: independent physical-assumptions reviewer. Scope: E1 only. Read directly: `src/quantum.mjs`, `research/math-crosswalk/panel/energy/thermal-channel.mjs`, and E1/E2 fields in `research/roadmap-update-2026-10-22/panel/energy/plan2.json`; read paired-physics and Tesla mechanism/resonance skills. No broader research or shared implementation edits.

1. **State domain.** Use the ground/excited basis and fixed nondegenerate Hamiltonian H = diag(0, ε), ε > 0. The normalized Hermitian state is ρ = [[1−p,c],[c*,p]], with p in [0,1] and |c|² ≤ p(1−p). These conditions are necessary and sufficient for positivity. Thus p = 0 or 1 forces c = 0. `coherence` in both implementations means κ = |c|/sqrt[p(1−p)] at interior p, not c itself. At endpoints different κ inputs describe the same diagonal state.

2. **Operations defining work.** E1 uses ergotropy: maximal decrease of Tr(Hρ) over all single-qubit unitaries, with a cyclic control Hamiltonian returning to H at the endpoints. The mathematical optimum assumes arbitrary coherent control and a suitable phase reference. It does not give achieved work for restricted controls. Controls commuting with H alone cannot change the energy. No many-copy extraction or bath-assisted operation class is included.

3. **Phase.** c may have any complex phase; the spectrum, mean energy and unrestricted ergotropy depend only on |c|. A phase can be removed by a diagonal unitary commuting with H, so `qubitWork`'s zero-phase representative covers these scalar quantities. It does not show that an unknown phase is operationally irrelevant to a fixed experimental pulse. `thermalQubitWork` retains phase explicitly.

4. **Channel and physical range.** The scoped channel D_d keeps populations fixed and maps c to dc for real d in [0,1]. It is CPTP because D_d(ρ) = [(1+d)/2]ρ + [(1−d)/2]ZρZ. The larger family of real coherence multipliers is physical for d in [−1,1]; negative d includes a phase flip and is outside this retained-fraction contract. A complex multiplier can also be CPTP when its modulus is at most one. Neither extension is needed for E1. d is a channel parameter, not a rate or elapsed time.

5. **Energy ledger.** With this fixed energy basis, Tr[H D_d(ρ)] = εp. W(1)−W(d) equals the increase in passive energy, not an additional energy released as heat. Damping changes population and must be excluded from this comparison: set γ = 0, or regard p,c as the already-damped input and hold them fixed while varying d. The existing zero-temperature ledger reports γ εp leaving the qubit; the thermal ledger reports ε γ(ν−p) entering it. Neither term is a dephasing work loss.

6. **Supported conclusion and apparatus boundary.** If proved, E1 bounds the decrease in ideal unitary work accessibility of a supplied state. Its zero-loss and saturation conditions must include c = 0, d = 1, p = 1/2, and the zero-Bloch-length endpoint without dividing by zero. It establishes no preparation cost, dephasing implementation cost, extraction efficiency, controller cycle, reservoir reset, output power, or source-free apparatus. Those costs remain unmodeled rather than zero. E2 remains parked.
