# Frozen independent claim-equivalence gate

Research date: 21 October 2026. Panel role: independent skeptic, Round 1 of 2. This gate was written before reading the other five specialists' new panel reports. Previous project records and primary mathematical source documents may be read. Round 2 is not authorized until the advisor selects it from Round 1 findings.

## Unit of comparison

Compare a specific theorem, counterexample, algorithmic guarantee or experimentally tested claim with a specific prior project result. A result family, manuscript title, keyword, proof-file name or topic-network edge is not a proof of equivalence.

For each side record: repository revision; exact file/section/theorem; mathematical objects and state space; dimensions, domain and boundary; hypotheses and quantifiers; gauge/coordinate equivalence; observations or supplied data; regulator, volume and physical energy scale; topology and order of limits; conclusion; verification scope and unresolved dependencies.

## Admission classes

- **Exact solved:** the prior project proves or certifies the same proposition under assumptions no stronger than the target. An explicit mathematical reduction must map objects, input data and conclusion. A numerical example or tests cannot certify an infinite quantified claim. Priority is a separate dated-source inquiry.
- **Same attempted target:** prior dated artifacts explicitly formulate the same target and show substantive attempts, including incomplete or failed ones. A shared parent aspiration does not establish the same subproblem.
- **Restricted-model overlap:** a concrete related theorem or calculation exists, but its finite domain, simpler operator, stronger assumptions, observation model or limit differs. Record the missing bridge, not a fractional solve percentage.
- **Useful method transfer:** a verified or source-scoped mathematical/computational method can be applied after listing input obligations and acceptance tests. This does not mean we solved its originating problem.
- **Thematic only:** shared vocabulary or domain without a demonstrated mathematical reduction or concrete executable use.
- **Insufficient evidence / not assessed:** missing statement, source, provenance or comparison. Do not coerce this into either overlap or no-overlap.

A formalization label is admitted only for the exact encoded statement and its assumptions, not all manuscripts in a family. Read scope documentation, declarations and trust/dependency notes. Distinguish publisher-reported Lean status, source inspection and an actual build/axiom audit performed here. A successful kernel check can still concern an easier statement or a theorem conditional on a new axiom.

## Adversarial acceptance tests for a possible second round

1. Replace a finite resonance list with a complete static Dirichlet-to-Neumann map: the classifier must reject exact-equivalence unless the full data reduction is supplied.
2. Replace a positive finite-volume gap with a volume-uniform gap and continuum quantum field construction: the classifier must reject the jump. Include an explicit family with positive finite gaps tending to zero as a negative control.
3. Replace qubit ergotropy or a reciprocal circuit energy identity with positive full-cycle apparatus surplus: reject the transfer; include preparation/driving/reset/storage resources.
4. Attach a family's Lean badge from a companion nonuniqueness result to a uniqueness headline: reject exact formalization unless the declarations match.
5. Turn a topic graph connection into a statement of solved overlap: reject unless both claims and reduction evidence are attached.
6. Preserve a correct narrow identity or valid symmetry under a coordinate transformation as a positive control. The gate must not reject everything merely because it is cautious.
