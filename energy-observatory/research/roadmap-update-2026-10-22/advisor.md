## The mathematical questions

**E1:** with fixed gap ε, population p, coherence c and energy-basis dephasing d, prove

\[
0\le W(1)-W(d)\le \varepsilon|c|(1-d).
\]

Treat p=1/2,d=0 without dividing by a zero Bloch-vector length. This is a known-theory application with new project assurance, not a claim of mathematical novelty. A grid cannot replace its all-parameter proof.

**M1:** for the actual constant-coefficient two-coil/mechanical model, check positive storage Q and exact identities

\[
QA+A^TQ=-2L_{\rm loss}D_{\rm loss}L_{\rm loss}^T,
\qquad QB=C^T.
\]

Preserve both voltage and force ports. The browser comparison is only F=0, at three frozen frequencies, with RMS units and all parameters supplied explicitly. A consistent signed coordinate/port change must pass; one reversed reciprocal sign must fail. Arbitrary time-dependent driving u(t) is allowed. Time-varying model coefficients require rederived dynamics and the extra storage/control terms, including ½zᵀQ̇z; they are outside this certificate. The final review corrected an earlier sentence that accidentally excluded time-dependent driving.

**E2:** restrict initial preparation to a unitary orbit of the positive-temperature Gibbs state, use d=1, retain the joint two-qubit state, and reset it by a 16-dimensional SWAP with a fresh four-state Gibbs reference. The output reference keeps the full used state, including correlations. Target the ideal accounting identity

\[
W_{\rm cycle,on}=D(\omega_X\Vert\tau_X)/\beta\ge0.
\]

Prove the underlying entropy/work identity under its explicit initial-product, unitary and endpoint-decoupled assumptions. Reset bounds, simulated work and measured control cost are different fields. Device-state closure does not close reservoir regeneration or a real controller. The correlated diagonal-vs-product control must fail a marginal-energy-only reset test.

**Y1:** in the unchanged fixed-spacing patterned SU(2) model, seek an explicit C giving a correlation error ≤C N^(−1/4)‖A‖² for every integer N≥5 on the declared compact time window. Freeze r=2 below N=16 and floor(N^(1/4)) thereafter; certify N=5…255 and prove an analytic tail for N≥256. The growing volume exponential cannot itself remain bounded. Its product with boundary decay is the actual object to control. The old half-box schedule is a negative control for an unrestricted rate, while its valid finite range remains preserved. No continuum conclusion follows.

**A1:** fit a finite gain-aware sphere/receptance model at 30, 36, 54 and 63 kHz; test held-out complex responses at 33 and 59 kHz. Include calibrated mass/geometry, a separate pole estimate, shared clock error and phase uncertainty. The specified gain-slope and delay stresses can challenge this finite model; arbitrary functions agreeing at all sampled frequencies remain indistinguishable. Proposed calibration uncertainties are synthetic assumptions, not specifications of an instrument we possess. Predeclare rank/conditioning, coverage and abstention criteria before any run. The final likelihood and covariance review remain necessary.
