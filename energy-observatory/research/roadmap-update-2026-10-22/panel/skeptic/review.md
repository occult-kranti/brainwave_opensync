# Skeptic roadmap review

The roadmap should admit **bounded attempts**, not promise that a problem is “solvable now.” The frozen gate separates mathematical proof/counterexample work, engineering verification, measured experimental hypotheses and grand open targets. A target needs exact premises, noncircular imported results, a distinct objective, honest novelty, a decisive wrong control, a resource cap and a stop rule.

The prior panel supports useful finite-model identities and measurement constraints. Repeating its gap, scale-symmetry or heat controls is regression work, not new knowledge. A proof-checker run would improve assurance but would remain an engineering/formalization task unless it establishes a genuinely new statement. No finite numerical success fills the missing continuum Yang–Mills bridge. No single-channel energy closure establishes a reusable physical energy cycle.

Two concise candidates are proposed in `candidates.json`:

1. **SK1 — exact measurement sufficiency in a calibrated 2×2 model.** Determine what two frequencies plus diagonal/cross static compliance identify. The key adversarial pair has identical frequencies and diagonal compliance but opposite cross response. Fix signed coordinate conventions; otherwise the sign difference may be a gauge convention. This is bounded, known linear algebra applied to measurement design, not a general inverse theorem. Estimated budget: 4–8 agent-hours.
2. **SK2 — global cycle admission.** Make the ledger reject a “reset” that restores subsystem populations while consuming correlations or omitting controller/reset resources. The control compares a correlated diagonal two-qubit state to the product of its marginals. Equal local energy does not imply equal global free energy. This is an accounting/verification task, with no automatic claim that all correlation free energy is extractable. Estimated budget: 6–12 agent-hours; park if no complete operational cycle can first be specified.

A copper-location effect remains an **experimental hypothesis** requiring calibration, nuisance controls and physical measurements. The continuum Yang–Mills goal remains a **grand open target**, with any near-term bridge separately specified. Neither should be promoted to a bounded solve merely because simulation code can be written.
