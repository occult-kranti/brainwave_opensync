## Objection → response → decision

**Objection:** A statistically significant lag alignment between a Schumann plot and a purported contact event might be called a link to ET origin. An arbitrary upload date could silently stand in for the session time, and all cyclic offsets could be treated as physically exchangeable.

**Response:** The supplied vectors have no timestamps or physical data. The exact p-value is conditional on a specified synthetic randomization mechanism. It does not validate real-world exchangeability, event provenance, missing data, nonstationarity or an origin model. Existing `channels-ET` and `bashar-index` records explicitly distinguish uploads, reposts, catalogue dates and original sessions. Those distinctions must survive a future cross-link.

**Decision:** Accept the finite-group demonstration as known-method validation only. A future association, even if accurately timed and replicated, would still be compatible with shared solar/weather/calendar drivers, ordinary sensory influences, information leakage and other specified mechanisms. Reject an `establishes ET origin` edge. A source-qualified `tests temporal association` edge is appropriate only after actual measured records and their analysis are supplied.

## Conditions for an empirical timestamp join

- Record original event/session time separately from recording, publication, upload, forecast, retrieval and later interpretation times, each with timezone and precision.
- Fix the event definition and complete eligible event list before viewing the environmental outcome; retain non-events and misses. An ambiguous prediction later fitted to an occurrence is not a timestamped successful prediction.
- Select frequency/component/site, lag family, window, exclusions and missingness rules prospectively. Do not choose one favorable station, intensity scale, lag or interpretation after inspection.
- Require a comparison scheme that preserves relevant calendar, day/night, sampling and nuisance structure; arbitrary cyclic shifts alone do not ensure it.

No prospective participant event series or new human experiment exists in this round. The official Bashar handout and authorized transcript frequency assertions identified in R1 remain documentary assertions; neither is validated by these correlation calculations.

Next recommendation: challenge a shared-calendar/common-driver alternative before promoting a timing association. Then consider independently hidden information targets as a separate endpoint. Novelty remains protocol/benchmark engineering, not a new contact result.
