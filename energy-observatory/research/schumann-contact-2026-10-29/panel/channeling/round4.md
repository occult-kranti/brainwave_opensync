## Objection → response → decision

**Objection:** Resolving a statistically significant timing relationship might be presented as resolving which agent or mechanism caused it.

**Response:** The model pair is an explicit example of observational equivalence. For channeling, the analogous ordinary alternatives include a shared public narrative, interviewer prompts, repeated source material, event-selection rules and information leakage. This is not an accusation that any particular participant used such routes; it states what a design must distinguish. The high within-channeler correspondence in the 2026 prior-art study is especially incompatible with treating every claimed entity label as an independent externally authenticated source.

**Decision:** Accept improved synthetic resolution and the rank/identity counterexample. Retain separate graph edges for an attributed identity, a semantic resemblance, a statistical association and evidence discriminating an origin. None of the first three substitutes for the fourth. R1's corrected official numerical frequency attributions remain documentary claims.

## Proposed next endpoint: hidden information, before source attribution

Use a fixed K-category target universe and fixed N trials. For each trial, an independently administered randomization selects an equiprobable target inaccessible to participant, facilitator and scorer until the response is locked. Predefine a single objective response and hit rule, missing/abstention treatment and complete trial sequence; do not choose the best session, target set, outcome or participant afterward. Preregister the planned analysis before actual collection. Record actual lock/reveal timestamps and audit custody rather than assigning them after the fact.

Under the null, target assignment must remain uniform **conditional on the response and available history**. Marginally balanced target counts alone are insufficient: leakage could preserve target balance while conveying its value. For independent fixed-N trials with this no-information condition, the hit count has the declared binomial benchmark. Alternative randomization schemes, dependence or adaptively stopped collection require their own analysis.

A decisive synthetic control is an ordinary leakage channel engineered to yield the same hit distribution as a proposed anomalous channel. Equal scores then demonstrate why the information test alone cannot identify ET agency. A future positive result would first justify an audited replication of the specified information effect. Distinguishing its source requires additional independently testable predictions; no source probability is manufactured from an unusually small p-value.
