## Proposed next discriminating test

Freeze an engineering benchmark before production, labelled established-method stress test. Use a known ordinary shared driver to generate two synthetic correlated channels, then add a prespecified independent perturbation to one channel. Test the locked statistic against blocked/time-matched nulls and untouched scenarios; retain a naive shuffled-time control to expose autocorrelation or lag-selection bias. Log all failed cases. Acceptance concerns false alarms and recovery under the declared generator, not the reality or impossibility of contact.

For physical intake, require synchronously timestamped calibrated Bx/By (T orT²/Hz), optionalEz(V/m), transfer functions, sample rate, clock uncertainty, station noise/maintenance flags, a locked modal estimator and quiet/control intervals. Contact reports need independent prior timestamps, fixed coding, all sessions including non-events, concealed outcomes and an ordinary information-leak control. Astronomy can supply UTC/solar-geometry nuisance coordinates; an astrological label is a separate interpretation until a locked out-of-sample prediction improves an assumptions-matched baseline.

A simple dimensioned acquisition check is Faraday induction for a uniform sinusoidal field: Vpeak=N A(2πf)Bpeak. For declared illustrative N=1000,A=1m²,f=7.83Hz,Bpeak=1pT, Vpeak=4.9197340955×10⁻⁸V. A Python arithmetic check ran. This is49.2nV ideal open-circuit voltage, not measured sensor sensitivity, usable power, brain stimulation or a detection guarantee. Real core effective area, noise, load and calibration remain separate. Do not start another round merely to re-derive this standard law.
