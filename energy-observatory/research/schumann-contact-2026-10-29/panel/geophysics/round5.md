## Scope review

The binomial model needs independent uniform targets conditional on each response and prior history, plus fixed trials and objective scoring. A balanced label histogram or a report's confidence does not establish those assumptions. Its p-value is a tail probability under that model, not the probability that an extraterrestrial source exists. Correlated sessions, target reuse, adaptive selection and omitted failed attempts need a different design or must fail intake.

The ordinary-leak and exotic-label models with identical independent hit probability produce the same count distribution. This is an attribution limit, not an allegation that a particular channeler leaked information. A hypothetical elevated hit rate would first justify scrutinizing custody, leakage and scoring, followed by independent replication; it would not become a measured electromagnetic pathway.

The two compromised-trial bounds must remain distinct: fixed/preselected compromised trials permit a remaining-trial binomial model, whereas outcome-dependent corruption requires the more conservative underlying-N-trial bound. The assumed numberm is a sensitivity input, not estimated by the correctness count or certified by a clean magnetic record.

## Recommended Round6 admission fields

Keep documentary, electromagnetic, information and cross-link statuses separate rather than combining them into one confidence badge.

1. **Electromagnetic record:** source/station ID, actual raw or processed numeric record locator and hash, event UTC interval and clock uncertainty, physical component, SI units, sample rate/filters, measured complex transfer/calibration with validity interval, QC/missingness masks, instrument changes, estimator and uncertainty procedure, baseline and unchanged held-out intervals. An image, Kp value or hardcoded7.83Hz does not substitute for a calibrated Schumann record.
2. **Information record:** fixed protocol and target-generation design, independently maintained custody, response lock before reveal, objective rubric, every attempted trial including exclusions with reasons, scoring output and relevant uncertainty, and explicit leakage/compromise assumptions. A video catalog or upload date is not an attempt-level dataset or event timestamp.
3. **Cross-link contract:** pair keys, overlapping UTC support, prespecified endpoint and direction/lag family, temporal-null justification, missingness treatment, ordinary solar/local-time/weather/instrument/reporting controls and an untouched evaluation set. Geographic differences and station independence must be documented rather than inferred from different names.

Hard rejects include absent numeric SR records; non-finite measurements or units absent; calibration outside validity; mismatched/unknown event times; unrecorded exclusions; no attempt denominator; after-the-fact selected events or lags; and claims that Kp/image brightness establishes the required physical endpoint. An otherwise complete synthetic example can be `ready-for-analysis` only. It remains synthetic and cannot be promoted to an observed anomaly, validated channel or contact.
