## Three historical passages and their proper edges

1. **Gateway assessment, 9 June 1983, section 9:** the author attributes to Bentov a body/ionosphere resonance story and a roughly 40,000 km telepathic carrier. The official GovInfo scan was retrieved (29 pages, SHA-256 in `sources.json`) and selected pages visually read. This is `documents` an historical claim. It does not `supports` a measured body-to-Earth transfer function. The quotation is PDF page 8 / printed page 6, section 9; the current repo index cites section 8 / scan page 9 incorrectly.
2. **Corpus Hermeticum I.1–16:** a revelatory conversation and cosmological narrative support a `methodological-analogy` or cultural-comparison edge to modern channeling discourse. Divine Mind and planetary rulers are not thereby identified as modern extraterrestrials. Read in a Mead translation/electronic witness, not collated Greek manuscripts.
3. **Corpus Hermeticum XI.18–20:** contemplation beyond ordinary bodily/time boundaries supplies a historical motif. It supplies neither an electromagnetic unit nor a measurable signal, so it cannot be a physical edge to Schumann modes. The modern hermetic-timeline exercise is a reconstruction of this motif, not an independently measured result.

These summaries distinguish the existence of a claim from its physical truth. No assertion that all occult texts have been read is warranted.

## Corrections that must survive integration

- Gateway's archived dossier calls 7.83 Hz “exact geophysics.” It is a selected familiar approximate first-mode value; measured apparent peaks depend on environmental, source and receiver conditions. Exact carrier subtraction in a synthetic audio preset does not make the terrestrial resonance exact.
- Its website and dossier reject sound effects using photon energy and a universal tissue Q≈1. Sound pressure and power must be analyzed mechanically; a single-quantum energy comparison cannot settle a classical driven-response question, and a universal tissue-Q value is unsupported. Removing this invalid rebuttal does not establish the proposed effect.
- Binaural audio difference, body displacement, EEG alpha activity, electric field and magnetic flux density are different channels, even when their numerical frequency ranges overlap.
- Army authorship/government archival custody establishes provenance. It is not an experimental validation or proof of ET involvement.
- Astrology workbench's astronomical coordinates can be useful timing covariates. Dignities, correspondences and symbol choices remain tradition-specific interpretive variables unless separately tested. They cannot be used to select successful events after looking at outcomes.

## Closest scientific literature and novelty gate

The selected primary literature already includes protocol-specific binaural null results (López-Caballero–Escera 2017), human EEG responses to rotated Earth-strength fields (Wang et al. 2019; limited read depth), a current holographic/Schumann theoretical proposal (Cavaglià–Tuszynski, online December 2025 / January 2026 issue, no data used), and Lacy's 2026 neural frequency-lattice analysis. Lacy explicitly says frequency correspondence does not demonstrate neural-geophysical electromagnetic coupling. These are different experimental/model questions; none establishes a contact sender.

The 2026 papers should enter the source graph with their actual method/read depth and should be audited before anyone calls neural/Schumann frequency correspondences new. The exact published methods and data still need independent replay. Search coverage is bounded; no exhaustive SOTA or novelty claim is made.

## Next discriminating question

Freeze one bridge before simulation: **Can an analysis distinguish a preselected, independent physical association from shared timing and post-hoc feature/lag selection?** Use synthetic null and injected-effect controls with fixed station/UTC windows, held-out evaluation, clock uncertainty, calibrated channel units, and predeclared features. Astrology may contribute a timestamped cultural label, but may not determine a new lag/window after outcomes are seen. Report false-positive behavior, not a simulated ET discovery.

For an actual contact claim, add separately a prospective concealed-target task, auditable randomization, blind scoring, leakage controls and independent replication. A positive statistical result would still leave source identity unresolved. No external contacts or human experiment were conducted in this round.

**Outstanding missing premise:** a calibrated, time-aligned observation packet containing both the physical signal and independently recorded contact claim, with a predeclared discriminator and plausible ordinary alternatives. Without it, no current Schumann-contact anomaly can be adjudicated.
