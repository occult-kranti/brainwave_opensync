# Round 2 — the proposed physical channel and historical assertions

Incoming objection: R1 found that the Gateway source jumps from a body-oscillation narrative to an Earth-scale telepathic carrier without a calibrated transfer map. The advisor narrowed R2 to known-law magnetic acquisition. This review assesses that contract; producer/verifier execution was not yet available when this source review was written.

## Source comparison

The inspected official Gateway scan, section 9 / PDF8 / printed6, attributes to Bentov a roughly 40,000 km wave and approximately one-seventh-second travel around Earth. It further asserts negligible obstacles/attenuation and suitability for telepathic transmission. The wavelength/travel-time numbers alone cannot establish those propagation, reception or information-content claims. A large wavelength does not specify field strength, loss, channel capacity, source work or a receiving transducer. Nor does agreement with an approximate terrestrial-cavity scale identify a biological source.

The section also switches between bodily sound/vibration, electromagnetic pulse and Earth's electrostatic field. Those descriptions require separate observation and transfer maps. A wave cannot simultaneously be treated as an unspecified propagating signal and established merely by referring to a static field. The original passages were read as historical assertions; no experiment in these selected passages supplies the needed calibration.

Astrology-sim-ant's calculated sky positions and tradition-specific correspondence labels supply time/geometry metadata, not coil turns, flux, coupling coefficients or sender identity. A planetary label cannot substitute for a measured B(t). A daily/seasonal association may instead encode lightning geography, local time or sampling choices.

## Independent dimensional check

For uniform axial flux through N stationary turns, flux linkage is N A B cos(theta), in webers; Faraday differentiation gives volts. A sinusoidal RMS amplitude therefore gives V_rms = 2πf N A B_rms |cos(theta)|. For a circular integration path in uniform axial time-varying B, 2πr E = πr² |dB/dt|, hence E_rms = π f r B_rms. These are **declared geometric idealizations**, not measurements of a brain, person or cavity.

At f=7.83 Hz and B=1 pT RMS, the requested N=1000, A=1 m² coil produces approximately 4.92×10⁻⁸ V RMS in this ideal open-circuit model. At r=0.1 m, the circular-path field is approximately 2.46×10⁻¹² V/m RMS. This hand calculation is an independent model check, not a physical sensitivity claim. Zero magnetic coupling or DC produces zero induced sinusoidal voltage under the stated stationary geometry; other magnetic sensing mechanisms are outside this result.

The optional resistor-noise figure compares one modeled noise source with one modeled receiver response. It neither proves a built instrument can detect the signal nor excludes every biological transducer. The approximately 35 µT / 1 pT amplitude ratio is 35 million, but a rotated geomagnetic field and an ELF oscillatory field are different temporal stimuli. That ratio alone is not a biological dose-equivalence calculation.

## Decision and next discriminator

**Accept the contract as a bounded engineering replication, subject to the independent numerical checks. Reject an upgrade to a body–Earth or ET mechanism.** Keep source amplitude, receiver sensitivity, nuisance fields and sender attribution as separate missing premises.

Next question: even if two channels can be measured, can a preselected association survive common time-of-day structure, colored noise, choice of lag and independently held-out evaluation? The astronomy tool may define a frozen covariate, but cannot be used to tune the selected lag after inspecting outcomes. A positive association would still require a separate source-identity test.
