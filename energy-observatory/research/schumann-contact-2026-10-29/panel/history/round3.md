## What astronomy contributes

The inspected astrology-sim-ant implementation distinguishes computed sky positions/time conventions from tradition-specific interpretation. The usable research import is a **frozen observation description**: UTC timestamp, site coordinates, coordinate frame, time-scale conversion, ephemeris revision, and uncertainty. Local apparent solar time is not interchangeable with UTC or an arbitrary civil-clock label. The same UTC can imply different local illumination and lightning geography at different stations.

Planetary hours, mansion bins and dignity scores are deterministic symbolic transformations under specified conventions. Their repeatability establishes computational consistency, not predictive validity, biological reception or ET source identity. Western and sidereal labels derived from the same moment are correlated encodings, not independent replications. A season or lunar-phase association can be investigated as a predeclared predictor, but its symbolic interpretation is an additional hypothesis.

## Objection to a naive randomization interpretation

The locked maximum over lags and full reoptimization within each shift correctly match the statistic to its selection rule under the contract's **uniform relative-offset null**. That null needs a separate justification before use on real records. Actual journal entries can be selected by meditation schedules, daytime availability, public anomaly alerts or regional observatory schedules. Shifting them against physical records may destroy those shared structures and yield a small p-value without any direct physical communication.

A source label saying a session occurred in a particular planetary hour cannot supply random assignment. Nor can switching zodiac/calendar systems after seeing outcomes be treated as a single predeclared test. All tried label systems, band definitions, preprocessing choices and lag windows belong to the selection family; unlogged choices invalidate the restricted interpretation of the reported p-value.

The all-lag degeneracy control is particularly useful: if every cyclic lag is allowed, the maximum is shift-invariant. An exact p of one then means the test has no ability to discriminate relative offset under this group. It does not mean the two signals have no shared structure or that a physical link is impossible.

## Decision

**Accept R3 as an established-statistics synthetic benchmark, subject to worker checks.** The incoming ordinary explanation is addressed only for the declared relative-offset experiment. No present-day Schumann anomaly or channeling association is tested. Preserve the term “exact” for finite enumeration, not for the real-world null assumptions.

## Next precise counterexample

Use a declared common calendar-like driver d(t) with two outputs x(t)=a d(t)+u(t) and y(t)=b d(t)+v(t), where u and v are independent or explicitly orthogonal residuals under the chosen finite design. Freeze all coefficients, sample support and a matched no-direct-link model before seeing results. Compare raw association with residual association after accounting for the known d(t). A shared daily component can make the raw signals agree even though the construction contains no x→y coupling.

Keep the exact claim narrow: attenuation after a known-driver adjustment demonstrates this synthetic confound, not proof that the same driver explains any actual experience. Conversely, residual association does not establish ET origin; omitted drivers, inaccurate clocks and selection remain. The decisive empirical packet still requires independently timestamped sessions and calibrated physical records, including all null sessions and their selection rules.
