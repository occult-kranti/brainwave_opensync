## What retrospective correspondences lack

An archived text can establish that an author expressed a belief. A prediction claim additionally needs the exact assertion and its original publication/custody date, the target event definition, a deadline, a scoring rule and a complete denominator of attempts. A later upload timestamp is not necessarily the time the prediction was made. A transcript edited after an event cannot by itself establish prior specificity.

Astrological correspondences and broad channeling statements commonly permit several readings. For a test, freeze the permitted interpretation before unblinding: which planet/chart convention, what outcome, which numerical range and what time window? Do not pick the tradition, metaphor, date conversion or lag that fits best after the event. Retain incorrect, ambiguous and unscorable statements under predeclared handling rules. Reposts, translations and copies of one statement do not count as independent predictions.

The current repositories supply cultural records and computational conventions; this review has not located a complete prospective hidden-target experiment packet within its selected files. That bounded absence is not evidence no such experiment exists anywhere.

## Probability scope and source identity

The contract's binomial model requires conditional unpredictability of each target, given the response and available history. Four target types appearing equally often does not ensure that property: a repeating sequence could be perfectly predictable while remaining marginally balanced. A fixed trial count and objective answer key prevent some selection problems, but do not establish concealed custody.

The independent perfect-leak mixture gives q=0.2+0.8/4=0.4. A hypothetical exotic source producing independent hits with q=0.4 has the same hit-count likelihood. Thus a score can test the specified no-information distribution while leaving the origin of the information unresolved. This is a model equivalence, not an allegation about any named channeler.

The two corruption analyses properly distinguish a preselected compromised subset from outcome-dependent alteration. Their m is a declared sensitivity parameter, not an observed or verified amount of leakage. Using the more favorable fixed-subset calculation for a post-hoc selected set would overstate evidence.

## Minimum prospective record for Round 6

Each prospective test packet should contain:

- Claim ID and exact predicted observable; source witness/version and original assertion time, with unresolved timestamp provenance recorded explicitly.
- Frozen protocol, target alphabet, conditional-randomization model, fixed N, scoring rule, exclusions/abstentions and stopping rule, with a pre-observation commitment and custody trail.
- Per-trial response and independently recorded response-lock/reveal times; complete target sequence and answer key after the study; all scheduled trials, including nulls and failures.
- Randomization provenance and an auditable account of who could access targets, responses and identifiers, including known leakage opportunities. A seed field alone does not prove unpredictability or independent custody.
- Outcome-blind scoring records and analysis-version/hash; an explicit family of all endpoints/lag/calendar variants tested; untouched replication/evaluation cases.
- If an environmental association is also claimed: station identity, UTC mapping and clock uncertainty, calibrated channel units, sampling/coverage/QC, receiver transfer, baseline, common-driver covariates and uncertainty method.
- Separate conclusions for information score, physical association and sender identity. No automatic ET-origin field may be set from a small p-value.

## Decision

**Accept the restricted scoring and sensitivity contract, subject to independent execution.** It operationalizes a question missing from historical analogy while preserving leakage and source ambiguity. Round 6 should implement a fail-closed *metadata completeness* check with explicit unresolved fields and adversarial fixtures. Passing that check must mean “eligible for human/source review,” not “authentic data,” “valid experiment,” “coupling proved,” or “contact confirmed.”
