## Shared-driver audit

The observational design is 5×2: centered columns X and z are equal, rank1, nullity1, with kernel witness (1,−1). Both Y=2X and Y=2z yield the same five observations. These models assert different effects of changing X while holding z fixed, yet this observational packet cannot distinguish them. Duplicating the measured column gives a 5×3 design of rank1 and nullity2; sensor count has increased, identifying information has not.

For the **stipulated independent perturbation** u, X′=z+u, the centered unscaled Gram matrix is [[64/5,10],[10,10]], determinant28. The producer uses centered columns multiplied by5, so its determinant is 28×5⁴=17,500; these representations agree. Rank becomes2 and nullity0 in this two-column model. Predicted outcomes differ by2u=[2,0,−2,0,2].

This verifies that an independent perturbation would discriminate these two declared linear models. It does not show that such a perturbation of a human or planetary field has been performed or can be isolated. Nor does rank2 identify an extraterrestrial source: uniqueness of coefficients inside a specified model is different from completeness of the physical model and attribution of an agent.

## Implications for cross-links and the next round

The accepted cross-link is methodological: synchronized, independently calibrated channels and interventions can help separate physical explanations under explicit assumptions. A calendar/astrological feature may be a numerical covariate; its agreement with a field series does not add a new causal mechanism. Equal Hz values and copied sensors remain insufficient.

For the next round, test a **blinded information endpoint and evidence-admission contract**, rather than imply the statistics now establishes contact. A bounded four-choice target protocol could specify independent target assignment, blinded response, scoring locked before reveal, record integrity, unsuccessful trials retained, and a fixed stopping rule. A score above chance would test the particular information-isolation/null assumptions. It would not by itself identify ET agency. Synthetic fixtures can verify arithmetic and block invalid data; an actual experiment would still require independently auditable target custody and ordinary-leakage controls.

Do not implement an agency classifier or automatically turn a small p-value into a graph edge labelled `supports ET contact`. A claim status can advance from proposal to numerical test to observed association only with the corresponding evidence, and each edge needs its own source, units and discriminator. Round 1's unavailable synchronized field/contact packet remains unavailable.
