# Prospective Schumann/contact cross-link protocol

Prepared 29 October 2026. **Design only:** no human sessions, contact attempts or new station measurements have been run. This document is not a claim of preregistration in an external registry. The synthetic calculations in the round directories test methods under declared assumptions.

## Four separate questions

1. **Documentary:** What did a specified source actually assert, and how faithfully is it attributed?
2. **Environmental:** Did a specified calibrated electromagnetic observable differ from its matched baseline?
3. **Information:** Did locked responses identify independently concealed targets more accurately than the predeclared chance model permits?
4. **Cross-link and origin:** Is information performance associated with an independently measured environmental quantity after the declared controls? What additional observation discriminates a proposed sender from ordinary alternatives?

Success on an earlier question does not answer a later one. An unexplained result is not an identified extraterrestrial source. No probability of ET origin is calculated by this project.

## Environmental acquisition

Record the station, location, component and units. Use calibrated numeric Bx/By in tesla or an explicitly separate Ez channel in V/m, with raw reference files, complex response, effective noise bandwidth, receiver noise, anti-aliasing and saturation/interference records. Freeze the sampling rate, analysis band, windowing, estimator and missing-data rule. A tallest PSD bin is not automatically a cavity pole; amplitude and frequency are different observables.

Synchronize UTC clocks and quantify timestamp uncertainty. Define baseline eligibility before viewing the target interval; retain quiet intervals, dropouts, local interference and all exclusion decisions. For a global claim, require independent appropriately distributed stations and a justified shared observable; the local packet validator does not certify global coverage. Lightning/source geography, ionospheric conditions, local mains, receiver response and estimation choices remain ordinary alternatives.

The illustrative coil calculation is an acquisition equation, not a hardware design. Its N, area and resistance are stipulated independently. Real inductance, loading, effective area, amplifier and environmental noise require engineering characterization. No body or health inference follows from a circular-path Faraday calculation.

## Concealed information acquisition

Choose one fixed endpoint and stopping rule before collection. The worked example uses N=100, K=4, objective correctness and a single upper-tail test. It does not establish a recommended sample size or actual power for a human experiment. Size a real study from a justified effect/minimum-detectable improvement and audit missing/clustered trials.

Keep target custodian, participant/facilitator, response archive, scorer and analyst appropriately separated. Record every trial identifier, original event time, response-lock time, reveal time, abstention and exclusion. Preserve every miss; abstentions must not disappear from the denominator. Make targets unpredictable conditional on previous responses and history, not merely balanced in aggregate. Human participation requires the appropriate independent consent and ethics arrangements before collection; model agents do not supply institutional review.

A public SHA-256 digest of one of four known targets discloses the target by four guesses. Use a separately reviewed commitment/custody scheme, with an unpredictable secret nonce kept secret until after response lock, or an independently secured schedule. A declared salted scheme does not prove security; a declared nonce length does not establish actual entropy, secrecy or correct disclosure. Inspect original custody logs, not self-attestation alone. Cryptographic hashes establish byte identity when checked; they do not establish that a timestamp or account is truthful.

With an actual K-way guess on every trial and independent uniform targets conditional on responses/history, the exact binomial null is available. If pre-reveal abstentions are retained as nonhits, their success probability is zero; the same Binomial(N,1/K) upper tail is a conservative reference under the stated unpredictability assumptions, not generally the exact hit-count law. Optional stopping, adaptive target prediction, information leakage or selected exclusions change the model. Keep the two contamination sensitivity models separate: a preselected subset of compromised trials is not arbitrary after-outcome editing. Actual contamination count m remains unknown until independently bounded. Even a replicated above-chance information effect would not identify its source by hit counts alone.

## Pairing and calendar controls

Join original session times to the environmental windows using a declared crosswalk and clock tolerance. Video upload, repost, catalog and later recollection dates are not substitutes. Require the protocol to be locked before the first eligible event and environmental analysis interval. An apparently complete join remains a declaration until original evidence is audited.

Freeze UTC/local solar time, station geometry, season, experiment schedule and any astronomical features before looking at outcomes. Astrology Workbench can assist with declared astronomical geometry and with keeping symbolic interpretations explicitly labeled. Several zodiac encodings of the same moment are not independent replications. Any proposed symbolic feature must improve a predeclared held-out prediction beyond a matched time/geometry baseline; do not optimize it after inspecting the target data.

Predeclare the entire lag, window, estimator and comparison family. Repeat selection inside every valid null transformation. Validate the transformation assumptions for the actual data: generic stationarity does not imply the exact finite cyclic symmetry used in the demonstration. Missingness and reporting schedules can break it. The 32-offset, seven-lag example could never reject at 0.05; the separately versioned 256-offset example resolves that particular resolution problem, not every power or causal problem.

## What the intake code checks

The final-round schema checks declared reference syntax, timestamp consistency, units, sampling/band compatibility, counts, full trial accounting and prospective analysis fields. It reports documentary, environmental, information and paired **metadata readiness** separately. It does not download source files or authenticate calibration, randomness, blinding, custody, hashes or outcomes.

A fully fabricated packet that claims observational origin may pass structural checks. It is still unauthenticated and supplies no empirical result. Synthetic-origin packets never become observational paired evidence. Keep `evidenceAuthenticated=false`, `empiricalResult=null`, `couplingEstablished=false` and `etContactEstablished=false` visible. These mean “not established by this validator,” not a proof that every proposed effect is impossible.

## Current admission and next step

The reviewed station quick looks, Kp records and channeling texts do not supply the required calibrated, synchronized empirical packet. Their source-specific documentary or contextual value remains intact. The practical next step is one small licensed calibration-bound environmental packet and an independently reviewed information-task protocol—not another claim of discovery from frequency overlap or a graph connection.

Before any novel-effect cycle, identify the exact prior result, residual gap, feasible data, matched baseline, held-out test and rejection criterion. Retain replication as replication. The six rounds in this study are complete only when their source-bound advisor decisions and worker receipts are frozen.
