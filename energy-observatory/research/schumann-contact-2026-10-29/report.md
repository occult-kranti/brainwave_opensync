29 October 2026 · Source-led review and synthetic method checks

**The inspected evidence supplies useful connections for research design, but no demonstrated Schumann–ET contact mechanism.** This study distinguishes documented assertions, calibrated geophysics, attributed communications, model results and missing causal or source-identification evidence. It establishes neither a new force nor an impossibility theorem about every proposed experience.

The work used four actual model-agent panelists—history, geophysics, channeling evidence and skepticism—and two dedicated implementation/verification workers. Root coordinated six sequential question–critique–decision rounds. These agents are not human scientists or independent experimental laboratories. Round1 inspected prior art before numerical work; later rounds were selected from actual objections. No new physical measurement or human trial was conducted.
