# Round 6 independent fixture freeze

Read only the advisor contract and producer's frozen `schema.json`/`example-packet.json` before making these fixtures. Producer implementation, outcomes and tests were not read. The schema fixes canonical millisecond UTC, the supplied asOf ceiling (not a queried wall clock), inclusive environmental event boundaries and independent lane flags. Those conventions are retained rather than silently strengthened during comparison.

The verifier independently authored fabricated records with five attempts: two hits, one miss and two abstentions. All HTTPS references use example.invalid and hashes of explicit fake labels. They are not retrieved records. A complete packet declaring observational origin and every self-attestation true may pass all metadata lanes while remaining unauthenticated. Synthetic-origin packets cannot pass pairedMetadataReady. Spoofed top-level empirical/contact/coupling fields must be ignored in favor of the unconditional false/null outputs.

Fixtures exercise the complete observational and synthetic cases; B/E component units; documentary/environment/information-only separation; canonical and impossible calendar dates; future complete-record dates; malformed references/hashes; images/Kp/constant-frequency lookup; Nyquist equality, negative bands/rates and boolean numeric inputs; missing calibration/noise/baseline; nonfinite uncertainties; trial IDs/counts/status; upload-time substitution; event/lock/reveal/commitment/nonce order; weak or unsalted commitment declarations; fixed stopping/conditional randomness; environmental crosswalk/protocol/clock limits; and in/out-of-window timestamps. Actual-material descriptors contain no invented measurements or guessed calibration hashes and must remain insufficient.

These assertions test syntax and internal consistency only. A correct hash-shaped string does not establish byte identity. A declared randomization audit, nonce length, event-time basis or calibration reference does not establish its truth. The checker should preserve that distinction even when every stage flag is true.

Before schema delivery, the verifier independently executed Python hashlib on an additional four-element literal dictionary in `hash-dictionary-oracle.json`. After receiving the frozen schema, it also computed SHA256 for the producer's fixed copper/silver/gold/iron strings without reading producer code. Gold's digest is recovered by four-candidate enumeration. This is a small known-input dictionary disclosure, not a SHA256 cryptanalytic break. Neither calculation validates a salted commitment implementation or proves actual nonce secrecy, unpredictability, domain separation or trustworthy timestamps.

After root receives these frozen hashes, a bridge may inspect and call assessPacket. If an initial result fails, retain its exact packet/output and code hash before any repair. Integration/publication checks after this final research round are delivery checks, not a seventh round.
