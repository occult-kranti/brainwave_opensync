# Continuing this study

The user requires an in-depth novelty and state-of-the-art review **before any next three-round cycle**. Read `prior-art-gate.md`, the four `panel/*/prior-art-gate.md` reports and `roadmap.json` first. The October 29 three-round cycle is a closed historical record. Do not call a later repetition a new contribution.

Before approving a new cycle, record an exact proposed proposition or measurable improvement, closest primary precedents with theorem/section and hypotheses, explicit unresolved gap, available data/calibration, baseline, falsification/stop criteria, and an advisor go/no-go decision. A failed literature search is not evidence of novelty. Replication and engineering validation may be useful but must be labelled accordingly. Unavailable proof artifacts cannot be imported as lemmas.

Preserve frozen contracts, snapshots and receipts. Corrections belong in named addenda or explicitly superseding versions, with reasons and new bindings. Keep historical reference frequencies, synthetic output, geomagnetic context and calibrated Schumann observations separate. Metadata readiness is not an anomaly result. Keep static magnetic energy density distinct from accessible energy and power.

Use the two-worker producer/independent-verifier split and four reviewers only when they resolve distinct evidence questions. Complete each contracted stage, freeze actual outputs, then select the next question from the observed objections. Do not prewrite acceptance reports or execute future cycles automatically.
