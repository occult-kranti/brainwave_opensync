# Source attribution supplement

Added after the initial producer freeze in response to the source review; the frozen model, runner, report and results were not changed.

The ideal thin-shell relation and approximate historical sequence 7.8,14.3,20.8,27.3,33.8 Hz are documented in [Simões, Pfaff and Freudenreich, NASA NTRS 20120000051](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20120000051/downloads/20120000051.pdf), printed page 3. The sequence is attributed there to Sentman (1995). It is distinct from the manuscript's own May 31, 2008 satellite observations on printed page 4. The present fitted-speed residuals do not digitize those satellite observations or establish present-day anomalies.

The magnetic examples use an illustrative static background of 50 microtesla and a separately illustrative zero-mean RMS perturbation of 1 picotesla. Neither is an October 2026 station measurement. The round's all-domain oscillator and energy identities are independently derived in `report.md`; no external source is being credited with a new local proof claim.
