# Schumann prior-art and data gate — 2026-10-29

**No-go for a fundamental novelty claim or another automatic three-round solving cycle. Conditional go for an explicitly labeled replication benchmark, after its dataset and target are frozen.** Source-driven peak changes, multistation inference, reliability diagnostics and solar-wind-associated apparent-frequency changes have substantial direct prior art. Our R1–R3 controls are useful verification and explanation, not new Schumann physics. This review performs no new fit or research round.

## The particularly close October 2026 result

Tacza et al. select 173 solar-wind proxy onsets spanning 2005–2024 independently of Kp/AE, use epochs −15 to +20 days, baseline −15 to −5 and response days 0/+1. Reported mean changes depend on estimator/station: approximately 0.0586 Hz for Hornsund Lorentz fits versus 0.0248 Hz for its weighted estimator, with smaller Finnish responses. The main paper describes 5,000 event bootstrap and 5,000 month-matched non-event ensembles; additional autocorrelation/selection tests are described in supplementary material not inspected here. These details make estimator identity, baseline and effective sample support essential. The mechanism remains non-unique, including conductivity and source effects. This is reported prior art, not our independent replication.

## The next admissible question

**Recommended replication question:** Can we reproduce the published Nagycenk model-curve/processed-data distinctions with the stated source geometry, field component and peak estimator, while keeping grid spacing separate from observational uncertainty?

This is deliberately a reproducibility task, not a promised discovery. Required inputs are the licensed benchmark files, its paper, the exact forward-model implementation/version and parameter definitions, and an explicit schema reconciliation. Freeze a published target table/curve, estimator, units, tolerated numerical discrepancy and held-out checks before computation. A discrepancy must first be assigned to transcription, grid/interpolation, model version, calibration or data processing. Actual February 2023 residuals do not provide ground-truth cavity change.

**Conditional later question:** Within explicitly bounded source, receiver and propagation nuisance models, do independent field observations require a cavity-parameter change beyond an established fixed-cavity source model? This is an unverified research question, not a novelty claim. It needs joint-model prior-art review beyond the source-only 2022 inverse, calibrated multichannel/multistation spectra, defensible error dependence, contemporaneous lightning information and a preregistered comparison. Our oscillator examples alone cannot answer it.

For a Tacza replication, split the deliverables: event-window means and estimator sensitivity may be reproducible from the listed archive; the published non-event null requires the full eligible continuous SR support or independently verifiable null products. Missing controls must remain missing. October 23–29 quick-look images and Kp cannot substitute for that support.

## Admission and kill criteria for a future contract

1. **Exact prior art:** If the proposed proposition or estimator already appears under materially the same assumptions, relabel it replication; do not launch a novelty cycle merely by changing notation or adding agents.
2. **Data and rights:** If the required interval, calibration, field definition, license or matched control support is missing, stop that empirical claim. A smaller supported replication is acceptable only under an explicitly narrower contract.
3. **Identifiability:** If admitted nuisance models yield indistinguishable observables, report the compatible parameter set or non-identifiability. Brute-force search cannot turn it into unique recovery.
4. **Baseline:** Freeze a published established model and held-out metric before optimization. If a proposed extension does not improve the declared metric beyond uncertainty, stop the improvement claim; no post hoc target change.
5. **Statistical meaning:** Use valid time-block/event dependence, eligible non-event support and multiplicity/selection controls. No anomaly significance from synthetic perfect-data tolerances or screenshot colors.
6. **Scope:** No claim of a new energy source, globally changed Earth frequency, Yang–Mills solution or transfer of OpenAI mathematics follows from success on these datasets.

Search scope includes primary 2024–2026 SR papers, their linked datasets and the closest 2019–2023 inversion foundations. It is bounded, not exhaustive. Failure to find another paper is not positive evidence of novelty. This gate recommends conditional replication only and does not execute another round.
