**Round 1: reference geometry and energy.** For an ideal thin shell, fℓ=c√[ℓ(ℓ+1)]/(2πR) and λℓ=2πR/√[ℓ(ℓ+1)]. At R=6,371 km the ideal first mode is about 10.5913 Hz. The separate vacuum reference c/7.83 Hz is about 38,287.67 km; the first horizontal modal scale is about 28,305.61 km. These quantities are not interchangeable or a measured Earth propagation wavelength.

For the mechanical oscillator m q̈+2mγ q̇+mω₀²q=F, with positive m,γ,ω₀, white-force displacement PSD peaks at √max(0,ω₀²−2γ²), velocity PSD peaks at ω₀, and oscillatory free ringdown exists only for γ<ω₀ with frequency √(ω₀²−γ²). Thus the thresholds Q>1/√2 and Q>1/2 differ. Changing readout at fixed full transfer is a fixed-pole control; changing damping is not.

Multiplying by velocity proves Ė=Fq̇−2mγq̇². Periodic steady-state input equals viscous loss.
