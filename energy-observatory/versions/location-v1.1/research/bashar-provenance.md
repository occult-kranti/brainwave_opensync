## Conflicting specifications and provenance limits

The older secondary blog https://www.ourwonderlife.com/time-is-an-illusion/ reports a table at least ten feet long and interprets a hand gesture as a roughly nine-inch hollow sphere; it also allows steel. This conflicts with the 3–4 inch wording in the 2013 transcript. Treat them as source variants, not one precise manufacturing specification. The blog contains unrelated unsupported scientific and historical claims; it is useful only for locating the sphere story.

A 2018 third-party transcript contains a likely unit/transcription problem: https://www.superphysics.org/research/other/alien/essassani/transmissions/2018/2018-09-30b/ reports 100–150 **cm** diameter with a 1–2 mm wall. This was a search lead, not an audio-verified specification. Do not silently substitute millimeters or price a device from it.

## Existing related project

https://spheredesci.org/protocol/ proposes randomized, blinded A/B comparisons of a movable hollow copper sphere against a fixed reference sphere. It explicitly states proposal stage and no confirmatory measurements. Its October 2026 materials discuss common clocking, temperature, mounting, and uncertainty. It is a related proposal, not independent evidence of a location effect or a completed preregistration. Its homepage’s “above 230 kHz” assertion was not traced to an authenticated source passage in this audit and should not become a lab requirement.

## Operational interpretation for the new page

The motivating claim has two separable parts: (A) residual position dependence in a well-defined measured spectrum; (B) destination-selective motion when a measured signal is reapplied. Neither entails the other. The first can motivate controlled resonance metrology; the second requires a separate force/momentum experiment.

No source specifies a measurement operator for an object’s “entire” or “true” frequency. A measurement must name its channel, bandwidth, sensor position, reference clock, excitation, boundary conditions and uncertainty. Acoustic displacement modes, emitted electromagnetic spectra, local magnetic noise and table transfer functions are different observables. A finite set of spectra is not a complete state description. A peak shifting when a sphere moves does not distinguish intrinsic position dependence from support loading, temperature, room propagation, mode visibility or instrument response.

Recommended question: **Does a preselected mechanical eigenmode of the same sphere show an A/B frequency shift, beyond a preregistered uncertainty threshold, after temperature, mounting, orientation, excitation and reference-clock controls?** The location coefficient is a hypothesis parameter, not a physical law. Keep a null outcome fully valid; “instruments not sensitive enough” cannot indefinitely protect a claim from a stated detection threshold.

A second channel can characterize the empty table’s point-to-point frequency response and room acoustic field. A point does not possess a unique scalar “true frequency”; one measures a response field there under declared excitation. Use physical coordinates and reference frames explicitly. Uniform translation in a homogeneous environment changes none of an isolated object’s intrinsic material parameters.
