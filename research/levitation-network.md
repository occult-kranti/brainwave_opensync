# Levitation laboratory: research and implementation

Research cut: 16 October 2026. This is a selected, source-linked investigation, not an exhaustive survey of every government record, patent, book, video or experiment. The new page extends the existing Sound Research and Newton–Tesla laboratories in both current editions. Historical readings, physical models and listening illustrations have different evidentiary roles.

## What the investigation supports

Sound, magnetic fields, electric fields, optical fields and flowing gases can exert forces. Under appropriate conditions these forces support or trap objects. That fact does not establish a reduction in gravitational interaction. A supported particle, an orbiting laboratory and a material with a quantum state are three different physical situations. A shared word such as “frequency,” “field” or “resonance” is not a mechanism connecting them.

The source registry records the exact edition or revision, inspected passage, access level, provenance, claim and limitation. Public program descriptions are separated from measured research. Patent claims and assignees are recorded without treating a grant as proof of performance. Source status applies to the stated claim, not everything the author or institution has ever said. The USA, China and Russia filters describe the record’s institutional context; the original Soviet Gor’kov paper is identified historically, not presented as a modern Russian program.

There are two historical questions. First, what did Newton, Tesla and their contemporaries actually write? Second, can a modern testable model be constructed from a particular passage? The General Scholium is evidence about Newton’s reasoning and theology. Tesla’s tuning discussions describe electrical experiments, and his inspected aircraft patent describes aerodynamic arrangements. Neither supplies a verified occult route to gravity control. The earlier Newton–Tesla dossier contains the wider alchemical and historical reading; this page connects it to force and energy questions without pretending to have reread every book.

Maxwell’s field framework enters through an inspected author derivation of electromagnetic energy and momentum. Einstein’s selected lecture passage concerns inertia, gravitation and reference frames. Casimir’s original final page gives a boundary-dependent quantum force. Gor’kov gives a restricted acoustic-scattering approximation. Berry and Geim analyze diamagnetic support; modern optical experiments address the quantum motion of externally trapped particles. These are specific mathematical connections, not evidence that the scientists all proposed the same device. Rayleigh and King remain historical antecedents cited by the inspected literature; their complete works were not newly audited here.

## Three different meanings of “free energy”

In equilibrium thermodynamics, Helmholtz free energy is F = U − TS and Gibbs free energy is G = H − TS. Under the appropriate fixed-temperature constraints, changes in these quantities bound obtainable work. “Free” describes availability under constraints, not energy appearing without a source. Environmental harvesting can also be real: a temperature difference, sunlight, vibration or radio-frequency input is an external resource and must be measured.

A claim of net cyclic output without any source is a third meaning. Use a declared boundary and the residual R = Eout + ΔEstored − Ein. Heat, radiation, mechanical work and electrical inputs belong in the same ledger. Stored electric, magnetic, chemical, elastic and gravitational energy must return to its initial state before comparing repeated cycles. Correlated uncertainties and instrument bandwidth matter. For AC circuits, average real power is the time average of v(t)i(t); multiplying separate RMS magnitudes omits phase and distortion. A remaining discrepancy calls for instrument checks and independent replication before assigning a new physical mechanism.

Casimir attraction alone is not such a cycle: restoring plate separation changes the energy budget. Resonance similarly exchanges and concentrates supplied energy. In the implemented damped oscillator, mean source power equals mean loss power at steady state. A stationary permanent-magnet support can exert force without doing mechanical work on an unmoving load; this does not make it a power generator. In each case, specify displacement, energy flow and the boundary before interpreting a striking demonstration.

## Experiments and countermodels

Every proposed experiment has a question, prediction, setup, variables, control, measurement, falsifier and limits. They include acoustic force-versus-potential checks, comparison with measured force maps, missing streaming forces, center-of-mass and apparent-weight controls, magnetic balance versus stability, quantum thermometry and an energy-cycle audit. None is reported as a newly completed physical experiment.

The browser performs only bounded numerical calculations. Its acoustic pressure is a hypothetical peak field amplitude, not a device operating instruction or exposure recommendation. A real ultrasonic array needs calibrated spatial fields, appropriate facilities and an apparatus-specific review. The magnetic calculation supplies a local signed field-squared gradient without designing a magnet. Superconducting, high-voltage and laser experiments are referenced research setups rather than home-build prescriptions. Precise setup illustrations are original SVG schematics; they are not photographs or authenticated reconstructions of an inventor’s apparatus.

The standing-wave model demonstrates that force balance and restoring slope are separate tests. It includes buoyancy and both signs of acoustic contrast. Its transverse directions are unconstrained. A positive sum of curvature values is insufficient: a Hessian with eigenvalues 3, −1 and −1 has positive trace but two unstable directions. Full conservative stability requires the relevant Hessian, and nonconservative or driven systems need their own dynamical analysis.

## Software and media choices

The existing React, TypeScript and Vite application remains the delivery stack. Pure bounded functions and SVG are sufficient for these teaching calculations and accessible exports. Adding a finite-element or boundary-element framework to the browser would not create missing calibration data. Pinned Levitate, AcousTools and Ultraino source inspections show how more complete array and field tools are organized. They are research references, not installed controller integrations. Their MIT license and inspected revisions are recorded. No external source code was copied into the product.

An independent numerical comparison evaluates the actual app output against selected unmodified reference routines over 244 samples. Agreement at floating-point precision verifies the translation on those fields; the models share assumptions and this is not experimental confirmation. The separate code audit documents a verified AcousTools v7 equation/code sign discrepancy without claiming that its whole library is invalid.

Official NASA/JPL text was read where available. A TU Delft lecture’s supplied English captions were genuinely downloaded with the open-source yt-dlp tool and read with timestamps. This is caption retrieval, not new speech recognition. The FLOAT lecture record states what could be established from its official page and what was not watched or transcribed. No inaccessible video is represented as reviewed, and no full third-party transcript is redistributed. WikiLeaks results are recorded as a bounded search and access log; irrelevant or inaccessible records are not evidence for gravity control.

## Listening and preservation

Four original grade-D recipes illustrate a steady frequency reference, stereo phase drift, rising frequencies and falling frequencies. They are grouped as “Levitation studies.” The name links the reading collection; the sounds do not levitate matter or establish a medical effect. Frequency is only one field parameter. Headphones do not reproduce a calibrated ultrasonic array or magnetic apparatus.

The existing playback engine and safety gates are reused. The new model modules never emit sound. All 155 previous WAV previews are preserved byte-for-byte. Four compact, two-second 12 kHz PCM previews are valid for the new recipes, whose tones do not exceed 900 Hz. Per-entry format metadata is checked against every WAV header, while existing manifest defaults remain unchanged. Full listening recipes use the existing synthesis path. The catalog now contains 159 presets and stays below the existing 60 MB preview budget.

Implementation locations: `src/levitation/`, `src/pages/LevitationLab.tsx`, `src/data/levitationPresets.ts`. The public dossier combines this introduction, individual topic audits, the source registry, typed connections, mathematical contracts and validation notes. It distinguishes observed results, analytical predictions and future proposals throughout.

## Evidence network

Each relationship has a status and a missing test; proximity is not causal proof.

### Newton: motion and gravity → Levitation force budget

Status: **derived**. In a near-Earth model, upward support must balance weight and the response to a displacement must also be checked. The acoustic bench includes buoyancy.

Missing bridge: A force-balanced point does not establish stability in three dimensions.

Sources: [lev-newton-scholium](#source-lev-newton-scholium), [acoustic-settnes-2012](#source-acoustic-settnes-2012)

### Newton’s theological and subtle-spirit language → Gravity-control apparatus

Status: **unsupported**. The inspected Scholium records historical inquiry and explicitly lacks sufficient experiments for precise laws of the proposed subtle spirit. Earlier alchemical sources are discussed in the Newton–Tesla Lab.

Missing bridge: An operational variable, measured coupling, quantitative prediction and controlled replication are absent from this proposed bridge.

Sources: [lev-newton-scholium](#source-lev-newton-scholium)

### Tesla: tuned electrical systems → Acoustic resonance

Status: **analogy**. Driven systems can share oscillator mathematics while storing energy in different physical variables. Similar equations do not make sound an electromagnetic field.

Missing bridge: A transducer law, geometry, material response and calibrated losses are needed to connect a circuit to an acoustic force.

Sources: [lev-tesla-lecture](#source-lev-tesla-lecture), [foundation-energy](#source-foundation-energy)

### Tesla aircraft patent → Aerodynamic lift

Status: **documented**. The inspected patent describes air propulsion and wing arrangements. Its flight claim is not a documented sound-levitation or gravity-shielding mechanism.

Missing bridge: A patent describes claimed subject matter; performance still needs an instrumented demonstration.

Sources: [lev-tesla-aircraft](#source-lev-tesla-aircraft)

### Gor’kov: small-particle scattering → Acoustic potential and force

Status: **documented**. The original Soviet paper and later derivation connect pressure and velocity fields to a leading small-sphere radiation force under stated limits.

Missing bridge: Finite-size scattering, viscosity, streaming and traveling-wave dominance can invalidate the ideal potential-only approximation.

Sources: [acoustic-gorkov-1961](#source-acoustic-gorkov-1961), [acoustic-settnes-2012](#source-acoustic-settnes-2012), [acoustic-stone-2026](#source-acoustic-stone-2026)

### Standing-wave pressure node → Every acoustic trap

Status: **unsupported**. Pressure-node trapping is a useful particular case. The 2026 Bessel-beam experiment reports trapping in a high-pressure core, illustrating why field geometry and particle response matter.

Missing bridge: A universal node rule would need to explain contrast signs, scattering, reflections and the measured stability in different fields.

Sources: [acoustic-koroyasu-2026](#source-acoustic-koroyasu-2026), [acoustic-brown-2024](#source-acoustic-brown-2024)

### Published acoustic equations → Open-source simulation

Status: **documented**. Levitate and AcousTools implement pressure-gradient force calculations. Pinned code permits coefficient, sign and unit audits before using an optimizer.

Missing bridge: Numerical agreement under shared assumptions is not experimental validation or a complete hardware reproduction.

Sources: [tool-levitate](#source-tool-levitate), [tool-acoustools](#source-tool-acoustools), [acoustic-ultraino-2018](#source-acoustic-ultraino-2018)

### Diamagnetism and superconductivity → Magnetic support

Status: **documented**. Field gradients can supply support; flux pinning adds history-dependent forces. These are distinct mechanisms and the simple susceptibility bench does not model a superconductor.

Missing bridge: A realizable field geometry, lateral stability and material characterization are needed beyond local vertical balance.

Sources: [berry-geim-diamagnetism-1997](#source-berry-geim-diamagnetism-1997), [qin-superconductor-hysteresis-2001](#source-qin-superconductor-hysteresis-2001), [wang-pinned-flux-dynamics-2019](#source-wang-pinned-flux-dynamics-2019)

### Maxwell fields: momentum transfer → Optically trapped quantum motion

Status: **derived**. Optical fields exchange momentum with particles. Cooling and measurement can prepare low-occupation motional states in an externally maintained trap.

Missing bridge: The optical setup, pressure, feedback and thermometry must be specified; a headphone frequency does not reproduce them.

Sources: [foundation-maxwell](#source-foundation-maxwell), [delic-optical-ground-state-2020](#source-delic-optical-ground-state-2020), [tebbenjohanns-feedback-2021](#source-tebbenjohanns-feedback-2021)

### Einstein: equivalence and free fall → Apparent weight and gravity change

Status: **derived**. A scale measures support force, while free-fall behavior addresses a different observable. Ordinary acceleration or external support can change a scale reading.

Missing bridge: An anomalous-gravity claim needs independent motion measurements and rejection of electromagnetic, aerodynamic, vibration and instrumental explanations.

Sources: [foundation-einstein](#source-foundation-einstein), [lev-nasa-mechanical](#source-lev-nasa-mechanical), [lev-nasa-capacitor](#source-lev-nasa-capacitor)

### Casimir boundary energy → Cyclic power without an energy source

Status: **unsupported**. The ideal plate calculation gives a separation-dependent attractive force. An energy decrease during approach does not establish net work after restoring the original state.

Missing bridge: A full-cycle energy ledger including the reset and every external input, followed by independent measurements, is missing.

Sources: [foundation-casimir](#source-foundation-casimir), [foundation-energy](#source-foundation-energy), [foundation-ratchet](#source-foundation-ratchet)

### Resonance amplification → Energy generation

Status: **unsupported**. The vibration bench can magnify displacement near resonance while average input power equals dissipated power in steady state.

Missing bridge: A large amplitude or reactive voltage is not net output energy; measure real power and changes in stored energy.

Sources: [lev-tesla-lecture](#source-lev-tesla-lecture), [foundation-energy](#source-foundation-energy), [foundation-maxwell](#source-foundation-maxwell)

### USA: government reports and assigned patents → Validated gravity engineering

Status: **unsupported**. NASA’s assessment records a mixture of tests, unresolved work and proposals. The inspected Navy-assigned inertia-reduction patent provides claims rather than a reproducible performance dataset.

Missing bridge: Government custody, funding or assignment does not supply missing controls, raw measurements or independent replication.

Sources: [lev-nasa-bpp](#source-lev-nasa-bpp), [lev-nasa-capacitor](#source-lev-nasa-capacitor), [lev-navy-inertia](#source-lev-navy-inertia)

### China: space electrostatic levitation program → Containerless materials research

Status: **documented**. Two CAS institutional records describe one space materials program and its samples. Electrostatic positioning in microgravity is the stated research context.

Missing bridge: These program reports do not demonstrate altered gravity or validate a terrestrial acoustic recipe.

Sources: [lev-china-rack](#source-lev-china-rack), [lev-china-samples](#source-lev-china-samples)

### Russia: magnetic and droplet-support grants → Ordinary-force research

Status: **documented**. The inspected Russian Science Foundation records concern superconducting magnetic levitation and vapor-flow droplet support, respectively.

Missing bridge: Different mechanisms and dated grant reports are not evidence of a unified secret antigravity or free-energy program.

Sources: [lev-russia-superconductor](#source-lev-russia-superconductor), [lev-russia-droplets](#source-lev-russia-droplets)

### Audible listening presets → Physical or biological levitation

Status: **unsupported**. The four quiet recipes illustrate frequency, phase drift and ordering. They do not supply a calibrated ultrasonic array, magnet, optical trap or clinical intervention.

Missing bridge: Frequency alone omits amplitude, field geometry, material coupling and dose. No levitation or healing outcome is established for these presets.

Sources: [acoustic-tinylev-2017](#source-acoustic-tinylev-2017), [berry-geim-diamagnetism-1997](#source-berry-geim-diamagnetism-1997)

### Chinese university acoustic patent application → Levitation as a sensing mechanism

Status: **documented**. The inspected NUAA application proposes an ultrasonic-levitation accelerometer. A patent can suggest a measurable architecture without providing independent accuracy data.

Missing bridge: The application is not assumed granted, and the proposed instrument still needs calibration, uncertainty analysis and replication.

Sources: [lev-china-acoustic-patent](#source-lev-china-acoustic-patent)

### Russian state-university maglev patent → Track-supplied mechanical power

Status: **documented**. The inspected design places powered motion in the track. Removing a levitation power supply from the vehicle does not remove the energy source from the full system.

Missing bridge: Test the complete track-plus-vehicle energy and force budget; the patent is not a demonstrated source-free generator.

Sources: [lev-russia-maglev-patent](#source-lev-russia-maglev-patent), [foundation-energy](#source-foundation-energy)

### Gor’kov: small-particle forces in an ideal fluid

[acoustic-gorkov-1961](https://www.mathnet.ru/rus/dan25473) · 1961; English translation 1962 · International · historical

Version: Doklady AN SSSR 140(1), 88–91; original Russian edition

Read: Four-page scan; assumptions on p. 88, potential and standing-wave equations 12–13 on p. 90, qualifications and references on pp. 90–91.

Access: Original scan read, with equations 12–13 checked visually; English translation not inspected.

Provenance: L. P. Gor’kov, Soviet Academy Institute for Physical Problems; archived by Math-Net.ru. Historical Soviet research, not a current government-program claim.

Supports: Derives a time-averaged force potential from incident and scattered sound for particles small relative to wavelength in an ideal fluid.

Limits: Viscosity and heat conduction excluded; near-plane traveling waves need the higher-order scattering contribution. The paper does not validate our app or remove gravity.

### Settnes and Bruus: where viscosity changes radiation force

[acoustic-settnes-2012](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.85.016327) · 2012-01-30 · International · derived

Version: Physical Review E 85, 016327; inspected author version arXiv:1110.6037v1 (2011-10-27)

Access: Full author PDF accessible at https://arxiv.org/pdf/1110.6037v1; final publication identity verified.

# Acoustic levitation: primary-source audit and model boundaries

Audit date: 2026-10-16. This is a selected reading of eight sources, with recent primary research verified through August 2026. It is not a systematic review or a claim to have read every historical work, patent, supplement or dataset. The browser laboratory evaluates an ideal mechanical model. It neither drives an ultrasonic array nor demonstrates physical levitation through ordinary audio playback.

The most useful distinction is between a field calculation, a measured force, and a stable physical trajectory. These are related but different observations. A correct standing-wave plot alone establishes none of the other two. Published experiments support acoustic momentum transfer in particular apparatuses; they do not support gravity cancellation, energy creation or a medical effect.

## Reconstructing the ideal 1D model

This algebra is our explicit reduction of the radiation-potential model to a single ideal standing plane wave. It is not a reconstruction of the complete experimental apparatuses above. Let the upward coordinate be \(z\), particle radius \(a\), volume \(V=4\pi a^3/3\), fluid density \(\rho_0\), particle density \(\rho_p\), and sound speed \(c_0\). Define \(k=\omega/c_0\), \(\theta=kz+\phi\), and compressibility \(\kappa_0=1/(\rho_0c_0^2)\).

Choose **peak standing-wave pressure amplitude** \(p_A\), not RMS amplitude and not the amplitude of either constituent traveling wave:

\[
p(z,t)=p_A\cos\theta\cos\omega t,
\qquad v(z,t)=\frac{p_A}{\rho_0c_0}\sin\theta\sin\omega t.
\]

These fields obey the linear momentum equation. Time averaging one cycle supplies the factor \(1/2\) in each squared field. The dimensionless compressibility and density coefficients are

\[
f_\kappa=1-\frac{\kappa_p}{\kappa_0},\qquad
f_\rho=\frac{2(\rho_p-\rho_0)}{2\rho_p+\rho_0},\qquad
\Phi=\frac{f_\kappa}{3}+\frac{f_\rho}{2}.
\]

The potential is

\[
U=V\left[\frac{f_\kappa\langle p^2\rangle}{2\rho_0c_0^2}
-\frac{3\rho_0f_\rho\langle v^2\rangle}{4}\right]
=VE_{ac}\left[f_\kappa\cos^2\theta-\frac32f_\rho\sin^2\theta\right],
\quad E_{ac}=\frac{p_A^2}{4\rho_0c_0^2}.
\]

Differentiating, rather than choosing attractive-looking nodes, gives

\[
F_{ac,z}=-\frac{\partial U}{\partial z}
=3VkE_{ac}\Phi\sin(2\theta).
\]

Brown's equation 2 uses \(f_0=f_\kappa\), \(f_1=f_\rho\), but its named contrast factor is \(f_0+3f_1/2=3\Phi\). Mixing the two conventions creates a factor-of-three error. Gor’kov's original equation 12 has equivalent potential coefficients; notation and publication editions differ.

| --- | --- |
| \(E_{ac}\) | \(\mathrm{Pa^2}/(\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}\,\mathrm{m^2s^{-2}})=\mathrm{Pa}=\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}\) |
| \(U\) | \(\mathrm{m^3}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}=\mathrm{J}\) |
| \(F_{ac,z}\) | \(\mathrm{m^3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}=\mathrm{N}\) |

## Balance is necessary; restoring behavior is another condition

Including hydrostatic buoyancy gives effective weight \(W=(\rho_p-\rho_0)Vg\). With \(A=3VkE_{ac}\Phi\),

\[
F_{tot}=A\sin(2\theta)-W,\qquad
\sin(2\theta_*)=W/A.
\]

For nonzero \(A\), \(|W|\leq|A|\) is the existence condition. A root is locally restoring along the axis only if

\[
K_z=-\left.\frac{\partial F_{tot}}{\partial z}\right|_{z_*}
=-6Vk^2E_{ac}\Phi\cos(2\theta_*)>0.
\]

At the equality \(|W|=|A|\), stiffness is zero. Labeling that boundary as a stable trap would be wrong. For positive contrast without gravity, a pressure node is an axial potential minimum. At the exact node, the radiation force is zero; a nonzero weight therefore requires a displaced equilibrium. Negative contrast exchanges the preferred positions. Zero contrast removes the leading-order force, rather than creating a special levitation frequency.

Near a restoring root, a separate dynamical approximation is \(m\ddot\xi+b\dot\xi+K_z\xi=0\). Without damping, it predicts bounded local oscillation rather than settling. An assumed damping term does not account for every streaming force. An ideal 1D wave has no transverse confinement, so this calculation cannot certify a 3D trap. More generally one needs the full force Jacobian, coupling between axes and the relevant dynamics.

Particle volume cancels from the ideal force-to-weight ratio. This does **not** imply that arbitrary particle sizes levitate equally: the approximation itself fails as scattering, shape, loading and material-response effects change. Equally, varying a digital amplitude does not determine \(p_A\); the speaker, array, geometry and calibration are absent from a digital signal's units.

## Assumptions and useful counterexamples

The model requires a small sphere relative to wavelength, weak acoustic perturbations, an idealized single incident field, negligible higher-order scattering and sufficiently slow particle motion for cycle averaging. The chosen browser cutoff \(ka\leq0.3\) is a conservative interface convention, not a published universal accuracy guarantee. No fixed cutoff validates every material or boundary condition.

Viscous thickness scales as \(\delta=\sqrt{2\nu/\omega}\). An inviscid approximation needs more than small \(ka\): \(\delta/a\), thermal boundary layers, absorption and material behavior matter. The broader [Karlsen–Bruus thermoviscous paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.92.043010) was located and its abstract/introduction inspected as a follow-up lead; its full derivation was not audited or implemented. Nothing here models biological samples.

Four failure cases keep the interpretation honest:

1. A force-balanced root with the wrong slope is unstable; a pressure plot can hide that distinction.
2. A perfect axial wave may support weight locally while leaving a particle free to escape sideways.
3. Multiple objects scatter sound and alter each other's forces; adding independent point-particle results need not reproduce that interaction.
4. A velocity-gradient term can favor a high-pressure region in another field geometry. The Bessel result does not contradict the restricted 1D derivation; it rejects extending its node rule to all fields.

Radiation force comes from momentum exchange with the driven acoustic field and its surroundings. A stationary supported object has zero mechanical work rate \(\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf v\), but the apparatus can still consume power through dissipation and sound radiation. Static force balance is therefore neither a power-efficiency measurement nor evidence of free energy.

## Newton and the documented historical chain

The relevant Newton connection is methodological. In [De motu corporum in gyrum, late 1684](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/NATP00089), the inspected Theorem 2 corollaries and Theorem 3 connect motion with force under specified geometric and temporal assumptions. Our modern adaptation is to infer a force model, calculate consequences, then test those consequences with an independent observable. Newton did not supply this acoustic radiation potential or the modern array mechanism.

Gor’kov's inspected bibliography cites King and Yosioka–Kawasima, and the modern sources identify that scattering lineage. The term “Rayleigh limit” denotes the small-particle approximation here; it does not certify a close reading of Rayleigh's books. This audit does not invent a direct Newton→Tesla→Gor’kov influence chain from shared vocabulary such as force, resonance or vibration. Historical influence needs documentary evidence; mathematical analogy needs an explicit mapping and its limits.

## Three proposed comparisons

These are hypotheses and study designs, not completed experiments or measured results. The implementation exposes their predictions, controls, measurements, falsifiers and limitations separately.

**1. Independent gradient calculation.** Compare the analytic force above with a separately computed finite-difference gradient of the potential. Check numerical convergence, periodicity, zero-pressure and zero-contrast limits, and stiffness classification at the force ceiling. A persistent disagreement rejects the implementation. Agreement establishes mathematical consistency only.

**2. Force and trajectory as separate observables.** Begin with licensed published data. Fit one observable and hold another condition out before fitting. Compare force profiles, equilibrium displacement and restoring stiffness with propagated calibration uncertainty. A later qualified-laboratory study could use an enclosed apparatus, independent force measurement and optical tracking. Probe-present checks, background forces, drift monitoring and independently repeated runs are essential conceptual controls. Systematic held-out errors reject that model in the tested range; a null result retains its uncertainty and detection limit.

**3. Competing explanations for residual error.** Compare radiation-only predictions with models whose airflow and reflection terms are constrained by independent observations. Hold field calibration and test conditions fixed across the comparison. A model that improves only after every condition is refitted has not demonstrated explanatory prediction. Failure to improve within uncertainty leaves the mechanism unresolved rather than proving it absent. This app does not implement a streaming or full reflection solver.

High-output airborne ultrasound requires dedicated apparatus and professional acoustic assessment. These study concepts deliberately specify observables and controls rather than voltages, array-driving sequences or exposure settings. Browser controls remain an educational mathematical interface.

## Energy and connection audit

# Magnetic support, quantum motion and institutional media

Audit date: 16 October 2026. This is a selected primary-source review, not an exhaustive corpus or an independent replication. The seven source records in `src/levitation/quantum-media.ts` identify what was actually read. A `measured` label means that the authors report a measurement; it does not mean OpenSync performed it.

## What the mechanism must explain

Levitation means a specified object is supported without an ordinary contact support. A claim must identify the upward force, reaction path, equilibrium and stability. A material can remain subject to gravity while another force balances its weight. Reduced mechanical contact does not establish lossless motion, an unlimited energy source or gravitational shielding.

For the browser's small, isotropic, weak-susceptibility approximation, with upward coordinate z:

\[
U=\rho Vgz-\frac{\chi V B^2}{2\mu_0},\qquad
F_z=\frac{\chi V}{2\mu_0}\partial_z B^2-\rho Vg.
\]

Here χ is dimensionless, ρ is kg/m³, V is m³, B is T, its squared gradient is T²/m, U is J and F is N. With χ<0 an upward magnetic force requires a negative squared-field gradient. Volume cancels from the force-to-weight ratio under these assumptions. At equilibrium, a positive-definite Hessian of U is a sufficient nondegenerate local stability condition. The current local-gradient calculator does not supply that Hessian or a complete field geometry. [Berry & Geim, sections 2–3](https://www.physics.umd.edu/grt/taj/411c/FlyingFrogs.pdf).

Superconducting response needs a separate model. Flux expulsion and history-dependent pinned flux cannot be replaced indiscriminately by the weak-χ approximation. In the reviewed disk calculation, force follows spatial currents and magnetic field; the magnitude law E=Ec(|J|/Jc)^n models creep. E is V/m, J is A/m² and n is dimensionless; the vector direction/sign must be restored in a numerical implementation. Local stiffness is −∂Fz/∂z in N/m. This nonlinear, history-dependent model is not implemented in the browser. [Qin et al., v2, sections II and III D–E](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0111316v2).

The [Wang et al. experiment](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08748v5) supplies an instructive counterexample: positional trapping by pinned flux may hinder the nearly free precession desired for sensing. A stable position is not automatically a useful force sensor.

## What makes an observed result quantum

An optically trapped particle can first behave as a classical oscillator. A quantum claim requires a calibrated observable, not the word “levitated.” For a harmonic motional mode, Ē=ℏΩ(n̄+1/2), with Ω in rad/s. Its zero-point position scale is √(ℏ/(2mΩ)), in metres. Neither expression changes the gravitational force term. Mean occupation below one does not imply every realization is exactly in the ground state.

## 1. Ideal standing acoustic wave and gravity

The primary derivation is [Settnes and Bruus, *Physical Review E* 85, 016327 (2012), author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1110.6037). We use its inviscid small-sphere limit, not its more general viscous correction. Its liquid examples are not a validation of an air experiment. Fixed teaching parameters are air density $\rho_0=1.2\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, sound speed $c_0=343\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and $g=9.80665\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

Let upward position be $z$, $k=2\pi f/c_0$, and $\theta=kz+\phi$. The imposed fields are

$$p(z,t)=p_A\cos\theta\cos\omega t,\qquad v(z,t)=\frac{p_A}{\rho_0c_0}\sin\theta\sin\omega t.$$

$p_A$ is the **peak pressure at an antinode**, not RMS. Exported pressure and velocity amplitudes are signed spatial coefficients with different temporal phases; they are not simultaneous instantaneous peaks. Pressure RMS at a location is $|p_A\cos\theta|/\sqrt2$.

For a homogeneous sphere with volume $V=4\pi a^3/3$, particle density $\rho_p$ and compressibility $\kappa_p$,

$$\kappa_0=\frac{1}{\rho_0c_0^2},\quad f_1=1-\frac{\kappa_p}{\kappa_0},\quad f_2=\frac{2(\rho_p-\rho_0)}{2\rho_p+\rho_0},\quad \Phi=\frac{f_1}{3}+\frac{f_2}{2}.$$

Our coefficient convention follows the cited preprint; other papers can number these coefficients differently or call $3\Phi$ the contrast factor. With $E_{ac}=p_A^2/(4\rho_0c_0^2)$,

$$U_{ac}=VE_{ac}[f_1\cos^2\theta-\tfrac32f_2\sin^2\theta],\qquad F_{ac}=-\frac{dU_{ac}}{dz}=3VkE_{ac}\Phi\sin2\theta.$$

The sphere experiences weight $\rho_pVg$ downward and buoyancy $\rho_0Vg$ upward. Define $W=(\rho_p-\rho_0)Vg>0$ over the chosen range. Then

$$U_{total}=U_{ac}+Wz,\qquad F_{net}=F_{ac}-W.$$

Force and acoustic potential repeat after $\lambda/2$. The signed pressure field repeats after $\lambda$; a half-wavelength changes its sign. The gravity tilt makes total potential nonperiodic. Increasing spatial phase by $\Delta\phi$ translates the landscape by $-\Delta\phi/k$. This phase is a coordinate of the ideal standing wave, **not a claimed mapping to two real transducer controls**.

Writing $A=3VkE_{ac}\Phi$, an equilibrium solves $A\sin2\theta=W$. If $|A|<W$, every net force is downward and no equilibrium exists. If $|A|>W$, one local restoring root and one unstable root occur per force period. The restoring condition is $dF_{net}/dz=2kA\cos2\theta<0$, and its stiffness is $K_z=2k\sqrt{A^2-W^2}$. Both positive and negative contrast are handled. At $|A|=W$ the roots merge with zero stiffness: this is **marginal**, not stable. Numerical equality uses a relative force-ratio tolerance of $10^{-10}$.

`minimumPressurePeakPa` is the algebraic force-ceiling threshold, not a pressure recommendation and not a sufficient condition for real trapping. Strictly restoring equilibrium requires pressure above this equality. It is `null` when contrast cancels within $10^{-14}$. Force, weight and stiffness scale with particle volume, while their force ratio and pressure threshold do not within this approximation. Real size effects outside the model do not cancel.

## 2. Local diamagnetic force budget

[Berry and Geim, *Of flying frogs and levitrons* (1997)](https://www.physics.umd.edu/grt/taj/411c/FlyingFrogs.pdf) discusses magnetic potential, levitation and spatial stability. We implement only the local weak, linear, isotropic susceptibility approximation:

$$U=\rho Vgz-\frac{\chi V}{2\mu_0}B^2,\qquad F_{mag,z}=\frac{\chi V}{2\mu_0}\frac{d(B^2)}{dz},\qquad F_{net,z}=F_{mag,z}-\rho Vg.$$

$\chi$ is dimensionless **SI volume susceptibility**, negative here. With $z$ upward, a negative $d(B^2)/dz$ gives an upward diamagnetic force. The gradient is $d(B^2)/dz=2B\,dB/dz$, so substituting a published $B\,dB/dz$ number without multiplying by two would be an error. We use $\mu_0\approx4\pi\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m}$ as a teaching approximation; it is not an exact defining constant in revised SI.

The local balance gradient is $2\mu_0\rho g/\chi$. Both magnetic force and weight scale with $V$, so their ratio and balance gradient are independent of sphere radius in this approximation. A uniform field has no force. A positive gradient pushes this diamagnet downward and adds to weight.

## 3. Driven mechanical vibration comparison

[OpenStax, Forced Oscillations](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/15-6-forced-oscillations) presents the driven damped oscillator. This panel is explicitly **not a levitation or feedback model**. A conventional linear spring supports the mass; gravity gives static downward sag $mg/k_s$. Dynamic displacement $x$ is measured from that equilibrium:

$$m\ddot x+b\dot x+k_sx=F_A\cos\omega t,\quad \omega_0=\sqrt{k_s/m},\quad b=2\zeta\sqrt{k_sm}.$$

With $r=\omega/\omega_0$, the steady displacement peak and force-relative phase are

$$X=\frac{F_A/k_s}{\sqrt{(1-r^2)^2+(2\zeta r)^2}},\qquad\alpha=-\operatorname{atan2}(2\zeta r,1-r^2).$$

Negative phase denotes displacement lag. Differentiating that amplitude equation gives our displacement-response maximum at $f_0\sqrt{1-2\zeta^2}$ only for $\zeta<1/\sqrt2$; otherwise there is no positive-frequency displacement maximum. It is distinct from natural frequency $f_0$. At $f_0$ the transfer phase is −90°. The transfer magnification remains defined at zero forcing, while actual dynamic motion and power are zero.

Source work and viscous dissipation are computed independently:

$$\overline P_{input}=\tfrac12F_A\omega X(-\sin\alpha),\qquad\overline P_{loss}=\tfrac12b(\omega X)^2.$$

They agree in steady state. The source supplies the loss; no extra gravity or energy term is added. Linear motion can exceed real spring travel within hypothetical combinations; the model does not assess a device's mechanical limits.

API: `calculateDrivenOscillator`, `buildMechanicalRows`, `mechanicalCsv`, `DEFAULT_MECHANICAL_INPUT`. The split logarithmic frequency sweep includes $f_0$ exactly and is clipped to 0.2–100 Hz. ## Paper equation 10 differs from active code

The visually inspected v7 PDF gives

$$K_{2,\mathrm{PDF}}=\frac{3V}{4}\frac{\rho_0-\rho_p}{\omega^2\rho_0(\rho_0+2\rho_p)}.$$

The pinned active `get_gorkov_constants` instead uses

$$K_{2,\mathrm{code}}=\frac{3V}{4}\frac{\rho_p-\rho_0}{\omega^2\rho_0(\rho_0+2\rho_p)}.$$

Both the paper's equation 8 and the active `gorkov_analytical` expression subtract the $K_2|\nabla p|^2$ term. Thus the numerator difference is consequential, not removed by that outer sign. The code coefficient agrees with Levitate and the app. Expanding the standard dipole factor $f_2=2(\rho_p-\rho_0)/(2\rho_p+\rho_0)$ and using $|v|^2=|\nabla p|^2/(\omega^2\rho_0^2)$ gives the active-code expression.

**Finding:** a specific document/code sign discrepancy is confirmed. Its cause and any author correction were not established here. Do not reproduce equation 10 as if it documented the exact active-code coefficient. No claim is made that the entire AcousTools library is wrong or that every calling path was audited. Commented-out expressions in the source are not treated as executed code.

## A positive trace is insufficient for a three-dimensional trap

Our counterexample is the quadratic potential

$$U(x,y,z)=\frac{k_*}{2}(3x^2-y^2-z^2),\qquad k_*>0.$$

At its stationary point, the Hessian is $k_*\operatorname{diag}(3,-1,-1)$, with positive trace $k_*$ but two negative eigenvalues. It restores in $x$ and is unstable in $y$ and $z$. Numerical evaluation gives eigenvalues $k_*[-1,-1,3]$. Therefore a positive Laplacian, the trace of the Hessian, does not certify a local minimum. Even positive diagonal entries alone would not generally suffice without checking mixed terms.

For a conservative smooth potential, a positive-definite Hessian at an equilibrium is sufficient for a strict nondegenerate local minimum. Negative eigenvalues identify unstable directions. Zero eigenvalues require additional analysis; they do not establish confinement. The implemented ideal plane standing wave has no transverse dependence at all, so its vertical restoring root coexists with neutral transverse directions. It is explicitly labeled a **local 1D** result. Damping, nonconservative forces, time-dependent control and full 3D dynamics require further analysis.

## Separate mechanical source clarification

OpenStax's forced-oscillation amplitude equation supports the implemented linear response. The displayed displacement-peak formula $f_0\sqrt{1-2\zeta^2}$ for $\zeta<1/\sqrt2$ is **our differentiation of that amplitude equation**, not a quotation of its prose, which describes the peak as occurring at the natural frequency. The app distinguishes natural frequency, displacement-response maximum and cycle-mean source/loss power.

No release-blocking disagreement with the app's implemented model was found in this bounded numerical comparison. Real-air validity, boundary-layer corrections, streaming, multi-particle forces, finite geometry and apparatus behavior remain outside its scope.

## Phase comparison

For separate channel frequencies, the change in relative phase is `Δφ(t)−Δφ(0)=2π(fR−fL)t`. The detuned pair advances by 0.75 cycles each second, or one full turn in 4/3 seconds. The preset schema has no initial-phase control. The shared-frequency reference has no ongoing phase drift from detuning.

Both signals are ordinary audible sine tones. Neither channel contains a standalone 0.75 Hz pressure-wave tone. Stereo headphones keep the signals in different channels; combining loudspeaker fields in a room would add propagation and reflection conditions that this preset does not specify. A listening impression of fluctuation would not demonstrate a spatial trap or measure a brain response.

[OpenStax, Mathematics of Waves, §16.2](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/16-2-mathematics-of-waves) supplies the phase and wave-speed relations. The frequencies, gain, duration and comparison design are ours.

## Frequency, wavelength and sequence order

For an explicitly fixed propagation speed, `λ=c/f`: the rising frequency sequence corresponds to wavelength ratios `1, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4`. The reverse sequence reverses those ratios. No measured propagation speed, room dimensions or particle properties are part of these recipes, so they report no wavelength in metres, node location or force. Their audible content is successive pitches, rather than all four frequencies sounding together.

[OpenStax, Standing Waves and Resonance, §16.6](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/16-6-standing-waves-and-resonance) derives a spatial standing-wave pattern from counterpropagating waves and boundary conditions. Shared-frequency headphone channels alone do not specify that physical arrangement. Frequency sequences can accompany the visual model as an illustration of frequency change, but do not render its spatial field.

Reversing the sequence preserves total exposure time at each pitch. This offers a simple comparison of ascending and descending order. It does not establish a therapeutic effect, nor does it isolate an effect of order from familiarity, expectations or ordinary loudness differences without a separately designed study.

[Marzo et al., *Holographic acoustic elements for manipulation of levitated objects*, Nature Communications 6, 8661 (2015)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4627579/) is the primary source for the limited connection: phased acoustic fields can be shaped experimentally, and force depends on their spatial structure and the target. ## Proposed experiment register

### Does the force follow the potential gradient?

Status: Proposed · Ideal 1D standing wave, small-sphere scattering and buoyancy-corrected weight

**Question:** Do two independent calculations agree on force, equilibrium and local axial stability?

**Prediction:** An analytic force and a converged finite-difference negative potential gradient agree; the force-ceiling equality has zero stiffness, not a restoring trap.

**Setup:** Computational comparison using the equations and conventions in the audit. Evaluate an analytic expression and a separate numerical derivative; no hardware or sound playback.

**Variables:** Phase, contrast sign, dimensionless weight-to-force ratio and numerical step size, varied separately within the model assumptions.

**Control:** Zero pressure, zero contrast and gravity-free limits; phase shifts by a full spatial period; compare stable and unstable roots.

**Measurement:** Force residual, equilibrium residual and stiffness sign with a convergence curve. Results would be calculated outputs, not measured levitation.

**Falsifier:** A residual that persists as the derivative step converges, wrong phase periodicity, or a stable label at zero or negative stiffness rejects the implementation.

**Limits:** Numerical agreement tests algebra and implementation only. It cannot validate material properties, real sound pressure, transverse confinement or losses.

### Can one calibrated model predict both force and displacement?

Status: Proposed · Radiation-force balance distinguished from local restoring stiffness

**Question:** Does a model fitted to one observable predict a separately measured observable without refitting?

**Prediction:** A useful model should predict held-out force profiles and small-displacement stiffness within declared calibration uncertainty.

**Setup:** Begin with licensed published force and position data. A qualified laboratory could separately compare an enclosed, calibrated levitation apparatus with optical tracking and a force probe; this is a study concept, not build or exposure instructions.

**Variables:** Particle properties, location, measured field amplitude and temperature; define a held-out condition before fitting.

**Control:** Background-force measurements, probe-present versus probe-absent field checks, repeated independent runs and a prespecified simpler 1D baseline.

**Measurement:** Calibrated force, position distribution, local stiffness and uncertainty, with independent runs kept distinct from repeated video frames.

**Falsifier:** Systematic held-out errors larger than propagated uncertainty, or a predicted restoring direction opposite to measurement, reject the tested model in that range.

**Limits:** No dataset has been reanalyzed here. A successful axial fit does not establish 3D stability; a null result must retain its sensitivity and uncertainty bounds.

### Do streaming and reflections explain the remaining error?

Status: Proposed · Independent airflow and boundary observations added to radiation-force modeling

**Question:** Do additional measured mechanisms improve held-out predictions rather than merely adding fitting freedom?

**Prediction:** A model with independently constrained airflow and reflections should reduce structured equilibrium error relative to a radiation-only model if those mechanisms dominate.

**Setup:** Compare published radiation-only and extended models first. Any later physical study belongs in a qualified laboratory with independently characterized flow and boundary conditions, using inert objects only.

**Variables:** Measured airflow, boundary configuration and particle position; retain the same independently calibrated field inputs across model comparisons.

**Control:** Radiation-only baseline, added terms fixed from separate observations, randomized comparison order and held-out conditions.

**Measurement:** Out-of-sample position and force residuals, flow uncertainty, and the number of adjustable parameters; retain failures and missing data.

**Falsifier:** No reproducible improvement beyond uncertainty, incorrect changes of direction, or improvement only after refitting every condition rejects the claimed explanation.

**Limits:** The 1D browser model does not calculate these forces. Inconclusive data do not prove their absence, and a better fit does not identify a unique mechanism.

### Virtual internal-motion control

Status: Proposed · Momentum conservation with and without an external friction force

**Question:** Can an internal mass cycle translate an isolated chassis?

**Prediction:** Without an external force, the combined center of mass maintains constant velocity even while the chassis moves relative to its internal mass.

**Setup:** Digital two-body model with equal and opposite internal forces. Add a separately recorded ground-friction force in a second run.

**Variables:** Mass ratio, internal-force time profile, friction model and integration step.

**Control:** Zero external force; reverse the cycle; halve the time step to check numerical drift.

**Measurement:** Track both bodies, total momentum, center-of-mass position and integrated external impulse.

**Falsifier:** A converged zero-external-force model with unexplained net momentum would contradict the model assumptions or reveal an implementation error; a simulation alone cannot establish new physics.

**Limits:** A conceptual model, not a claim of reproducing any inventor’s hardware.

### Virtual scale reading versus gravity

Status: Proposed · Support force, upward applied force and acceleration

**Question:** Does a smaller scale reading necessarily mean a smaller gravitational force?

**Prediction:** For a constrained body with upward acceleration a, N = m(g + a) − F_up. A known upward force lowers N while g remains fixed.

**Setup:** Digital force diagram and ideal scale, with a unilateral contact constraint: support force cannot pull the body downward.

**Variables:** Mass, applied upward force, acceleration and an optional sensor offset.

**Control:** Set the applied force and sensor offset to zero; compare stationary and freely falling cases.

**Measurement:** Show support force, gravitational force, applied force and acceleration as separate values.

**Falsifier:** A reported gravity change inferred solely from N, without accounting for applied forces and motion, fails this identification test.

**Limits:** This teaches a measurement ambiguity. It does not implement a gravity-control device, and requires no electrical or acoustic apparatus.

### A force zero is not a stable trap

Status: Proposed · Weak linear diamagnetism and local curvature

**Question:** Can identical vertical force balance coexist with different transverse stability?

**Prediction:** At equilibrium ∇U = 0; a positive-definite Hessian of U gives a nondegenerate local minimum. A negative eigenvalue makes one direction unstable.

**Setup:** Software comparison of local quadratic potentials. Keep the first vertical derivative fixed while changing curvature. A future physical interpretation must use a Maxwell-consistent field map; arbitrary quadratic examples are teaching counterexamples only.

**Variables:** Susceptibility, density, signed ∂z(B²), and three curvature eigenvalues; SI units and the coordinate convention must accompany each case.

**Control:** Zero magnetic gradient gives zero magnetic support. Match force and density across cases; include an explicit saddle and a minimum.

**Measurement:** Compare net force in N with curvature in N/m. Current browser computes force balance only; the full spatial-field/stability extension is proposed.

**Falsifier:** A candidate trap with a negative curvature eigenvalue fails the local static-stability claim even when vertical force is exactly zero.

**Limits:** No high-field build or material certification. Real samples require measured field geometry, anisotropy, dimensions and uncertainty.

### Can detector bias imitate a quantum signature?

Status: Proposed · Sideband thermometry and calibrated variance

**Question:** Which calibration errors can imitate an occupation below one or a variance below the chosen reference?

**Prediction:** For a calibrated harmonic mode, corrected sideband powers satisfy P_AS/P_S = n̄/(n̄+1). Changing detector gain or cavity transfer without correcting it can bias an inferred n̄.

**Setup:** Future offline synthetic-data audit and, when openly available, licensed raw-data reanalysis. Inject known response asymmetry, background offsets and phase drift; fit blinded synthetic warm-state nulls. This audit is not implemented by the force-balance bench.

**Variables:** Dimensionless n̄, response ratio, background PSD, phase uncertainty, detection efficiency and the chosen quadrature reference.

**Control:** Equal-response synthetic channels; injected classical thermal nulls; out-of-loop estimates; held-out calibration segments and unchanged fitting rules.

**Measurement:** Bias and coverage of inferred occupation/variance, including calibration uncertainty; no therapeutic or gravity endpoint.

**Falsifier:** If a purported sub-reference signature disappears under independently justified calibration, that dataset fails this claim. A plot alone cannot certify the quantum state.

**Limits:** The selected papers provide specific controls; this proposal does not assert that their results are artifacts. Hardware replication needs specialist facilities, not a construction recipe.

### Close the energy budget before claiming excess power

Status: Proposed · Conservation and measurement control

**Question:** Does an apparent output surplus survive a full cycle and explicit stored-energy accounting?

**Prediction:** The simulated damped oscillator has equal average drive and loss power in steady state; reducing damping changes amplitude, not the source of energy.

**Setup:** Use the virtual vibration bench. Export the response rows and compare input/loss columns. For a future low-energy instrument study, define a system boundary and calibrated input, load, temperature and storage measurements before collecting data.

**Variables:** Damping, drive frequency and amplitude; measurement interval and initial/final storage state.

**Control:** Zero-drive run, detuned run and known passive load. Include phase-sensitive electrical power where applicable, not voltage times current magnitudes alone.

**Measurement:** Residual R = Eout + ΔEstored − Ein, with the sign convention fixed in advance, all energy channels and a combined uncertainty.

**Falsifier:** A claimed surplus that disappears after storage, heat or phase correction fails this test. A residual exceeding uncertainty motivates independent replication, not an immediate mechanism claim.

**Limits:** The software comparison is an identity of its equations. No new physical experiment or free-energy device has been demonstrated.
