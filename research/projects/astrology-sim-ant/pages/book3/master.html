Book III Master Tool — the Nativity

One birth, read through Lilly's whole natal apparatus: the chart and its dignities, the Lord of the Geniture and temperament, the annual profection and Lord of the Year, the hyleg & alcocoden, the primary directions to the angles, and the solar return of a chosen year — every figure computed on the verified engine.

Honest note The mathematics is real; the interpretations are Lilly's, for historical study. The hyleg/alcocoden (length-of-life) and primary-direction methods are contested even within the tradition and are shown with their assumptions — never as predictions. Astrology has no demonstrated predictive validity. See Sources & Science.

Lord of the Geniture & temperament — Lilly CA Bk III

How to read it: the Lord of the Geniture is the planet of greatest total dignity (essential + accidental) — Lilly takes it as the dominant note of the character and life. The temperament weighs the elemental qualities (hot/cold, dry/moist) of the Ascendant, the Moon, the Sun and that lord. How it's computed →

Annual profection & Lord of the Year — Bk III (time-lords)

How to read it: each year of life the Ascendant advances one whole sign (and one house); the ruler of that sign is the Lord of the Year, and the affairs of the activated house come to the fore. The lord's natal condition (its dignity) colours how the year is likely to go. How it's computed →

Hyleg & Alcocoden — Bk III (contested)

How to read it: the hyleg ("giver of life") is the chart's vital point; the alcocoden ("giver of years") and its planetary-year figures are the tradition's length-of-life estimate. This is one of the most contested techniques in the literature — shown with its assumptions, never as a prediction of lifespan. How it's computed →

Primary directions to the angles — Bk III (Naibod)

How to read it: each planet's ecliptic arc to the Ascendant or Midheaven is turned into a rough age by the Naibod key (≈ 1° of arc per year of life) — an approximate timing of when a matter "comes to" that angle, not a literal date. How it's computed →

Solar return (revolution) — Bk III

How to read it: the chart cast for the moment the Sun returns to its exact natal longitude is read as the keynote of the year ahead — always governed by, and read against, the natal radix. How it's computed →

The twelve topics, house by house — Bk III

How to read it: for each topic (wealth, siblings, parents, children, marriage…) the reader weighs three testimonies together — the lord of the house, the natural significator of the matter, and the planets occupying the house — into a crude favourable / mixed / afflicted tone. The real art weighs the actual significators far beyond any tally; this is shown for study.

Rectification of the birth time — Bk III (contested)

How to read it: the Animodar of Ptolemy corrects the degree of an angle from the almuten of the pre-natal New/Full Moon; the Trutine of Hermes checks the birth Ascendant against the Moon at an estimated conception. Both are contested and shown only with their assumptions — the pre-natal syzygy is the same one the hyleg uses.
