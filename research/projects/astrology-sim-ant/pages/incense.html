Incense & Suffumigation

What the astral-magic texts assign to each planet to be burned — recorded as the sources name it, and indexed the other way round as well: one material, every planet that claims it. That second view is the one a single grimoire cannot give you.

This hour, now

Coordinates stay in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

The hours are unequal: the day is divided in twelve from sunrise to sunset and the night in twelve from sunset to sunrise, so they stretch and shrink with the season. The band above is drawn to their real lengths. This states what the texts assign to the hour's ruler — it is not an instruction to burn anything.

Per planet

The substance is given as the text names it. Quantity and process are withheld by design, not missing: this site carries at most two of {substance · quantity · process} for any harm-flagged material, so the operable triple cannot be assembled from a page here.

Per material

The same data read the other way. Composites are indexed as one material each — this site does not carry their ingredient lists, and expanding them would invent entries it cannot cite.

Where the traditions converge

Where this project's own earlier table diverged

The books are the authority here Every assignment this site carries comes from a cited text — Picatrix III.7 and Agrippa, Three Books II. An earlier instrument of this project shipped its own incense table whose footer called it "the multi-source table of this project" without naming the sources. That table is superseded and is not an authority; nothing here derives from it. It is kept below only as a documented contrast, because a corpus built on citation should show where its own past assumptions drifted from its texts rather than quietly delete the evidence.

What is not settled

These are live disagreements, kept as disagreements. Where two texts name two different things, both are recorded rather than reconciled into a tidier answer no source states.

What this page contains, counted

Every count names its unit, because a number without its unit is how two correct measurements start an argument.

Sources & honesty

What this page is not It is a record of what historical texts assign, not an instruction. It names no quantity, no preparation and no process parameter; it does not enumerate the ingredients of the two compound recipes; and it makes no claim that any of it works. Several entries name toxic or animal-derived substances and are marked as records rather than recipes — do not attempt any historical recipe. The traditions documented here belong to the communities that carry them; this site claims authorship of none of it.
