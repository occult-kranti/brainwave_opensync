The Operative Corpus — what these books tell an operator to do

The Great Confluence maps who read whom. This map asks a different question of the same shelf: what kind of procedure does this book contain, how completely does it account for it, and where did that sequence come from? Every work is typed against a controlled vocabulary of procedure-kinds; every grade is graded on three separate axes, each naming its own basis and carrying the sentence it rests on; and every relation is a node, not a hairline — a thing that can be opened, cited, filtered and struck.

Standing note — please read first This wing records that a named work contains a procedure of a named kind — its type, how many stages it has, what those stages are called, where in the text it sits, what is missing and how that is known, and who says so. It reproduces no operative text whatever: no ordered sub-steps, no quantities, durations, counts or repetitions, no formula, mantra or divine-name strings in any script, no drawable signs, seals or diagram templates, and no substance lists. Naming a stage is a map; ordering the sub-steps would be a recipe, and nothing here is one. Every technique is described as historical practice, never prescribed; the traditions surveyed here have no demonstrated predictive or operative validity and no plausible mechanism, and where a text claims a result that claim is attributed to the text and left there. Several first-rank works can be described here but not quoted anywhere on this site, because the only public-domain witness is abridged at precisely the operative core — that limit is stated on the record rather than hidden. Nothing on this page is health, medical, legal or safety advice.

How to read this graph

The relation is a node

There is never a work→work claim arrow here. Between two works sits an op-node carrying the claim itself — its relation kind, whether it is asserted, whether it is procedure-level (it names the procedure that moved) or merely work-level, its epistemic label, its citation. A grade is not a label on a hairline; it is a record that can be opened and struck.

a procedure claim · a relation claim · a work

Values are on the face

Each node is a record shape whose computed numbers are painted on it — { title | w 0.72 | ↓0.15 }. w is the gate weight, computed from the witness list by a published rubric and never hand-written. ↓ is attribution pressure: the weighted share of the claims touching that node which are themselves disputed, debunked, under-weight or flagged unverified. Attribution pressure is a number this site computes, not a scholarly finding, and it means nothing outside this dataset. A weak claim looks weak without anyone having to click it.

Completeness has three axes

repoCoverage — what this site already records. textCompleteness — what the work, as scholarship reconstructs it, contains. witnessCompleteness — what the edition actually in hand gives. Keeping the first two apart is what stops “this site says little” being read as “the text says little”. Grades: ▰ complete · ▰▱ partial · ○ referenced · ◌ fragmentary · ≠ unstable-plural (complete in each recension, and the recensions disagree) · ⊘ withheld. A withheld grade is a result, not a gap.

A grade names its basis

Every grade states how it was reached: a slot inventory, a comparison of recensions, a self-contained table, the edition’s own editor, an arithmetic check — or genre-norm, which means it was inferred from what the genre usually does. Genre-norm grades are drawn hatched and are excluded from every headline count on this page. A grade whose basis would have to read “the book looks finished” is one nobody writes.

Struck records stay visible

A relation recorded as not asserted is drawn struck, with its reason on its own face, and never hidden — hiding it is exactly how a flagged claim becomes an unflagged one two rounds later. A NON_EDGE is a claim that two works are not related, naming who confused them. A ⛔ marks a claim flagged do-not-quote. A ⚠ marks a harm flag, whose note is carried verbatim in the record.

Left to right is rank, not year

The horizontal axis is transmission rank — dependency depth over the asserted claim graph — not calendar time. The Confluence atlas owns time; rank is a different fact. Where rank and the recorded years disagree, that disagreement is drawn as a ⇄ badge and left visible, because it usually means a date or a direction is wrong. Colour is a finding aid; the label is the fact, and every culture accent is also written out in words.

What the machine checks, and what it found

The graph is rebuilt from a tracked curation gate by a tracked script, and three structural properties are re-derived on every build rather than remembered. The most useful of them is acyclicity: a cycle in a transmission relation is a dating error, not a drawing problem, so the check is a free correctness detector. Its result is printed below whichever way it comes out.

The ledger — every claim in this view, as text

This is the accessible representation of the diagram, and it is not a summary of it. The diagram is aria-hidden by design — a picture of several hundred shapes is not navigable — so every visual fact above is a row below: the same filter, the same records, the same grades and flags. One qualification, stated rather than glossed: the gate weight painted on every node face is a column only of the works table; for a culture, an author, a claim or a type chip it lives one step further in, as a field of the record the row opens. Where the diagram is capped, the ledger is not. Selecting any row opens the same record the diagram opens.

What this dataset cannot answer

Stated so that no later round mistakes a boundary for a gap to be closed.

  • Whether any of it works. Efficacy is not a field here and never will be. Where a text claims a result, the claim is attributed to the text and stopped there.
  • How to perform anything. By contract. A grade of complete is a claim about the structure of a text, never about anyone's ability to execute it — and the record shows that even ancient practitioners adapted formularies rather than running them, so even a “complete” handbook was not a closed algorithm to its own users.
  • The date of several of its own nodes. Some works carry no year this dataset trusts, and several carry three or four scholarly positions. Rank-versus-year detection therefore returns unknown for an undated node, and unknown never renders as fine.
  • Any of the contested questions it carries. Where the field disagrees, both positions print and neither is picked. Resolving one here would break the rule the whole site rests on.
  • Any grade whose only reachable witness is redacted. The third completeness axis exists precisely to make that visible instead of laundering it: a much-read English translation whose own preface admits that the ritual technicalities were kept out is a witness to its translator as much as to its text.
  • Anything in a culture with no coverage. Mesoamerican, sub-Saharan African, Mesopotamian, Greco-Roman mystery cult, indigenous American and Siberian, Shintō and Shugendō, Korean, Vietnamese and South-East Asian, Zoroastrian and Mandaean, Slavic, Baltic and Finnic material is absent here. Silence in this graph is not evidence of absence; it is evidence about what has been surveyed.
  • Whether what a living tradition withholds is what this graph would guess. Several of these systems are practised now. Where a tradition restricts its own material, that restriction is recorded as a dated, sourced fact about the text and is not second-guessed.

How this page is built

The runtime dataset is generated, never hand-edited: a tracked, dependency-free script reads a tracked curation gate and the audited research slices, recomputes every weight from its witness list, drops every excluded or struck record at write time, sorts everything on an explicit total key and prints a diff of what changed. A second mode of the same script rebuilds the module in memory and fails the build if it differs from what is committed, which is what makes hand-editing a generated file a test failure rather than a silent edit. All geometry lives in a pure, DOM-free engine that takes no clock and no random source, so the same inputs give the same picture on any machine; this page measures its container, asks the engine, and paints. The numbers on a node face are the engine's, not the painter's.

Weight is witness × primaryFactor × contestFactor × flagFactor, rounded to two places, with an admission floor of 0.40 — so a single-witness row carrying an unverified flag falls below the floor by arithmetic rather than by anyone remembering to drop it. A record that never enters cannot be laundered back in by restatement.

Method note A curated survey of five corpora, not an exhaustive register, compiled from published scholarship and audited adversarially before it was admitted. Every candidate ever considered stays in the gate file — including what was rejected, and what was admitted and then struck, with the strike that struck it. The procedure-type vocabulary is controlled, and a term with no occupants keeps its slot with a stated warrant, because an empty term is the reason a later round does not fold an unlike procedure into a near-enough one. Where a relation is already carried by the Confluence atlas, it appears here only if it changes what is claimed — from “one work influenced another” to “this named procedure moved, and here is the witness.” See Sources & Science.
