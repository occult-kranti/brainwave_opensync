## 1. The provenance problem, stated up front

The single most repeated body of biographical detail (birth date 22 March 1948 Sydney; Sydney
Grammar; Sydney University BA 1965–68; geography lecturing; the 1972 move to London) exists in
exactly two places — the English Wikipedia article, where most of it is **uncited**, and his own
site sskinner.com, which it closely tracks. Treat all of it as **self-reported** unless noted.
Independently verifiable anchors are:

1. **The PhD thesis exists.** University of Newcastle (NSW) repository: *"Magical techniques and
   implements present in Graeco-Egyptian magical papyri, Byzantine Greek Solomonic manuscripts and
   European grimoires: transmission, continuity and commonality (the technology of Solomonic
   magic)"*, thesis dated **2013**, handle **1959.13/1041669** (legacy record uon:13943; now on
   openresearch.newcastle.edu.au, item 29020349; also indexed at oatd.org). ~450 pp. The
   repository page itself was 403-blocked to our fetcher; metadata was confirmed through the OATD
   index snippet and the redirect chain from hdl.handle.net/1959.13/1041669, which resolves to the
   Newcastle record. **"PhD in Classics" is his own site's phrasing**; Wikipedia says School of
   Humanities and Social Science, awarded 2014. So: *degree verified; discipline label
   self-reported; award year 2013-vs-2014 unresolved.*
2. **Terrestrial Astrology is real, RKP, 1980, and was academically reviewed.** *Medical History*
   (Cambridge) carries a review: "London, Routledge & Kegan Paul, 1980, 8vo, pp. xiii, 293, £15."
   His own site lists the publisher as "Law Book" (evidently the Australian distributor) — the
   imprint is RKP; the ISBN prefix 0-7100 is RKP's.
3. **Golden Hoard Press Pte. Ltd.** is a live Singapore company, UEN **200822581C**, incorporated
   **3 Dec 2008** (companies.sg / sgpbusiness.com). The imprint was publishing in London from 2004
   (SWCM I–II carry the 978-0-9547639-x block); the relation between the UK "Golden Hoard Press
   Ltd" on later imprints and the Singapore Pte Ltd is **not established here**.
4. **First-edition dates of the SWCM volumes** are verifiable through antiquarian records
   (Weiser Antiquarian, AbeBooks) and contemporary reviews, and they **correct Wikipedia and his
   own list** in two places: *Goetia of Dr Rudd* is Golden Hoard **2007** (both lists say 2009 —
   the Llewellyn edition), and *Veritable Key of Solomon* is Golden Hoard **October 2008** (both
   lists say 2011). The Wellcome catalogue independently holds the Veritable Key and its source
   MSS 4669/4670.
5. **Crowley diaries**: first edition Neville Spearman (Jersey) **1979**; Weiser **1996** "first
   authorized edition", editorial copyright Skinner. Title: *The Magical Diaries of Aleister
   Crowley: Tunisia 1923*. That is the one Crowley diary edition he edited; the 2021 Watkins
   *Four Books of Magick* is an introduced re-edition, not diary editing.

## 2. Shape of the career (for the page)

Australian (b. 1948, Sydney — self-reported). Four phases:
- **Sydney to 1972**: BA Sydney; geography teaching/lecturing; 1967 underground magazines
  *Lucifer* and *Chaos* (this item carries an actual Wikipedia cite).
- **London 1972–2003**: founds **Askin Publishers** (1973) — publisher first, author second; the
  Askin list (Crowley's *Astrology* 1974, Lapidus *In Pursuit of Gold* 1976, Laycock's *Complete
  Enochian Dictionary* 1978, Agrippa/Paracelsus facsimiles) prefigures his whole later editorial
  program. *Living Earth Manual of Feng-Shui* (RKP **1976**) and *Terrestrial Astrology* (RKP
  **1980**) both come from this phase. 1990s: consumer-magazine publishing (self-reported), then
  **Feng Shui for Modern Living** magazine (1998; PPA Publisher of the Year nomination 2000 —
  cited on Wikipedia).
- **Malaysia/Singapore 2003–c.2021**: Golden Hoard as the vehicle; the **Sourceworks of
  Ceremonial Magic** series with **David Rankine** (vols I–VI, 2004–2010) and later **Daniel
  Clark** (vols X–XV+, 2018–2025); the Newcastle PhD (thesis 2013); the two *Techniques* books
  (2014, 2015) explicitly developed from it.
- **London again from 2021** (Wikipedia, uncited).

The page should describe him as **author, editor-publisher and (late) academically credentialed
historian of magic** — the AUTHORED / EDITED distinction is structural to his output: roughly
half his magic list is edited manuscript transcription (SWCM), not authored monograph, and *The
Complete Magician's Tables* is a compilation. His feng shui strand is authored popular +
reference work; his geomancy strand is authored history.

## 4. Relevance hooks for the site

- **Geomancy wing**: *Terrestrial Astrology* / *Geomancy in Theory & Practice* is the standard
  English history of Western divinatory geomancy and the natural reception anchor.
- **Opgraph**: the PhD thesis IS a transmission argument (PGM → Hygromanteia → Clavicula) — i.e.
  exactly the `claimedBy` shape FRAMING §2.4 requires: transmission claims can be attributed to
  "Skinner 2013 (thesis) / 2014 / 2015" as a named scholar's position, never adjudicated. His
  Hygromanteia volume (SWCM VIII, trans. Marathakis, 2011) is the edition-of-record citation for
  the Byzantine link. Note also SWCM XI uses **Robert Turner's 1657** Ars Notoria translation —
  ties to the existing Turner-1657 finding in the opgraph plan.
- **Contested positions to carry, not resolve**: the degree-discipline label; the "brought feng
  shui to the West" characterisation (reception claim with named repeaters vs self-description);
  the series numbering.
