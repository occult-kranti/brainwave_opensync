# Slice 11 — Skinner's grimoire editions: working notes

Companion to `11-editions-and-contents.json`. Living-person discipline in force; quoteSafe:false;
operative razor applied throughout (procedure TYPES and anatomy only; no operative text transcribed
anywhere in this slice, and none was needed — every fact here is bibliographic).

## The single strongest structural finding

**Golden Hoard title pages name their shelfmarks.** The series' full titles are of the form
"…being a transcription of X from MS Y, with other pertinent extracts from MSS Z…", and library
catalogues (Wellcome Collection, WorldCat, Stanford) transcribe those title pages. So the
manuscript-base question — the most checkable fact about the work — is answerable at
**library-catalogue provenance** for vols 1–9, which is the best evidence class available short of
holding the books. This is itself diagnostic of the kind of scholarship: the editions *advertise*
their witnesses; nobody in the record disputes a Skinner shelfmark. What gets disputed is the
historical superstructure (attributions, transmission arguments, dating narratives).

Note the economy of the corpus: **Sloane 3826 yields two volumes (6, 7); Wellcome 4669 yields two
books (vol 4 + the Avalonia volume); the Peter Smart/'Rudd' cluster (Harley 6482–6483) yields two
volumes (2, 3).** The series is built by systematically strip-mining a small set of London
collections (BL Sloane/Harley, Wellcome, Bodleian Rawlinson) — which is also why the shelfmark
record is so clean.

## Operative-graph characterisation (type level)

- **Evocation-centric with spirit catalogue + constraint apparatus:** vol 3 (Lemegeton recension:
  72-spirit catalogue, circle, instruments, graded conjurations, dismissal; plus the recension's
  signature *angelic-counter-control* layer pairing Shem angels against Goetic spirits).
- **Invocation/angel-magic:** vols 1, 2, 11, 12 (oration sequences; vol 1 adds
  name-derivation-from-tables; Ars Notoria adds figure-contemplation and a gloss/method layer in B).
- **Clavicula anatomy (purification → consecration → election → talisman):** vols 4, 10; vol 8 is
  its claimed Byzantine ancestor with divination procedures (hygromancy/lecanomancy class) attached.
- **Compendium/correspondence, thin operative core:** vols 6, 7 (times, suffumigation classes,
  purity regimen, virtue catalogues, lunar-mansion elections + image magic).
- **Composite service-magic:** vol 9 (cunning-man handbook: elections, mansions, image magic,
  theft/treasure/healing operation classes).
- **Concealment-as-structure:** vol 5 (multi-script encoding) and vol 13 (conjuration-form
  cryptography) — both map to the graph's `cryptographic-concealment` procedure-type.
- **Non-operative but graph-relevant:** vol 14 (1508 *bibliography of grimoires* = witness-list
  evidence), Psellus (doctrine only), Dee Spiritual Diaries (`procedure-as-session-record`).

## Relations to prior editors — the three clean cases

1. **Lemegeton (vol 3) vs Mathers–Crowley (1904) and Peterson (2001).** Mathers/Crowley printed one
   book (Goetia) from Sloane MSS. Peterson's critical edition chose Sloane 3825 (poss. 1641) as
   prime text and treats Harley 6483 as late. Skinner–Rankine deliberately edited the witness
   Peterson set aside, adding the Shem-angel apparatus and three further books. This is a genuine,
   civil recension dispute; the contested node is whether Harley 6483 (a Peter Smart copy, c. 1712,
   ascribed to Thomas Rudd, d. 1656) witnesses a 17th-c. practice or an 18th-c. compilation.
2. **Key of Solomon (vol 4) vs Mathers (1889).** The edition's own subtitle is the relation: "three
   different texts from those translated by Mathers" — different recension (French 1796 Fyot MSS
   with full colour talisman series), first English. Harms (2008) credits the Bulwer-Lytton/*Zanoni*
   manuscript-title find, doubts the Denley link.
3. **Sibley Clavis (vol 10) vs Peterson (Ibis, 2012).** Parallel witnesses of the same
   Sibley–Hockley tradition edited within seven years of each other; Skinner–Clark's value-claim
   ("45% longer") is self-reported and their witness has no public shelfmark — the weakest
   provenance point in the whole series.

Plus: **Hygromanteia (vol 8)** supersedes-in-scope Delatte 1927 (Greek only) and Torijano 2002 (one
recension in an appendix); **Summa (15/18)** stands against Gehr's Latin dissertation text and now a
rival independent English edition (Sneddon & Erneus 2025).

## Corrections the parent must keep

- **The Testament of Cyprian the Mage is NOT Skinner.** Jake Stratton-Kent, Encyclopaedia Goetica
  III, Scarlet Imprint 2014. The task brief conflated it; Skinner's Cyprian item is the Clavis
  Inferni (Wellcome 2000), an unrelated northern-European text. Do not encode it under Skinner.
- **Cunning Man's Grimoire = Bodleian Rawlinson D.253**, not a Wellcome MS (a search snippet
  suggested Wellcome; catalogue records are unambiguous).

## Provenance-class cautions (living person)

- "First complete English translation" claims (Steganographia, Antipalus, Summa, Ars Notoria B) are
  **publisher/self-reported**; plausible against the known prior-edition record but not
  independently certified here.
- The Golden Dawn "suppression" narrative around vol 1, and the Trithemius/Dee ownership claim for
  the Kassel Summa MS, are publisher-copy claims — label as self-reported wherever surfaced.
- Golden Hoard is Skinner's own imprint (Wikipedia-sourced). State as fact of publication channel;
  draw no inference from it.
